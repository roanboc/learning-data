# Rebuilding A map people and agents can read

*A map people and agents can read*, film 11 of 11 of *The map before the data* (a working title), is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/`](../../shared/src/), which this film uses as they are. Its newcomer, Zofia, and the map's agent are drawn in its own source, not in `shared/src/people.js`. Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás, Ama, ArchiMate, Hōkūleʻa, Papeʻete, Satawal, Mataiva, Nainoa, IT), and the moment its poster shows (Hōkūleʻa at night, inside the ring of its navigator's star compass). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: Hōkūleʻa sailing on into the night, then the title; the elements going into the map; and the map, full, handing its meaning over to *From words to data*, then the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `d11_`): the Pacific at night, a sky that turns slowly and the sea's long swells (`D11_STARS`, `d11_sky`, `d11_sea`, `d11_swell`); Hōkūleʻa, a double-hulled canoe with two crab-claw sails, its crew as silhouettes and its navigator at the stern (`d11_hullPath`, `d11_sail`, `d11_crew`, `d11_canoe`), and a tern (`d11_bird`); the star compass as a ring on the sea round the canoe, its points where fifteen bright stars rise and set at Satawal's latitude, or the thirty-two equal houses of the Hawaiian star compass (`D11_DEC`, `D11_AZ`, `D11_AZEQ`, `d11_ringXY`, `d11_compass`); Mataiva on the horizon and Papeʻete's waterfront (`d11_atoll`, `d11_harbour`); a chart of the Pacific, sketched from longitude and latitude, with a light for each person who holds the knowledge and its passing on (`D11_PAC`, `D11_ISL`, `d11_pac`, `d11_chartBg`, `d11_chart`, `d11_light`, `d11_pass`, `d11_name`); the map as a small picture, in a frame and printed on slides that drift (`D11_MAP`, `d11_mini`, `d11_mapCard`, `d11_slide`); each kind of element, with the glyphs this film adds, and its page, small and open, as the tool's form or as a text file (`D11_KIND`, `d11_glyph`, `d11_id`, `d11_fileMini`, `d11_file`, `D11_LINES`, `D11_RUL021`, `D11_DEC007`); the enterprise architecture tool, its window, its database and its graph, the sources that already keep the map's records, and a glossary term as the map holds it (`d11_window`, `d11_gglyph`, `d11_db`, `D11_GN`, `D11_GE`, `d11_graph`, `D11_SAND`, `D11_SRC`, `d11_srcIcon`, `d11_srcCard`, `d11_book`, `d11_term`); the map's side panel (or a repository's folders), its versions and history, a change request (or a pull request) and the checks on the map (`D11_TREE`, `d11_tree`, `d11_vline`, `d11_history`, `d11_pr`, `d11_checks`); the views, the glossary, dbt's YAML and Databricks' catalogue, the threads that carry the map one way, and a pencil (`D11_VIEWS`, `d11_vicon`, `d11_vcard`, `D11_GLOSS`, `d11_gloss`, `d11_thread`, `D11_DBT`, `d11_dbt`, `d11_dbx`, `d11_pencil`); what the agent is asked and what it finds, its answer, the source behind it and the outage agent's rights (`d11_ask`, `d11_trace`, `d11_hit`, `d11_agree`, `d11_col`, `d11_answer`, `d11_cursor`, `d11_source`, `d11_outage`); two sources that disagree, the agent's question to the owner, and the rule from *Keeping it true* (`d11_disagree`, `d11_question`, `d11_rule3`); the retail code amended, what a change touches, and the billing team's tracking table (`d11_code`, `d11_touches`, `d11_tgrid`, `D11_TRACK`, `d11_track`); and Zofia's first day: her badge, a page of acronyms, the list of systems and her question to the agent (`d11_badge`, `d11_acr`, `D11_SYS`, `d11_sys`, `d11_chat`). |
| `src/scenes.js` | The twelve chapters, and what they share: Tomás's last note (`D11_NOTE`), the tool's window and where its graph sits (`D11_WIN`), the six kinds of element (`D11_SIX`), the same element kept both ways (`D11_BOTH`), the map's versions and history (`D11_VERS`, `D11_HIST`, `D11_VERS2`), the views' readers and where each view sits (`D11_READERS`, `d11_vrect`), the 2007 pwo on Satawal and what was taught after it (`D11_PWO`, `D11_STAY`, `D11_GEN`), the series' eleven films and the layers each one adds (`D11_FILMS`, `D11_ADDS`), and the answer to Tomás's note (`D11_ANS`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds: for the Pacific, in 1976 and from 2007 to 2026, a vibraphone, bowed and struck softly, over held chords of bowed bars in B-flat minor, with the open ocean as long, low swells (no borrowed Pacific sound); then a nylon string in D-flat major, each chapter's chord spread up on it as the chapter starts, a note for each thing that lands, and the vibraphone wherever the map is read and gives an answer; the series' mark at the title and the end, and *From words to data*'s own four notes as the meaning is handed over. Since 20 October 2026 it follows the series' sound: music under the opening story, then only now and then (a chapter's turn, a moment on screen, a wordless pause), with nothing held under the explaining, nothing pulsing and nothing trembling. Each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs the series' tool (`../../shared/tools/run.py`) on this film. |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/enterprise-architecture/11-a-map-people-and-agents-can-read/source/`; the video is `dist/a-map-people-and-agents-can-read.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/a-map-people-and-agents-can-read/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/a-map-people-and-agents-can-read/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `risk`, `views` and `rights`. Progress is stored under `ld-a-map-people-and-agents-can-read`.
