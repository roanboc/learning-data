Read and follow [`AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md) at the repository root: its four stages (conceptual foundation, story and world, video craft at the highest effort, check and publish) and its rules that always apply. Ask the author for the reference framework and their real experience before drafting anything. Share every new or changed video in the chat as a review copy it can preview (`python site-tools/review_copy.py <video>`), with the full video and the pull request link, and wait for the author's approval or corrections.
