Leading a data team

Four ideas to lead by: do less on purpose, improve in small steps, put the team first, and let people decide.

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1Less, on purpose9 min · Helena's team puts everything it runs on one wall, keeps what people use, and stops the rest, together and openly.
  2. 2One small change at a time8 min · Ivy turns two hours of Monday checks into ten minutes, one small change at a time, with the time Helena gives her.
