In the weeds of data crafting

A technical series for analytics engineers: how an agreed data model becomes tables, with dbt, on Databricks. It follows Jun, the university's analytics engineer, from a question to versioned, tested and documented models, with an AI agent that helps at every step and a person who approves each one.

The films

Watch them in order. It's technical: it shows real code, YAML and Markdown from an example dbt project that runs on dbt Core with DuckDB, in this site's repository, so you can run it yourself. It makes most sense after From words to data.

One thread: the credential, built

From words to data ends with version 3 of the credential model approved. This series builds it, from one question asked by planning: how many learners are within 15 credit points of a graduate certificate, as at census date? It needs credentials from three systems, one learner keyed three ways, credit that counts towards awards, and history.
