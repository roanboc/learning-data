# More than a platform

The film was reshaped on 14 November 2026 from the author's brief, to introduce the portal directly. He revised the script four times that day and asked to build it ("Start building"). These are the questions the build still had to answer, and what it assumed.

| Question | Assumed |
|---|---|
| Where does *enterprise data architecture* live on the site? | On the *Enterprise architecture* topic's line: "How an organisation works, in layers, so its data is designed as part of it: enterprise data architecture." A page of its own could follow. |
| Which numbers does the lab on screen show? | The *Capture* lab's own: an enrolment reaches bronze in 1.8 s (0.7 s to the hub, 1.1 s on to bronze, in `site/assets/learn/labs.js`). |
| Which question and scenario are shown in action? | Drawn for the film from the site's parts: a Pause and think question about two offices' numbers, with a right answer and a near miss, and a scenario about a nightly file that arrives twice, with its reason. |
| How is the example project drawn? | A window with GitHub's mark and the project's real folder tree: the code folders, then the governance folders highlighted, then `docs/decisions.md`. Its badge on the route sits between *Analytics engineering* and *Data quality*, until the other thread's icon reaches main. |
| How are the author's roles shown? | Three cards on a timeline, *Applications*, *Data*, *Enterprise architecture*, with *16+ years*, and no name card (the author). |
| Which goal lights its stops? | *Build a report people trust*: four stops numbered 1 to 4, as before. |
| The Spanish narration | es-MX-DaliaNeural, each line from its English cue; six captions were shortened so no line needs more than about a quarter more speed. |
| Length | 6 minutes 2 seconds, in eight chapters; nothing more cut (the author). |
