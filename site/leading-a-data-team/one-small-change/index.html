One small change at a time: improve your own work, and check each step

In July 2027 Ivy Reyes spends two hours every Monday checking her room data by hand, and until she's done, readers may see a wrong number. Helena Novak asks what's in the way and gives her the time. Ivy measures, drops what nobody needs, writes the rest down and automates one step at a time.
