A hundred records: measure before you write checks

The graduation list passed every check, yet 41 certificates were printed again. On a Friday in February, Malia Fonoti, Mei Tanaka and Jun Park look at its hundred most recent records: 79 are perfect. Then the platform profiles all 2,031 graduates, and only then does Jun write the first checks.
