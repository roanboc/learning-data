# Rebuilding A map people and agents can read

*A map people and agents can read*, film 1 of 4 of *Continuous architecture* (until 23 November 2026, film 11 of *The map before the data*), is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, *The map before the data*'s components in [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/src/`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/src/), which this film uses as they are, and the components the series shares with *AI architecture* in [`../../../ai-and-continuous-architecture/shared/src/aca.js`](../../../ai-and-continuous-architecture/shared/src/aca.js), for Babylon's sky, skyline and people. Its newcomer, Zofia, and the map's agent are drawn in its own source, not in `shared/src/people.js`. Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás, Ama, ArchiMate, IT), and the moment its poster shows (a watcher on a roof in Babylon at night, the moon over the city, and the test: the next can read it, and carry on). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: Babylon's night going on over the roof, the diary's tablets on their shelf, then the title; the elements going into the map; and the answer, then the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `d11_`): Babylon at night in 652 BCE, its roofs, the moon (full, or crossed by the Earth's shadow in an eclipse) and the planets among the stars (`D11_MOONLIT`, `d11_moon`, `d11_planet`, `d11_night`, `D11_HOUSES`, `d11_city`, `d11_roof`); a watcher on his roof with his tablet, writing, and the path he walks (`d11_tab`, `d11_scribe`, `d11_pathAt`); the diary, on clay, night by night in the same order, with the month's end (`D11_NIGHTS`, `D11_GOODS`, `d11_crescent`, `d11_cloudIcon`, `d11_diary`), and nearly six hundred years of it under four lines of kings (`d11_yx`, `D11_KINGS`); the map as a small picture, in a frame and printed on slides that drift (`D11_MAP`, `d11_mini`, `d11_mapCard`, `d11_slide`); each kind of element, with the glyphs this film adds, and its page, small and open, as the tool's form or as a text file (`D11_KIND`, `d11_glyph`, `d11_id`, `d11_fileMini`, `d11_file`, `D11_LINES`, `D11_RUL021`, `D11_DEC007`); the enterprise architecture tool, its window, its database and its graph, the sources that already keep the map's records, and a glossary term as the map holds it (`d11_window`, `d11_gglyph`, `d11_db`, `D11_GN`, `D11_GE`, `d11_graph`, `D11_SAND`, `D11_SRC`, `d11_srcIcon`, `d11_srcCard`, `d11_book`, `d11_term`); the map's side panel (or a repository's folders), its versions and history, a change request (or a pull request) and the checks on the map (`D11_TREE`, `d11_tree`, `d11_vline`, `d11_history`, `d11_pr`, `d11_checks`); the views, the glossary, dbt's YAML and Databricks' catalogue, the threads that carry the map one way, and a pencil (`D11_VIEWS`, `d11_vicon`, `d11_vcard`, `D11_GLOSS`, `d11_gloss`, `d11_thread`, `D11_DBT`, `d11_dbt`, `d11_dbx`, `d11_pencil`); what the agent is asked and what it finds, its answer, the source behind it and the outage agent's rights (`d11_ask`, `d11_trace`, `d11_hit`, `d11_agree`, `d11_col`, `d11_answer`, `d11_cursor`, `d11_source`, `d11_outage`); two sources that disagree, the agent's question to the owner, and the rule from *Keeping it true* (`d11_disagree`, `d11_question`, `d11_rule3`); the retail code amended, what a change touches, and the billing team's tracking table (`d11_code`, `d11_touches`, `d11_tgrid`, `D11_TRACK`, `d11_track`); and Zofia's first day: her badge, a page of acronyms, the list of systems and her question to the agent (`d11_badge`, `d11_acr`, `D11_SYS`, `d11_sys`, `d11_chat`). |
| `src/scenes.js` | The twelve chapters, and what they share: the watcher's test on a card (`d11_testCard`), Babylon and its years and cycles (`d11_babylon`, `d11_years`, `d11_cycle`), Tomás's last note (`D11_NOTE`), the tool's window and where its graph sits (`D11_WIN`), the six kinds of element (`D11_SIX`), the same element kept both ways (`D11_BOTH`), the map's versions and history (`D11_VERS`, `D11_HIST`, `D11_VERS2`), the views' readers and where each view sits (`D11_READERS`, `d11_vrect`), the watch passed from one watcher to the next, as the close comes back to it (`D11_RELAY`), the answer to Tomás's note (`D11_ANS`), and the joins between chapters (`CHAPTER_JOINS`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds, the palette it had when it closed *The map before the data*, retuned for Babylon: for the night, from 652 BCE, a vibraphone, bowed and struck softly, over held chords of bowed bars in B-flat minor (no borrowed sound of Mesopotamia), its bowed notes following the watch from one watcher to the next and its struck notes the sky's lights and what the watchers write down; then a nylon string in D-flat major, each chapter's chord spread up on it as the chapter starts, a note for each thing that lands, and the vibraphone wherever the map is read and gives an answer; the series' mark, four notes rising, under the title and the end card. Music under the opening story, then only now and then (a chapter's turn, a moment on screen, a wordless pause), with nothing held under the explaining, nothing pulsing and nothing trembling. Each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs *The map before the data*'s tool (`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py`) on this film; `tools/quiet.py`, after `tools/audio.py`, checks the sound with the governance films' check (`films/data-governance/shared/tools/quiet.py`), which the series' films use. |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/continuous-architecture/1-a-map-people-and-agents-can-read/source/`; the video is `dist/a-map-people-and-agents-can-read.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/a-map-people-and-agents-can-read/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/a-map-people-and-agents-can-read/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `risk`, `views` and `rights`. Progress is stored under `ld-a-map-people-and-agents-can-read`.
