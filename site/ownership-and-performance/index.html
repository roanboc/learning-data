One thread: Ivy's first data product, and her first year

From 1 July 2027 Ivy owns Teaching space use, the counts per room per hour that Facilities and the faculties read every Monday. When forty rooms' sensors change in August, she notices first and says so. The team reads its measures together to fix how the work flows, never to rank anyone. When Tomás, the report developer, falls behind after the reports move to the platform, Helena looks for the causes with him before judging. Ivy's year ends with her next goal: helping the next graduate start.

Owning the outcomeKenya, from 1977: trees still standingWith the forests cleared, rural women's streams were drying up and their firewood was further away each year. In the Green Belt Movement they grew and planted trees, and were paid for each one still alive months later: they answered for living trees, not for the planting.
Measure the work, not the peopleSweden, 1997 to 2024: roads, not driversMost crashes were put down to drivers' mistakes. In 1997 Sweden's parliament adopted Vision Zero, and those who design and run the roads answered too: every fatal crash was studied, and cable barriers, roundabouts and lower speeds followed. Road deaths fell by more than half while traffic grew.
