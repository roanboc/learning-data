# The new skills

*The first series of the module Leading data people.*

What a data person needs to be good at now. In 2027, a year from now, Ivy Reyes joins the university's data team straight from her degree, and Graham Ellis, the new Director of Facilities, asks for "a dashboard of room use". Three films: *Many hats, one picture* (go deep in one thing, and learn enough of the others to see the whole route of a decision); *The draft and the decision* (when an agent writes the first draft, framing, checking and deciding are the skills that grow); and *Learning while doing* (learn on the real work, beside someone who knows, then teach it). *Working with people* follows. This folder also holds the plan for the whole module, in the [proposal](proposal.md), and the kit this module shares with the module *Data management*, in [`shared/`](shared/).

**Status:** set up on 5 November 2026, on assumptions the author reviews later; the films are being made, each marked draft on the site, and appear in the table below as each one is out. On the site, the series opens the topic *Leading data people*, at `the-new-skills/`, once its opening film is out.

## The look and the sound

- **Sunlit plaster**, assumed on 5 November 2026 for the author to review (the module's *Decided* table, in [proposal.md](proposal.md)): the data team's rooms in daylight. A warm limewash wall, the sun from a window on the right falling on it in panes with the soft shadows of leaves, timber and graphite things, and one-page sheets of thick paper pinned to the wall, each with a band of colour only where the colour means something (blue design and plans, green done or value, amber risk, cost or waiting, orange data, violet only an agent). For the story of Graham's rooms, the teaching building is cut open like an architect's section, each room a small stage, lit when it's in use. People, the camera and words are the agent films' kit, unchanged: every word at least 28 px in the 1920-pixel frame and none under the captions, people with an adult's proportions and one three-quarter head, arms down, and a camera that glides in one room and cuts only between rooms. The kit is in [`shared/`](shared/), and every film of this module and of *Data management* bundles it as it is.
- **Its own sound in each film.** A lead instrument the site doesn't have, reserved for each film. Music under the opening story, then only now and then, at a chapter's turn or under a moment on screen; nothing loops, trembles or pulses, except an urgent pulse where a story turns to an error or a rush.

## How the films are made

Like the site's other series, each film is generated from code, with the engine, components, fonts and voice of *The Inner Life of Data*, *A Sharper Sketch*'s scenes, *From words to data*'s tools and the agent films' kit, and checked with the governance and agent films' checks (`twice.py`, `steady.py`, `jump.py`, `edges.py`, `legible.py`, `quiet.py` with `held.py`). A film keeps its own pictures and helpers in its own source. The films are about people, so tools appear only where the story's work happens in them, at the level of what people do. Each film's page links what a newcomer can read next.
