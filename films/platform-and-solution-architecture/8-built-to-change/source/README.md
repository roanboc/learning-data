# Rebuilding Built to change

*Built to change*, film 8 of 8 of *Platform and solution architecture*, and the series' last, is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, *The map before the data*'s look and people from [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/), and this series' own kit, [`../../shared/src/psa.js`](../../shared/src/psa.js), which every film of the series bundles: its title and end cards and mark, Ravi Menon, Japan's coast and the road, its walkers, horses, stations and pines, the platform drawn as a slab, and the map's ArchiMate elements. Only what is new for this film lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its source files (the series' kit, then `scenes.js`), the words the voice respells (Tomás, Ravi, Tōkaidō, Nakasendō, Gokaidō, Kobe, SQL), and the moment its poster shows (the seam on the practice map: one data product from its owner to its files, and the amber line between those files and the product that reads them). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). The film re-times itself to the voice; a line's `say` gives a year as the voice should read it. |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and two wordless moments: Ravi beside the platform, a part just replaced and the utility's own files where they were, then the title; and the platform again, every part with a stage, a date and a way out, then the end card. |
| `src/scenes.js` | The nine chapters, and what they share (prefixed `p8_`): the map's view, with the Nakasendō's stretch of 1764, the main line as planned and the line as it opened in 1889 (`P8_VIEW`, `P8_ROSE`, `P8_PLAN`, `P8_RAIL`, `p8_rail`), the farmers who rose (`p8_rise`) and a load handed on station to station (`p8_hop`, `p8_load`); the road at dusk, with the helper villages' farmhouses and fields (`p8_house`, `p8_field`), a station's count of horses and porters (`p8_count`), the duty of 1694 and what it grew to (`p8_duty`), a horse taken unpaid (`p8_unpaid`) and a train of the 1880s going by (`p8_train`); the utility's room, with Ravi and Tomás at its left (`p8_room`, `p8_people`), the platform's slab on the utility's own storage, its files and the seam between them (`p8_slab`, `p8_store`, `p8_file`, `p8_seam`), data products set down on it and what they read (`p8_prod`, `p8_reads`, `p8_hosting`), and the parts either side of a seam (`p8_part`); the storm tool with its twelve years, its lock and its one export (`p8_tool`, `p8_year`, `p8_yearBox`, `p8_storm`, `p8_lock`, `p8_export`), Grace's engineers' lines (`p8_net`), the questions asked when it was bought (`p8_asked`) and the new tool sending each night (`p8_newtool`, `p8_night`, `p8_moon`); a part's stage (`p8_stage`), the trial on another engine (`p8_ghost`, `p8_solar`, `p8_lines`), the twelve years checked as they're brought out (`p8_chk`), the practice map's own ArchiMate drawing (`p8_prodEl`, `p8_agg`, `p8_access`, `p8_assoc`, `p8_comp`, `p8_compose`, `P8_UP`); chips, rows, runners and routes (`p8_chip`, `p8_row`, `p8_runner`, `p8_flow`, `p8_route`, `p8_mark`, `p8_q`); a strip of the road and the line that comes back to the story (`p8_strip`); and text in flight marked as decoration on purpose (`PH_OFF`, `p8_deco`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds: a cor anglais, low and plain, with a double bass plucked with the side of the finger, both made here; D flat major, and B flat minor and F minor for what goes wrong, tuned pure from D flat; the series' mark, *the relay*, in D flat (the cor anglais's D flat, E flat and A flat rising, the A flat taken an octave down by the double bass and carried to D flat), under the title and the end card; the film's own figure, *the carry*, a short tune the cor anglais plays and the double bass plays again an octave down, alone where nothing carries it and carried where a part changes and the utility's own goes with it; no instrument, scale or sound of Japan, the road heard only as porters' feet and hooves, the railway as a low rumble with no whistle. Music under the opening story, then only now and then; each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs the series' tool (`../../shared/tools/run.py`) on this film: *The map before the data*'s `build.py`, *From words to data*'s other tools (its `legible.py`, with `--overlaps`, and its `music.py`), and the governance films' checks (`twice.py` and `hands.py` in `tools/check.py`; `quiet.py`, with `held.py`, in `tools/quiet.py`). |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/platform-and-solution-architecture/8-built-to-change/source/`; then `python tools/quiet.py` after `tools/audio.py`, to check the sound. The video is `dist/built-to-change.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/built-to-change/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/built-to-change/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `buy`, `seams` and `exit`. Progress is stored under `ld-built-to-change`.
