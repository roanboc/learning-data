Everyone at the table: each one heard, everyone told the same story

Fourteen people and groups want different things from retiring the old warehouse. Helena Novak maps them by power and interest, asks each what they want from data, in their words, and sends everyone the same page every month. When HR shows a real constraint, the plan moves, and everyone hears why.
