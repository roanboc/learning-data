Built to write, built to read: why the same award is stored twice

On graduation day, thousands of awards are written one at a time; on planning day, ten years of them are read at once. Merchants in Venice kept the same facts in two books for the same reason, five hundred years ago.

How to read the film

Blue is the shape built to write: tables with keys, and transactions. Green is the shape built to read: the star, its facts and dimensions. Warm ink is the merchants' books of 1494; red marks a copy that went wrong. The university, people and numbers are fictional. The narration is a synthetic voice.

English captions are on by default; turn them off with Captions. The video you download has none on the picture: play it with its caption file, in English or Spanish.
