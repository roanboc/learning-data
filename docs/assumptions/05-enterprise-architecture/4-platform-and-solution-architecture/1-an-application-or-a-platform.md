# An application or a platform?: questions and assumptions

Made on 11 and 12 November 2026 by the series' thread, from the author's brief of 11 November 2026 and the series' plan he confirmed that day (`films/platform-and-solution-architecture/proposal.md`), while he was away. Each row is a question he would have been asked, and the assumption the film was made on, for his final checklist.

| 1 | May the four new architecture series pair two instruments the site already has, when no new one fits? | Yes, if no film pairs them and no film of the topic plays either, with the reason written in the palettes list. This film is a continuo, a harpsichord with a bassoon: about 150 rarer instruments, found objects and pieces of apparatus were checked against every film's music on main and on every branch, and each one with a sound of its own was already some film's, sounded like one, or belonged to another culture (a plucked string's buzz is Japan's own). Worth a listen. | The film's music; the four series' palettes list |
| 2 | Which part of the Gokaidō opens the series? | Ieyasu's order to each station on the road to Kyoto, early in 1601: keep 36 horses, carry official travellers on to the next station, and no horses without his seal; then the five roads, a letter relayed in three or four days against two weeks on foot, and one road for every domain. Checked against the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism's Tōkaidō Q&A (in Japanese), nippon.com and Samurai Archives. | *Five roads from Edo* |
| 3 | When was the order given? | "Early in 1601": it came in the first month of Keichō 6 (慶長6年正月), which isn't January in the Western calendar, so the film doesn't say "January", and the order's paper says only 1601. | *Five roads from Edo* |
| 4 | Did the shogunate set the prices on the road? | Not quite: travellers with the seal rode free, and others paid, at fares partly fixed by the shogunate and partly agreed. The film says "others paid their way", correcting the plan's "set prices for everyone else", and adds that the stations and nearby villages carried the burden. | *An owner who answers to all* |
| 5 | Is Sekigahara "the battle that ended a century of war"? | As the common telling has it; the Toyotomi fell only in 1615, at Osaka. The film calls Ieyasu "the most powerful lord in the land", since he became shogun in 1603. | *Five roads from Edo* |
| 6 | Which number goes wrong, and whose is it? | The enterprise domain's measure for the regulator, minutes without power per customer, built on the platform from outages, the network map and the old storm tool. The rule that leaves major storms out came in the storm tool's export and was lost in an update; Ama Owusu, the regulatory lead of *The map before the data*, owns the regulator's measures. The numbers (about eight minutes, nineteen in March, nine after the fix) are invented. | *A number nobody owns* |
| 7 | Who owns the platform? | A sponsor, with a platform product owner, answering to every domain that uses it, and measured by its users' outcomes: how many build on it, how fast a first data product ships, how reliable it is and what it costs. No standard defines a platform's owner; this is drawn from practice and from *Team Topologies*' platform as a product. | *An owner who answers to all* |
| 8 | What is the "enterprise domain"? | The author's term, from his brief: the domain that owns the measures that cross domains, for performance and analysis, with its own owners. The film doesn't call it data mesh's aggregate data product; the script notes the likeness. | *The test*, *A number nobody owns* |
| 9 | Who is on the series page? | Ama Owusu joins the series' people, after Ravi, Tomás, Inés and Grace, since she is in this film. | The series page |
| 10 | Which Spanish words? | An owner is a *responsable* and the enterprise domain the *dominio empresarial*, as in the captions; a station is a *posta*, and the road the *camino*. The title is *¿Una aplicación o una plataforma?* | Spanish captions and page |
