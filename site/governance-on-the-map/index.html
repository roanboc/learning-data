Governance on the map

The second series on data governance. The submission is in, and the project that paid for governance ends in June. Yusuf Demir and Noor Rahman, the data architect, put governance on the university's architecture map: what it must be able to do, the services it offers, who does what, and where each piece lives.

One thread: Noor's map

Each film adds one layer of governance to Noor's map of the university: what governance must be able to do, and the services it offers; who does what to deliver one of them; and where each piece lives, with its copies kept in step. By the end, the map is the brief for the series that builds it.
