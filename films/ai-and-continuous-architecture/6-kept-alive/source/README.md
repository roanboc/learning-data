# Rebuilding Kept alive

*Kept alive*, film 6 of 6 of *AI and continuous architecture* and the last film on enterprise architecture, is generated from code like *The map before the data*'s films: the same engine, components, voice and tools (run through [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py)), and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/aca.js`](../../shared/src/aca.js): its mark and cards (`acaMark`, `acaTitle`, `acaEnd`), Babylon's sky, city, ground, folk, bricks, tags and quotes (`bab_*`), and the agent and its step on a task (`aca_agent`, `aca_stepPill`). A change there is a new version of every film of the series. Only what is new lives here:

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/ai-and-continuous-architecture/6-kept-alive/source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `python tools/build.py`, `python tools/audio.py`, `python tools/build.py` again (it bundles the soundtrack), then the checks: `python tools/check.py`, `python tools/legible.py`, `python ../../../../site-tools/fits.py`, `python tools/pace.py` and `python tools/quiet.py`. The video is `dist/kept-alive.mp4`.

## Publish

`python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/kept-alive/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/kept-alive/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `moved`, `copy` and `job`, each opening the film's lab, `keep`, in the series' labs. Progress is stored under `ld-kept-alive`.
