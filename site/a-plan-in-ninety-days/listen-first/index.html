Listen first: ask people, count, and write down what is

In February 2027 Helena Novak becomes the university's data manager. Before designing anything, she spends six weeks listening: 31 people asked the same three questions, the strategy read, the systems counted. By 15 March the current state is in five layers, and sent back to everyone she heard.
