Every area, one job: see it whole, and plan ninety days in three lanes

On her first day, in February 2027, Helena Novak finds 46 open requests. She places them on DAMA-DMBOK's wheel of data management, finds who on her team is deep in each area, and by Friday has her first ninety days on one page, in three lanes: people, strategy and operations.
