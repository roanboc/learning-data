Written once: one home per fact, and everything else generated

A definition copied into four places drifts in three. Give each fact one home: meaning in the conceptual model, taken from the business glossary where it already defines the term, decisions with why and who in a log beside what they're about, accepted gaps on the model as known limitations, what the build uses in dbt's YAML. Generate the rest, sync it one way, and ship a breaking change as a new version with a date. The next film follows one question from start to end.
