# The module's shared look and tools

Everything the films of *Data quality* share: the look assumed on 5 November 2026 for the author to review (*the quality bench*: the module's *Decided* table, in [`../proposal.md`](../proposal.md)), its three places, its people, paper cards and the inspector's instruments, the tools' pages with their official logos, and the tools that build and check a film. *Fit for use* keeps it here; *Quality as a habit* bundles it as it is, as the agent films' later series bundle `films/what-an-agent-is/shared/`.

Never change these files for one film. A film's own pictures, places and helpers go in its own `source/src/`, with a prefix of its own (`ge_` for *Good enough for what?*, then `hr_`, `vt_`, `lw_`, `hf_`, `rf_`, `ra_` and `sw_`); a change every film needs goes in a pull request of its own, and it is a new version of every film whose `release.json` names the file (AGENTS.md, *A shared fix is a new version of every film*).

## How a film uses it

A film lives in its series' folder, such as `films/fit-for-use/<n>-<slug>/`: `script.md`, `site.json`, `release.json`, `decided.md`, `captions/`, and `source/` with:

- `film.json`: `key`, `title`, `sub`, `howto` (the page's line on what the colours mean), `src` (its own files, in order), `poster` ([chapter, line, offset] for the poster's moment), `say` (the voice's respellings: `["\\bJun\\b", "Joon"]`, `["\\bMei\\b", "May"]` and `["\\bA I\\b", "AI"]` in every film, and for this module's people `["\\bMalia\\b", "Mah-lee-ah"]`, `["\\bFonoti\\b", "Fo-no-tee"]`, `["\\bAndr(é|e)s\\b", "Ahn-dress"]` and `["\\bPe(ñ|n)aloza\\b", "Pen-yah-lo-sah"]`, and each name the voice gets wrong), `story` (the chapters of its opening story, where music may play throughout), `urgent` (the chapters where its story turns to an error or a rush, the only ones where the score may pulse), and `"logos": true` for a film that shows a tool's mark (it bundles *The Inner Life of Data*'s `logos.js`, with Databricks and dbt).
- `src/narration.js` (one line per id, chapter by chapter), `src/vodur.js` (made by `tools/tts.py`), `src/breath.js` (longer pauses), its own pictures and `scenes.js`, and `src/i18n/es/captions.js`.
- `tools/score.py` (its music and sounds) and, for every other tool, a three-line runner:

  ```python
  # Runs the module's build.py (films/fit-for-use/shared/tools/run.py finds it) on this film. Run it from this film's source folder.
  import pathlib, sys
  sys.path.insert(0, str(pathlib.Path(__file__).resolve().parents[3] / "shared" / "tools"))
  import run; run.tool("build.py")
  ```

  one each for `tts.py`, `build.py`, `check.py`, `legible.py`, `pace.py`, `stills.py`, `audio.py`, `quiet.py`, `captions.py`, `render.py` and `publish.py`. A film of *Quality as a habit* points here instead: `parents[4] / "fit-for-use" / "shared" / "tools"` from its own `tools/`.

Its `release.json` `paths` name its own `source` (less its Spanish captions), this folder (`films/fit-for-use/shared`), the agent films' `films/what-an-agent-is/shared`, `films/from-words-to-data/shared`, `films/data-governance/shared/tools`, *A Sharper Sketch*'s `sketch3.js`, and *The Inner Life of Data*'s `core.js`, `style2.js`, `ui3.js`, `engine3.js`, `fonts` (and `logos.js` with `"logos": true`).

Then, from the film's `source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `build.py`, `check.py`, `legible.py --overlaps`, `pace.py`, `stills.py --size 1920`, `quiet.py` (the sound, before it's mixed, and again after every change to `tools/score.py`), `audio.py`, `build.py` again, `captions.py` (and `FILM_LANG=es python tools/captions.py`), and `publish.py` for the site (`--keep-sound` when only the pictures changed).

To try a frame before a film exists, write `function draft(ctx) { ... }` in a file and run `python films/fit-for-use/shared/tools/sketch.py draft.js build/draft.png` (`--scale 2` for a frame at 3840 by 2160): it draws one 1920 by 1080 frame with every call below, the logos included, and says how long it took.

## What each file holds

Sizes are in the 1920 by 1080 frame. Every word is at least 28 px as seen, drawn live with `dq_T` (so `legible.py` measures it), and nothing but the captions goes below y 890. Every top-level name starts with `dq_` or `DQ_`. Each file's opening comment says what each call takes.

**`src/look.js`**: the colours, and what each means in every film of the module (`DQ`). Six colours carry meaning, and only these: the four verdicts, `pass` (green), `hold` (amber: a person looks before it goes on), `stop` (red) and `none` (grey: not checked); `brass`, the requirement or the reference, what good means for this use; and `steel` (blue), a measure taken. Everything else is paper, stone, granite, oak and ink, and means nothing; the lamp is graphite, not brass, for that reason. Each meaning has three tones (`DQ_TONE`): `col` for tags, fills and lines, `word` for its words on paper or the wall, and `dark` for lines and words straight on the granite or the hall's oak; `DQ_VERDICT` gives each verdict's words, their colour and its icon. Words: `dq_T(ctx, s, x, y, {w, size, col, dark, a, ls, align, f})` (`col` a colour or a meaning's name; `f: "mono"` for what is typed), `dq_tw`, `dq_wrap`, `dq_label` (a heading in capitals). The places, each painted once in well under 2 s:

- `ag_place("bench")`, the data team's workroom: a wall of warm limestone lit from a window on the right, the sun lying on it in a soft patch, and the granite bench along the bottom. People stand behind it at about `s` 0.96 to 1, their feet at y 560 to 600, cut at its back edge (`cut: DQ_BENCH.back`); cards and gauges stand before the wall, which takes their shadow; the bench's front, under the captions, is dark and calm.
- `ag_place("benchTop")`, the bench seen from above: the granite filling the frame, a warm pool of lamp light, daylight from the right. Records, tags and gauges lie on it (`on: "granite"` for their deeper shadow; `dark: true` for words straight on the stone).
- `ag_place("hall")`, the Great Hall at the height of the stage: leaded windows above, oak panelling with fluted pilasters, and the stage's plum backcloth in the middle. A person on the stage is seen from the waist up (`y + 504 s` at least 1080) with `warm: 1`, or stands behind the lectern.

A film paints a variant as a place of its own, under its own prefix: `AG_PLACES.hf_dusk = c => dq_paintBench(c, {sun: 0})`. `DQ_BENCH` and `DQ_HALL` hold their lines. Props, drawn live: `dq_lectern(ctx, x, y, s)` (500 px wide at `s` 1; the person behind it is cut at `y - 28 s` and no wider, `s` 0.95 or less), `dq_clock(ctx, x, y, r, h, m)` and `dq_lamp(ctx, x, y, s, {on})`, a pendant lamp hung where no card goes over it. Icons: `dq_icon(ctx, kind, x, y, s, col)`, with `tick`, `pause`, `cross`, `dash`, `warn`, `clock`, `calendar`, `person`, `people`, `form`, `card`, `table`, `lens`, `list`, `printer`, `certificate`, `cap`, `seat`, `programme`, `count`, `building`, `link`, `why`, `flag`, `mail`, `chat`, `gauge`, `band`, `tag`, `lock`, `refresh`, `hourglass`, `sheet` and `tools`.

**`src/people.js`**: `dq_person(ctx, who, x, y, s, o)` draws a person exactly as the agent films' `ag_person` does (an adult's proportions, one three-quarter head, arms down, a hand only on what the story is about with `pose: "hold"`; the same `x`, `y`, `s` and `o`), and returns the hands as it does. `o.warm` (0 to 1) lays the hall's warm stage light over them. A person who fades is drawn whole first, then faded as one picture. The cast this module adds (`DQ_CAST`): `"malia"` (Malia Fonoti, the graduations officer, who runs the ceremonies and sends the list to the printer), `"andres"` (Andrés Peñaloza, a graduate of the Master of Data Analytics, the person the data is about) and `"andresGown"` (Andrés at the ceremony, in a plain black gown whose hood is lined in amethyst, a colour that means nothing else in these films, and no cap); the agent films' cast works too (`"mei"`, `"jun"`, `"sam"`, `"noor"`, `"yusuf"` and the others). Each was looked at full size, facing both ways, on 5 November 2026. Andrés's student number may appear in the data, never next to his face. `dq_nameTag(ctx, x, y, who, {role, col, align, dark, a})` writes a name and a role, with a small bar only in a colour that means something.

**`src/cards.js`**: everything on the bench is paper or an instrument. `dq_card` (cream card stock with a soft shadow falling left, its edge in a colour only when the colour means something; `ruled` for an index card, `dash` for a card that is missing), `dq_textCard(ctx, x, y, w, {kick, title, sub}, {edge, icon, mono, k})` (a card that fits its words, springing from its centre with `k`), `dq_record(ctx, x, y, w, {head, id, rows: [{k, v, mark, was}]}, {lit, litCol, rowA})` (a record typed on an index card, each field with a verdict's small stamp, an old value struck through; it grows to fit its longest value), `dq_tag(ctx, x, y, verdict, {to, text})` (a result: a tag in the verdict's colour, tied on with string; `"brass"` and `"steel"` tags carry words of their own), `dq_chip` (a small outlined label: a use, a dimension, an owner), `dq_band` (the tolerance band: the brass range, what good means for this use, and a steel pointer, the measure), `dq_dial` (a dial gauge, the same in a circle), `dq_figure` (a big number and its line), `dq_kicker(ctx, "1", "Good enough for what?")`, `dq_mark` (the module's mark: a dial whose brass range draws itself, then its steel needle settles inside it), `dq_title(ctx, S, t, t0, title, sub, line)` and `dq_end(ctx, S, t, t0, title, line, "Film 1 of 4", "Fit for use")`.

**`src/tools.js`**: a tool's page drawn for the film, never a click-by-click tour and never a screenshot: `dq_window(ctx, x, y, w, h, {logo, product, title, draw})` (a light page with a small bar naming the product, with its logo when the film says `"logos": true`, and no release status anywhere on it), `dq_rows(ctx, x, y, cols, rows, {lit, rowA})` (a table of words, a cell with a verdict's stamp) and `dq_code(ctx, x, y, lines, {lit, lineA})` (code or a file, typed, each run in a colour that means something). The student system, the portal and the printer are the university's own, named by what they do, never by a maker.

**`src/page.html`**: the standalone player page.

**`tools/`**: `lang.py` (where everything lives), `build.py` (what a film is made of, in order, and its checks: no name declared twice, no key named twice in one object, every cue word whole in its line, no caption over 140 characters), `quiet.py` (the governance films' sound check, run with this module's `lang.py`), `run.py` (runs these, then the agent films' `check.py` and `edges.py`, then *From words to data*'s other tools, on a film) and `sketch.py`. The agent films' `check.py` draws every frame and runs the governance films' `twice.py`, `steady.py` and `jump.py` and its own `edges.py`, all of which see the module's people because `dq_person` draws them with `ag_person`.

The module's films bundle the agent films' `kit.js` (the camera and its shots, places painted once, words and timing), `people.js`, `agent.js` (the agent, a ceramic sphere with a violet band, for *Read by an agent*) and `finish.js`, but not their `room.js` or `cards.js`: the white lab and its cards are that module's look, not this one's.
