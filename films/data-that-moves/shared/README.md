# The module's shared look and tools

Everything the films of *Modern data basics* share: the look assumed on 5 November 2026 for the author to review (the intro's platform map, drawn up close: the module's *Decided* table, in [`../proposal.md`](../proposal.md)), its people lit for the dark, the map's parts, glass cards, the tools' pages with their official logos, and the tools that build and check a film. *Data that moves* keeps it here; the module's other series (*Data as software*, *From tables to decisions* and *Documents as data*) bundle it as it is, as the agent films' later series bundle `films/what-an-agent-is/shared/`.

Never change these files for one film. A film's own pictures, places and helpers go in its own `source/src/`, with a prefix of its own (such as `mv_` for *Moving is easy, agreeing is the work*); a change every film needs goes in a pull request of its own, and it is a new version of every film whose `release.json` names the file (AGENTS.md, *A shared fix is a new version of every film*).

## How a film uses it

A film lives in its series' folder, such as `films/data-that-moves/<n>-<slug>/`: `script.md`, `site.json`, `release.json`, `decided.md`, `captions/`, and `source/` with:

- `film.json`: `key`, `title`, `sub`, `howto` (the page's line on what the colours mean), `src` (its own files, in order), `poster` ([chapter, line, offset] for the poster's moment), `say` (the voice's respellings: `["\\bJun\\b", "Joon"]`, `["\\bA I\\b", "AI"]` and `["\\bHR\\b", "H R"]` in every film, and each name the voice gets wrong, as the module's `numbers.md` gives them), `story` (the chapters of its opening story, where music may play throughout), `urgent` (the chapters where its story turns to an error or a rush, the only ones where the score may pulse), and `"logos": true` for a film that shows a tool's mark (it bundles *The Inner Life of Data*'s `logos.js`, with Databricks and dbt, and this module's, with Microsoft Fabric and Power BI).
- `src/narration.js` (one line per id, chapter by chapter), `src/vodur.js` (made by `tools/tts.py`), `src/breath.js` (longer pauses), its own pictures and `scenes.js`, and `src/i18n/es/captions.js`.
- `tools/score.py` (its music and sounds) and, for every other tool, a three-line runner:

  ```python
  # Runs the module's build.py (films/data-that-moves/shared/tools/run.py finds it) on this film. Run it from this film's source folder.
  import pathlib, sys
  sys.path.insert(0, str(pathlib.Path(__file__).resolve().parents[3] / "shared" / "tools"))
  import run; run.tool("build.py")
  ```

  one each for `tts.py`, `build.py`, `check.py`, `legible.py`, `pace.py`, `stills.py`, `audio.py`, `quiet.py`, `captions.py`, `render.py` and `publish.py`. A film of the module's other series points here instead: `parents[4] / "data-that-moves" / "shared" / "tools"` from its own `tools/`.

Its `release.json` `paths` name its own `source` (less its Spanish captions), this folder (`films/data-that-moves/shared`), the agent films' `films/what-an-agent-is/shared`, *From words to data*'s `shared/tools`, the governance films' `names.py`, `cues.py`, `twice.py`, `steady.py`, `jump.py`, `quiet.py` and `held.py`, *A Sharper Sketch*'s `sketch3.js`, and *The Inner Life of Data*'s `core.js`, `style2.js`, `ui3.js`, `engine3.js`, `fonts` (and `logos.js` with `"logos": true`).

Then, from the film's `source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `build.py`, `check.py`, `legible.py --overlaps`, `pace.py`, `stills.py --size 1920`, `quiet.py` (the sound, before it's mixed, and again after every change to `tools/score.py`), `audio.py`, `build.py` again, `captions.py` (and `FILM_LANG=es python tools/captions.py`), and `publish.py` for the site (`--keep-sound` when only the pictures changed).

To try a frame before a film exists, write `function draft(ctx) { ... }` in a file and run `python films/data-that-moves/shared/tools/sketch.py draft.js build/draft.png`: it draws one 1920 by 1080 frame with every call below, the logos included.

## What each file holds

Sizes are in the 1920 by 1080 frame. Every word is at least 28 px as seen, drawn live with `md_T` (so `legible.py` measures it), and nothing but the captions goes below y 890. Every top-level name starts with `md_` or `MD_`.

**`src/look.js`**: the colours, and what each means in every film of the module (`MD`): `flow` (light blue) is data moving and the platform's structure (lanes, connectors, mirrors, the catalog); `agreed` (gold) is what people agree on, a contract at an interface, a definition or a measure; `pass` (green) a check that passes, `fail` (red) an error, `wait` (amber) a person's turn (a review, an approval, a question); `personal` (lilac, always with a lock) personal data, which stays where it may be read; the university's systems (`MD.sys`: `hr`, `fin`, `sis`, `lms`), its business domains (`MD.dom`) and the medallion's layers (`MD.layer`) keep the colours the intro's map gives them; `ink`, `soft` and `dim` are words, quieter words and frames. Words: `md_T(ctx, s, x, y, {w, size, col, a, ls, align, f})`, `md_tw`, `md_wrap`, `md_label` (a heading in capitals). The places, painted once: `ag_place("map")`, the intro's navy with its faint grid, and `ag_place("night")`, the same without it; `md_vign` a vignette over a shot. Lanes of light: `md_curve(a, b, {bend})`, `md_path`, `md_lane(ctx, pts, col, a, {u, dash})` (dashed for a lane that only points the way: nothing is copied along it), `md_flow(ctx, pts, t, {kind, col, every, back})` (`"events"`, one change after another; `"batch"`, a whole file now and then; `"stream"`). Icons: `md_icon(ctx, kind, x, y, s, col)`, with `connector`, `query`, `mirror`, `share`, `api`, `event`, `batch`, `stream`, `table`, `view`, `volume`, `store`, `contract`, `doc`, `lock`, `key`, `tick`, `cross`, `warn`, `clock`, `refresh`, `person` and `eye`.

**`src/people.js`**: `md_person(ctx, who, x, y, s, o)` draws a person exactly as the agent films' `ag_person` does (an adult's proportions, one three-quarter head, arms down, a hand only on what the story is about with `pose: "hold"`; the same `x`, `y`, `s` and `o`), and lights them for the dark: the side away from a warm key light sinks into the navy, the body sinks into the night below the chest, and a cool rim of the map's light traces their back (`o.light`: `key`, `reach`, `shade`, `ground`, `warm`, `rim`, `rimA`, `rimW`). A person who fades is drawn whole first, then faded as one picture. The cast this module adds (`MD_CAST`), as the module's `numbers.md` sets them: `"ingrid"` (Ingrid Holm, HR, who owns the HR system's data), `"rohan"` (Rohan Desai, Finance's management accountant) and `"linh"` (Linh Nguyen, the casual tutor the data is about); the agent films' cast works too (`"noor"`, `"sam"`, `"jun"` and the others). Each was looked at full size, facing both ways, on 5 November 2026. `md_nameTag(ctx, x, y, who, {role, col, align, a})` writes a name and a role, with a small bar in the colour of their side.

**`src/cards.js`**: `md_card` (a pane of dark glass, its edge in a colour only when the colour means something; `dash` for what is shown but not copied), `md_textCard(ctx, x, y, w, {kick, title, sub}, {edge, icon, mono, k})` (a card that fits its words, springing from its centre with `k`), `md_pill` (a solid verdict, dark words on its colour), `md_tag` (an outlined tag), `md_kicker(ctx, "1", "Moving is easy")`, `md_mark` (the module's mark: a wheel of light drawing itself, its hub gold), `md_title(ctx, S, t, t0, title, sub, line)` and `md_end(ctx, S, t, t0, title, line, "Film 1 of 3", series)`.

**`src/map.js`**: the intro's platform map, its parts drawn near: `md_system(ctx, x, y, w, "hr")` (the HR system, the finance system, the student system, the learning platform, or `{n, s, c}`), `md_platform` (Databricks, with Unity Catalog along its foot), `md_layer` (bronze, silver, gold), `md_table(ctx, x, y, w, {name, kind, ghost})` (a table, a streaming table, a foreign table read in place, a mirrored one, dashed with `ghost`), `md_store`, `md_reader` (who reads it: Microsoft Fabric, Genie, dashboards, apps), `md_way(ctx, kind, a, b, t, o)` (the ways data moves: `connector`, `events`, `batch`, `query`, `mirror`, `share`, `api`), and `md_map(ctx, t, {lit, dim, parts})`, the whole map at once, with `MD_MAP` holding where each part is, for the camera to go there.

**`src/tools.js`**: a tool's page drawn for the film at the level of architecture, never a click-by-click tour: `md_window(ctx, x, y, w, h, {logo, product, title, draw})` (dark glass with a bar naming the product, with its logo when the film says `"logos": true`, and no release status anywhere on it), `md_rows(ctx, x, y, cols, rows, {lit, rowA})` (a table of words) and `md_tree(ctx, x, y, items)` (a catalog's tree).

**`src/logos.js`**: Microsoft Fabric's and Power BI's icons and Fabric's icon for a mirrored catalog, Microsoft's official files, unaltered (`logo(ctx, "fabric", x, y, h)`, `"powerbi"`, `"fabricMirror"`). They are Microsoft's trademarks, used as Microsoft permits in training materials, and are not covered by this repository's licences (NOTICE.md). A film that needs another product's mark, such as GitHub's, adds it here from the product's official files in a pull request of its own, with a line in NOTICE.md.

**`src/page.html`**: the standalone player page.

**`tools/`**: `lang.py` (where everything lives), `build.py` (what a film is made of, in order, and its checks: no name declared twice, no key named twice in one object, every cue word whole in its line, no caption over 140 characters), `quiet.py` (the governance films' sound check, run with this module's `lang.py`), `run.py` (runs these, then the agent films' `check.py` and `edges.py`, then *From words to data*'s other tools, on a film) and `sketch.py`. The agent films' `check.py` draws every frame and runs the governance films' `twice.py`, `steady.py` and `jump.py` and its own `edges.py`, all of which see the module's people because `md_person` draws them with `ag_person`.

The module's films bundle the agent films' `kit.js` (the camera and its shots, places painted once, words and timing), `people.js`, `agent.js` and `finish.js`, but not their `room.js` or `cards.js`: the white lab and its cards are that module's look, not this one's.
