One thread: Helena's first month, and the seven people she finds

In 2027, a year from now, Helena Novak takes over the university's data team. In February, her first month, a chart of seven boxes and a list of 46 open requests are on her desk. Before she plans anyone's work from either, she sits down with the seven people, one at a time. Film by film, she learns who they are, changes the work that wears one of them down, asks each how they work, and the team writes its first page of agreements. It's on the wall when Ivy Reyes arrives on 1 March.

After a steel bridge was built beside it in 1968, a visitor found it decaying. Its people chose to rebuild it, and today four communities make it new each June.
