How to read the film

The past is drawn warm, like clay and parchment; the present is the films' dark glass. Each office keeps its colour: amber for the registrar, pink for short courses, violet for careers, green for the learning platform. The pale blue triangle joins a word, an idea and a thing. The university, people and numbers are fictional. The narration is a synthetic voice.

English captions are on by default; turn them off with Captions. The video you download has none on the picture: play it with its caption file, in English or Spanish.
