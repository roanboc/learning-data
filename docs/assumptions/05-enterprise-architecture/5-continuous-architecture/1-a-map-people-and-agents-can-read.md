Film 1 of 4 of *Continuous architecture* (`films/continuous-architecture/1-a-map-people-and-agents-can-read`), until 23 November 2026 film 11 of *The map before the data*. It moved when the portal was consolidated, and Rodrigo asked that morning to "prioritise the stories per architecture layer/domain, so let's rebuild the films that need to be rebuilt". Its opening and its close were made again in this series' true story; the present-day chapters, which he approved on 20 October 2026, are as they were but for three lines. Each question I would have asked, the assumption I made instead, where it shows, and what changes if it's wrong.

Written 23 November 2026.

| 1 | Which part of Babylon's story opens this film? | The diary of the sky, kept by the city's scholars from 652 BCE: the series' films 2 to 4 follow Etemenanki's rebuilding from about 620 BCE, so the opening film sets the scenario a generation earlier, in the same city, and its idea is the film's: a record kept one way outlives everyone who keeps it. | 3 | What did the fix give people? | Nearly six hundred years of the sky (652 to 61 BCE), the cycles later scholars used to predict the coming year's planets and eclipses (Venus every 8 years, Jupiter every 83), and eclipse records astronomers still read, which show the day growing longer by about 1.8 milliseconds a century (Stephenson, Morrison and Hohenkerk, 2016) | *The watch* | Three lines and the line of years |
