Quality on the platform: checks that tell the owner

At the university, a failed check named no owner, a warning ran for a year unread, and data quality was a survey, 3.9 out of 5. Now each check names its owner and its level, checks run where data is typed and loaded, monitoring watches the rest, and every result lands in one scorecard.
