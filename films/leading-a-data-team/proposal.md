## The brief

The first two series are about the skills of each person. This one is about leading the team they make, through four ideas, one per film, each taught from its own sources and never as anyone's philosophy (the module's *Decided* table). Simpler is better, and it doesn't just happen, so *Less, on purpose* stops, with the team, what nobody uses. Improvement is everyone's job, so *One small change at a time* shows the cycle of plan, do, study and act on one person's own work. A person is a person through other people, so *The team comes first* puts the team's collaboration and well-being first, then each member, then the manager. And a leader can't make every decision, so *Rules that let people decide* gives people a few clear shared rules, good tools and trust, and lets decisions be made where the knowledge is. Minimalism balances the last: few rules, each with its reason.

The series follows the team from May to October 2027 (the module's shared numbers): the 17 of 38 old reports not opened in twelve months; standing meetings from Monday 10 May 2027 (a 15-minute stand-up each morning, planning on Monday, a review every second Thursday); the alerts, from about sixty a week to twelve by Monday 7 June; Ivy's Monday refresh, two hours by hand in July, ten minutes, checked, by October; the night rota of four from Monday 5 July, after pairing on the platform in June; Sam's two weeks away in August, when nobody calls him; and the faculties' timetable officers, from October, reading *Teaching space use* to plan 2028's classes.
