## The author's answer, 12 November 2026

The expert review asked about rows 5 and 6: the film ordered requests by the cost of delay over the effort (Reinertsen's CD3, which SAFe calls WSJF, neither named), where risk meant what goes wrong while a request waits. Is that the method you'd teach? Rodrigo answered in the project chat on 12 November 2026: "We generally follow the principle value (positive risk or negative risk prevention) and effort."

So rows 5, 6 and 21 are replaced by the rows below, and the film was changed and recorded again that day. Rows 24 and 25 are the two to look at first: they go a step past what he said.

## The author's second answer, 12 November 2026

Row 26 left urgency out as a measure. That was put to Rodrigo in the project chat the same day: "Value and effort. This leaves out urgency. Two requests with the same value and effort are not equal if one loses its value next month. I'd keep time as part of value, without naming a method." He answered: "Agreed."

So the film says it, and was recorded once more. Row 33 is the one to look at first.
