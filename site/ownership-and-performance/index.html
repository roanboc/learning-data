The last series of Leading data people. From July 2027 Ivy Reyes owns her first data product, Teaching space use, which Facilities and the faculties read every Monday. What does it mean to own an outcome, how should a team measure its work, and how do people grow, or catch up?

One thread: Ivy's first data product, and her first year

From 1 July 2027 Ivy owns Teaching space use, the counts per room per hour that Facilities and the faculties read every Monday. When forty rooms' sensors change in August, she notices first and says so. The team reads its measures together to fix how the work flows, never to rank anyone. When Tomás, the report developer, falls behind after the reports move to the platform, Helena looks for the causes with him before judging. Ivy's year ends with her next goal: helping the next graduate start.
