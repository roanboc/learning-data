More than a junior: supervise an agent for what it is

Jun's team treats its AI agent like a junior who needs supervision. But it reads the whole project in a minute, forgets a correction by its next session, and drafts deleting a table read once a year. So the team limits its rights, keeps its memory in shared notes, and leaves every yes to a person.
