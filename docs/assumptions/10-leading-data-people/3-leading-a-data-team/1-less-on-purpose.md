| 13 | How do 60 alerts become 12? | Sam, who answers them, goes through them with Noor and Jun one kind at a time ("when this fires, what do we do?"). Confirmed in `numbers.md`. | Chapter 4, "alerts" to "twelve" lines |
| 14 | How much time comes back, and where does it go? | About five hours a week each, about 40 across eight people: "about one person's whole week". It goes to rebuilding the 21 reports on the platform, each with an owner. One rule keeps it (anything new needs a reader, a reason and an owner), and the wall comes back at each quarterly review (27 July and 26 October 2027, the data management module's dates). | Chapter 6 |
