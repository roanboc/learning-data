The films

Watch them in order: each film adds a layer to the map.

  1. 1Day one5 min · An org chart, 140 systems and a backlog: all true, none of it the place. Learn how it works before you ask the questions.
  2. 2Who it serves, and how it pays5 min · Edison's customers wanted light, not electricity. Who pays, who uses and who decides, and a canvas for each.
  3. 3Why it moves4½ min · Three letters pull the utility three ways. Who cares, what pushes, what must become true, and a principle to settle it.
  4. 4What it must be able to do5½ min · The runners changed; the ability to move a message stayed. A capability outlasts whoever does it, however and with whatever.
  5. 5How value reaches people6 min · Lunch crosses Mumbai, changing hands. Power comes back to forty homes. Value from the customer's side, and detail only where order matters.
  6. 6Who does it, and where meaning changes6 min · Japan runs on two frequencies. A utility has two kinds of customer. Roles, actors, and a translation at the edge.
  7. 7Today, and where it's going5 min · An imagined street in 2045. Today's 140 systems on the map. A target, and the path to it, with reasons.
  8. 8When strategy moves7 min · A social tariff for every eligible household. One change, walked down every layer to its owners, rules and contracts.
