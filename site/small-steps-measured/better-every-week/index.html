Better every week: improve how the team works, one change at a time

In September 2027, a request to Helena's team waits six working days for its first question back, and nobody stops to look. She keeps half an hour every Friday: one measure, one small change, tried for a week, then kept or dropped out loud. By December, fourteen changes are kept.
