# Decided: More than a platform

The film's own decisions. They make the *Decided* table in [proposal.md](proposal.md): run `python site-tools/build_series.py` after adding a row.

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 12 November 2026 | A second introduction film, *More than a platform* (*Más que una plataforma*), follows *The Inner Life of Data*: about three minutes on why a platform isn't enough, the topics on the learning route, and what a visitor can do on the site. It is the film to get serious about data and about using the portal (the author). |
| 12 November 2026 | Its tone is closer to a trailer than to a lesson, but it carries one real idea, the author's advice to a newcomer: understand the organisation, the definitions and the meaning, in environments that keep getting more complex. |
| 12 November 2026 | The reference behind it is DAMA-DMBOK's wheel, named once as one well-known map; the film says another would serve. The teaching order is the learning route, not the wheel. |
| 12 November 2026 | The film names every station of the route, in the route's order, and ends on *Leading data people*, the last station. It shows the portal's tools without going into how each works: watch, pause and think, the labs, the situations, the route and its goals, the search. |
| 12 November 2026 | The film comes second, after *The Inner Life of Data*. How to use the site in detail is a guided tour on the site itself, opened from a "?" in the header, not a film: a film of the interface dates every time the interface changes. |
| 12 November 2026 | Spanish narration uses the voice of the agent films (es-MX-DaliaNeural), as *The Inner Life of Data*'s Spanish cut will. English narration uses the voice the films have used so far. |
| 12 November 2026 | The title is *More than a platform*. On Start here it is a poster card that opens the film's own page, to see whether that works better than a second player on the same page (the author had thought of the same page). |
| 12 November 2026 | No story from the past: it is a follow-up, and its story is the ending of *The Inner Life of Data*. No Pause and think questions. |
| 12 November 2026 | The closing line doesn't call the platform easy, because it isn't. It says that platforms have matured, and that the hard part is now everything else, in as few words as it can (the author). |
| 12 November 2026 | The author confirmed the proposal by merging it, with the closing line "Platforms have matured. The hard part is everything else." and the three assumptions under *In practice*. |
| 12 November 2026 | The script corrects the proposal in two places, for the author to confirm: three systems count *enrolled*, not four, since HR and payroll doesn't count enrolments; and the class's 312% stays with *The Inner Life of Data*, where a wrong sketch causes it. The meeting's two numbers are 176 and 149, for a class of 180 seats. |
| 12 November 2026 | The film was made from the first draft of the script, as the author asked that day ("Continue and tell me when done"); what it assumed is in `docs/assumptions/00-data-platforms/1-more-than-a-platform/`. It is 3 minutes 22 seconds, in six chapters, with a title card after the opening's question and an end card. |
| 12 November 2026 | The film has no people: it is shapes and words on the introduction's map, and two sheets of paper on a table stand for the two offices. The route is drawn as the Topics page draws it. |
| 12 November 2026 | On the site the film stands alone, on a page where a series' page would be (`/more-than-a-platform/`), with no series page, no Pause and think, no labs and no scenarios. `site-tools/build_series.py` learned both: a film that stands alone (`single` in `SERIES`), and a film with no questions. |
| 12 November 2026 | Its sound is chime bars, made for this film, as a station's announcement plays them. A station arriving is one soft tap, not a note. |
| 12 November 2026 | Its Spanish narration starts each line on its English cue and may run up to 0.35 s into the pause after it, never closer than 0.45 s to the next line, so short lines needn't be rushed. |
