# Making of: the films

Two short films at the top of the site's [Making of page](../../site/journey/index.md), before the written story. Same design language and voice as the other films; no labs.

- **Watch:** [https://roanboc.github.io/learning-data/journey/](https://roanboc.github.io/learning-data/journey/), and in Spanish, around the English films, [https://roanboc.github.io/learning-data/es/journey/](https://roanboc.github.io/learning-data/es/journey/).
- **Download the videos (MP4, 1080p):** [data-for-films.mp4](https://github.com/roanboc/learning-data/releases/latest/download/data-for-films.mp4) and [thats-not-quite-right.mp4](https://github.com/roanboc/learning-data/releases/latest/download/thats-not-quite-right.mp4).

| Film | What it shows | Length | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Data for Films* | How the films are drawn: one frame of *The Inner Life of Data*, at 2:31, taken apart from a single pixel and built back up with shapes, layers, components, a camera and time; then the two ways to play it, live and as a video | 4:58 | [treatment](1-data-for-films/treatment.md), [script with rigour sheet](1-data-for-films/script.md), [captions](1-data-for-films/captions/en.srt), [source](1-data-for-films/source/README.md) |
| *That's not quite right* | How the films are made: what the person drives, what Claude does, and why a cheap next version makes room for more pushback | 4:27 | [treatment](2-the-process/treatment.md), [script with rigour sheet](2-the-process/script.md), [captions](2-the-process/captions/en.srt), [source](2-the-process/source/README.md) |

Both are first cuts, for review. Their players are sealed (see each source README), so both can play on one page.

The number in a folder's name only keeps the folders in order, and the release workflow and the site's links to GitHub use these names: don't rename a folder, and never show the number to visitors.
