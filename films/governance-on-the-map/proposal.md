# Governance on the map

*Proposal for the second of the three series of data governance: governance designed into the university's architecture map. Planned on 28 October 2026 from films 11 to 13 of the plan for data governance, [`../data-governance/proposal.md`](../data-governance/proposal.md), which the author approved on 23 October 2026. That plan holds the brief, the reference and its alternatives, the insights, the world, the people and the facts the three series share; this file holds what is this series' own. Status: its scripts are being written.*

## The brief

From the author, 28 October 2026: "Also, for data governance we should have 3 series in the data governance pack, the first one for data governance principles, the second one to architect it within the organizational map, and the third one making it operational and useful (beyond the policies)". This is the second.

The part of the plan it comes from, asked for on 20 October 2026: "I like the approach, but I think we would benefit of another section after video 10 with two or three videos focusing on designing data governance as a capability in enterprise architecture terms and the elements end to end (services, processes, business glossaries as business products, relevant DG roles and actors, information assets, models, etc). This should go before tooling and would link with enterprise architecture and the implementation would get the architectural context knowing there are multiple governance artifacts maintained in other repos and apps."

## The reference

The plan's: DAMA-DMBOK, named once in *Off the shelf*, with DAMA-DMBOK's roles and DCAM's capabilities as checklists for governance itself, not as the map; and the ideas of *The map before the data*, drawn in ArchiMate 4, as that series draws them.

## Rules from the plan

- No code, settings files, pull requests or tool screens: the university's own papers, boards and screens, in plain words. Applications are named for what they do (the glossary, the catalogue, the data platform, the data team's repository); product names wait for *Governance you can use*.
- At most one diagram-like picture per film: Noor's map, painted as a board in her room, which gains a layer in each film.
- Each opening story follows the four beats in AGENTS.md, checked against Dutch, Spanish and Korean sources when its film is scripted.
- Each film's title and end cards say "Film 1 of 3 · data governance" and "Film 1 of 3", and so on.
