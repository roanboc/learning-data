# How, more than who: questions and assumptions

*Leading data people* · *People first*, film 4 of 4 · merged, PR #452 · [pull request](https://github.com/roanboc/learning-data/pull/452)

[Watch it on the site](https://data.learn-all.org/people-first/how-more-than-who/)

Film 4 of 4 of *People first* (`films/people-first/4-how-more-than-who`), made on 12 November 2026 from the series' plan, which the author confirmed that day ("Yes, start the series as proposed and add the film for management styles"), and from the series' brief for this film. It was made as the module's other films were: no script review and no render, and it merges as a draft. It waited from 13 to 17 November 2026, as the author asked, and was brought onto main on 17 November. Its pull request (#422) was held: that day the author asked for more of the people and less of the data problem in this module's films, and for a softer look for its people (rows 23 and 24). It was made again in *Ink line* and merged on 18 November 2026 (PR #452), and on 19 November its story got views of its own and its boards and pages were drawn as a designer would (rows 27 to 29). Each question I would have asked, the assumption I made instead, where it shows, and what changes if it's wrong. The series' own questions are in [`0-series.md`](0-series.md).
