Rules that run: measure quality from what ran

The university's policy says data quality is monitored, but monitored means a yearly survey of how each owner feels about their data. Yet the data team already runs 160 checks every night. Yusuf gives each check an owner and a dimension, each owner sets its level, and the scorecard is read from what ran.
