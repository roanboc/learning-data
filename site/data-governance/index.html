Governance you can use

A series on data governance that runs where the work happens. Yusuf Demir starts as the university's data governance lead, with a shelf of thirty-four policies, and the same week two reports disagree on one number. Film by film, he follows one symptom at a time to what's worth keeping, and to who decides and acts: who decides, one word in one home, who may see it, rules that run, fixes at the source, change without surprises, products with owners, an agent that checks, and the people who teach it all. Then three films draw it all as one model, on the university's architecture map, and six build it, with dbt and Databricks.

One thread: the number reported to the government

Every year, the university reports its awards to the government. This year, the census report says 4,812 and the draft submission says 4,867, and the same week the meaning of award changes. None of the thirty-four policies would have caught it. Film by film, Yusuf puts one piece of governance in place where the work happens, so the number can be trusted on the day it's sent, and shows what's left on the shelf.
