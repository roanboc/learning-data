Measure the work, not the people: read it together, fix the system

In 2027 the university's data team reads its measures together every second Thursday, and Helena asks what slowed the work, never who. Waits in how the work was arranged are found and removed, each measure is read beside one that balances it, and asked to count each person's tickets, she says no.
