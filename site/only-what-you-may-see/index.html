Only what you may see

The second series of Data protection and privacy. The Faculty of Health asks for learners' records for a study. Three films show agreements, classification and the ways to hide a value, with no product named; five build them in Databricks and dbt.

The films

Watch them in order: the ideas first, then the tools.

An agreement first

Each person signs a promise, each use gets an agreement, and access is made from it: the end of an agreement, not the start.

Film 3 of 8 · Ways to hide a value

Show the least

A study needs learners' records without knowing who they are. Each column gets a way to hide it that keeps what the study needs.

7½ min · Pause and think · Only what you may see

One thread: a study's request, from a question to a proof

Anil Kurian, an analyst in the Faculty of Health, asks for learners' records for a study of how health students progress. Film by film, the request becomes a signed agreement; every column gets its class from its owner; the class travels to every table made from it; one policy hides each class wherever it lands, and shows the faculty only its own learners; copies keep the rules; and a review proves it held, until the agreement's end date.
