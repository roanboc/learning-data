# More than a platform

*The film that follows The Inner Life of Data, on a page of its own.*

It introduces the portal directly: why *Learning Data* exists (many courses are too technical, or too neutral), the route of stations from the foundations to leading data people, the example dbt project with its governance folders, what the portal adds beyond DAMA, and enterprise data architecture. It shows a question, a lab and a scenario in action. It has no story from the past, no labs, no scenarios and no Pause and think of its own (the author, 14 November 2026).

**Status:** the proposal was confirmed on 12 November 2026; the film was reshaped from the author's brief and built on 14 November 2026.

## The look and the sound

- **The intro's platform map**, as the films of *Modern data basics* draw it ([`../data-that-moves/shared/`](../data-that-moves/shared/README.md)): dark navy, glass cards, words in white. The learning route is drawn as the Topics page draws it: one blue line, an amber loop for sharing and protecting, a violet loop for agents. The film has no people: it is shapes and words, with the example project's windows drawn with GitHub's, dbt's and Databricks' marks.
- **Chime bars**, made for this film: single bars struck with a yarn mallet, as a station's announcement plays them before it names the next stop. Music under the opening, then only now and then; a station arriving is one soft tap.
- **Spanish**: captions, the page, and a Spanish narration (`tools/audio_es.py`, es-MX-DaliaNeural) over the same pictures.
