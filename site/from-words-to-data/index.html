The films

Watch them in order: each film stands alone, and each builds on the ones before it. They make most sense after The Inner Life of Data and A Sharper Sketch.

  1. 1What's in a word6 min · Four offices, four numbers, one word. Before you can count anything, you have to agree what it is.
  2. 2Older than the systems5½ min · Clay, wax, paper, a digital key: the materials changed, the idea didn't. Model it once, and hold every system up to that.
  3. 3Built to write, built to read5 min · One set of facts, two shapes: one built to write each award, one built to read ten years of them.
  4. 4Many ways to read4 min · Star, vault or one wide table? Each shape has a job and a place. Choose per question, not per fashion.
  5. 5Both at once4 min · One database that writes and reads. It removes the copy, not the model.
  6. 6Meaning machines can read5½ min · An assistant answers from the meaning it can read. Glossary, taxonomy, ontology and semantic layer stack.
  7. 7Keeping it true5 min · Three dashboards, three numbers for one measure. The agent recommends, people approve, and everything is versioned.
