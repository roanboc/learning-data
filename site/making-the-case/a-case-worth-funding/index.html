A case worth funding: options, costs, benefits and one clear ask

The old warehouse costs A$310,000 a year, and its licence renews unless someone decides. Helena Novak, with Rohan Desai from Finance, asks the data council to retire it: three options, one carrying on as now; costs once and every year; benefits with owners and dates; one clear ask, on one page.
