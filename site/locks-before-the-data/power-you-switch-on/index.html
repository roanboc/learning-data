Power you switch on: great power, only for a task

Sam's and Jun's everyday accounts, the ones that open their email, can read every file in the storage, change the admin settings and restore any table, every hour of every day. Whoever takes one of those accounts takes all of it. How do they keep that power off until a task needs it?

Read the guidance yourself

The film names no product and no standard. These are the public guides its admin accounts follow, read on 10 November 2026.

  • NCSC: Use privileged access managementThe UK's guide to admin power given just in time and just enough: asked for one job, with a reason, approved by someone else, and short-lived.
  • CIS Controls: Account managementControl 5, whose safeguard 5.4 keeps admin rights in dedicated admin accounts, and everyday work, email and the web in an account without them.
  • NIST SP 800-53: Security and privacy controlsThe catalogue's least privilege control, AC-6: only the access a task needs, everyday accounts for everyday work, and every admin action logged.
