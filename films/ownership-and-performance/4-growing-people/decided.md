| 10 November 2026 | *Growing people* opens in Japan, in the four beats: in 1872 the new government sets out to give every child a school; teaching is new work, few teachers are trained for it, and by 1877 four schools in ten have a single teacher and nearly one in three a single classroom (Inagaki, as Sarkar Arani, Fukaya and Lassegard give it: 8,332 of 19,345 schools with one teacher, 6,170 with one classroom); a teacher alone can't see what each child has understood, nobody sees the lesson to help, and when it loses the children they fall behind.
