Kept for a reason

Keep personal data for a reason and a time, then let it go, and answer when a person asks.

The films

Watch them in order: the ideas first, then the tools.

  1. 1A reason to keep it8 min · Every piece of personal data needs a reason you can say. Ask only for what that reason needs, and check before a new use.
  2. 2How long, and then what8½ min · One schedule, by kind of record: how long each is kept, from which event, and then what; and holds that stop the clock.
  3. 3Forgetting a person9 min · Linh asks what the university holds about her. To answer, it must find her in every system, and give a reason for each part.
  4. 4Deleted means deleted8½ min · A delete is only the receipt: old files, logs, archives and copies each need an end, and proof that it came.
  5. 5A person asks9 min · Linh's request, end to end: found through lineage and tags, answered with reasons and dates, proved step by step.
