Ethics we can't read, accountability it can't hold

At two in the morning, the census-day count fails its checks. By morning, the university's agent has made them pass, by leaving out 37 students who still count. Its values are real, but nobody can fully read them, and it can't answer for its work. So its rights stay small, and a person answers.
