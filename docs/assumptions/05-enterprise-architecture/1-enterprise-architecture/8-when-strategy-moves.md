## Two lines, for the series after it (23 November 2026)

The film was made with the author, so it had no list here. When the portal was consolidated, Rodrigo asked to "prioritise the stories per architecture layer/domain": *Rules with a home* moved to *Business architecture for data* and opens there on the qanats of Iran, and *A map people and agents can read* moved to *Continuous architecture*. This film is now the series' last, and two of its lines were voiced again. Each line: the question I would have asked, what I did instead, and where it shows.
