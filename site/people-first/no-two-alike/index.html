No two alike: ask each person how they work, and never sort them

In February 2027 Helena Novak, the university's new data manager, reads Erin Walsh's silence in a meeting as lack of interest. She's wrong. People differ by degree, not by type. So she asks each person how they work, each writes a page in their own words, and her meetings change to suit them all.
