Risks, said out loud: one sentence, one owner, one early sign

In 2027, Helena's team is moving 21 reports off the old warehouse. The data council says how much risk it will take; the team writes eight risks, each one sentence with an owner and an early sign. A pre-mortem finds one risk's cause, and on 16 June its early sign shows in time.
