# Rebuilding A road people choose

*A road people choose*, film 7 of 8 of *Platform and solution architecture*, is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, *The map before the data*'s look and people from [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/), and this series' own kit, [`../../shared/src/psa.js`](../../shared/src/psa.js), which every film of the series bundles: its title and end cards and mark, Ravi Menon, Japan's coast and the road, its walkers, stations and pines, and the platform drawn as a slab. Only what is new for this film lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its source files (the series' kit, then `scenes.js`), the words the voice respells (Tomás, Ravi, Edo, Tōkaidō, Ise, oshi), and the moment its poster shows (Ravi beside the road teams choose, lit along the platform, with the teams' lights going along it, a data product of each of three domains on it, and what makes it a road teams choose). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). The film re-times itself to the voice; a line's `say` gives a year as the voice should read it. |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and two wordless moments: the road teams choose, then the title; and the road at dusk beside the platform's path lit end to end, then the end card. |
| `src/scenes.js` | The ten chapters, and what they share (prefixed `p7_`): the map with the ways to Ise, the Miyagawa and its ferry (`p7_ways`, `P7_TOISE`, `P7_MIYA`), a village at dusk with its farmhouses, coins and lots (`p7_house`, `p7_coin`, `p7_lots`), an oshi with his box (`p7_oshi`), the grove of the shrines (`p7_grove`), and the sheets that followed (`p7_sheet`); the utility's room, with Ravi at its left (`p7_room`, `p7_me`), the platform's slab and the data products set down on it (`p7_slab`, `p7_prod`), the path laid along it with its stations and guardrails (`p7_road`, `p7_rails`, `p7_light`, `p7_at`), the form, the repository and the six weeks (`p7_form`, `p7_repo`, `p7_weeks`, `p7_slip`), the teams that gave up (`p7_gave`); chips, rows, runners and routes (`p7_chip`, `p7_row`, `p7_runner`, `p7_flow`, `p7_route`, `p7_mark`, `p7_q`); a strip of the road that comes back to the story (`p7_strip`); and text in flight marked as decoration on purpose (`PH_OFF`, `p7_deco`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds: a violin as it was played about 1600, bowed plainly, with a gut-strung mandolin plucked with the fingertip, both made here; A flat major, and F minor for what goes wrong, tuned pure from A flat; the series' mark, *the relay*, in A flat (the violin's A flat, B flat and E flat rising, the E flat taken an octave down by the mandolin and carried to A flat), under the title and the end card; the film's own figure, *the way*, a short tune the violin sets out on alone and the mandolin takes up a beat behind, alone where nobody takes the road and followed where people choose it; no instrument, scale or sound of Japan, and the road heard only as feet, coins, lots, paper and a wide river. Music under the opening story, then only now and then; each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs the series' tool (`../../shared/tools/run.py`) on this film: *The map before the data*'s `build.py`, *From words to data*'s other tools (its `legible.py`, with `--overlaps`, and its `music.py`), and the governance films' checks (`twice.py` and `hands.py` in `tools/check.py`; `quiet.py`, with `held.py`, in `tools/quiet.py`). |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/platform-and-solution-architecture/7-a-road-people-choose/source/`; then `python tools/quiet.py` after `tools/audio.py`, to check the sound. The video is `dist/a-road-people-choose.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/a-road-people-choose/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/a-road-people-choose/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `weeks`, `rules` and `count`. Progress is stored under `ld-a-road-people-choose`.
