Fit for use

A list can pass every check and still be wrong. Ask what it's for, measure it, and check it against the truth and the clock.

The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1Good enough for what?7 min · The graduation list seated the Great Hall well and still sent 41 certificates to be printed again. Good depends on the use.
  2. 2A hundred records8 min · The list passed every check. A hundred records looked at by hand said how good it was; a profile of all of it said where to look.
  3. 3Valid isn't true9 min · Every name passed the checks, and 88 were still wrong. Only a reference outside the data, such as the graduate, says what's true.
  4. 4Late is wrong too8½ min · The night's file didn't come, every check passed, and 23 graduates were missing. Agree how fresh, and say how old.
