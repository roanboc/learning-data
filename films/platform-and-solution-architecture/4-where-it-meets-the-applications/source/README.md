# Rebuilding Where it meets the applications

*Where it meets the applications*, film 4 of 8 of *Platform and solution architecture*, is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, *The map before the data*'s look and people from [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/), and this series' own kit, [`../../shared/src/psa.js`](../../shared/src/psa.js), which every film of the series bundles: its title and end cards and mark, Ravi Menon, Japan's coast and the five roads, the road at dusk, its stations, horses and walkers, and the platform drawn as a slab with each domain's logic set down on it. Only what is new for this film lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its source files (the series' kit, then `scenes.js`), the words the voice respells (Tomás, Ravi, Yasmin Saleh, Shinano, chūma, Iida, Matsumoto, shogunate, Lakeflow), and the moment its poster shows (the 34 flows sorted onto their routes, every one on one map both teams keep). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). The film re-times itself to the voice. |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and two wordless moments: every flow on its route, on one map, then the title; and the two platforms, every flow on one map, then the end card. |
| `src/scenes.js` | The nine chapters, and what they share (prefixed `p4_`): Yasmin Saleh (`PEOPLE.yasmin`); Shinano on a map whose camera glides (`p4_cam`, `p4_land`, `p4_shinano`, `p4_ridges`, `p4_rivers`), its passes, the road from Matsumoto to Iida and the roads out to both coasts (`P4_PASS`, `P4_INA`, `P4_OUT`); the road at dusk with its station (`p4_road`), a load, a coin and a string of the farmers' horses (`p4_pack`, `p4_coin`, `p4_string`); the utility's five applications, the integration platform with its gateway and events, and the data platform (`P4_APPS`, `P4_BUS`, `P4_DP`, `p4_util`); the 34 flows and their kinds (`P4_KIND`, `P4_F`); a message and a light running along a route (`p4_msg`, `p4_send`, `p4_runner`); a night's meter readings in bulk, and the queue they would make as messages (`p4_bulk`, `p4_queue`); the flow map (`p4_map`); a feeder (`p4_feeder`); and a name on a chip (`p4_chip`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds: a hurdy-gurdy, whose wheel bows its melody string so the sound never breaks between notes, with a bandora, the wire-strung bass of the English consort, each note plucked and gone before the next, both made here; F major, D minor and A minor for what goes wrong, tuned pure from F; the series' mark, *the relay*, in F (the hurdy-gurdy's F, G and C rising, the C taken an octave down by the bandora and carried to F), under the title and the end card, over the hurdy-gurdy's drone on F, which sounds nowhere else; the film's own figure, the bandora's phrase plucked note by note, as goods went from station to station, and the hurdy-gurdy's played through, as a farmer's horse went the whole way, in the opening, and the two together at the end; no instrument, scale or sound of Japan, and the rivers, the passes and the road heard only as water, hooves, feet, coins and a load set down. Music under the opening story, then only now and then; each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs the series' tool (`../../shared/tools/run.py`) on this film: *The map before the data*'s `build.py`, *From words to data*'s other tools (its `legible.py`, with `--overlaps`, and its `music.py`), and the governance films' checks (`twice.py` and `hands.py` in `tools/check.py`; `quiet.py`, with `held.py`, in `tools/quiet.py`). |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/platform-and-solution-architecture/4-where-it-meets-the-applications/source/`; then `python tools/quiet.py` after `tools/audio.py`, to check the sound. The video is `dist/where-it-meets-the-applications.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/where-it-meets-the-applications/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/where-it-meets-the-applications/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `kinds`, `route` and `score`. Progress is stored under `ld-where-it-meets-the-applications`.
