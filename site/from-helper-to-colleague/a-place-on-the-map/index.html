A place on the map, before an agent works alone

On Monday 2 November, the planning agent joins Planning, to work on its own. If it simply started, nobody could say what it may do, who answers for it, or who can stop it. So it takes its place on the university's map first: a role on three of Planning's seven steps, an owner, and a gate to pass.
