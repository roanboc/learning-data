Explaining so people can decide: the decision first, and what it turns on

In May 2027, Graham Ellis must put one choice to the University Council, on one page: a new teaching building now, or not yet? Ivy Reyes's first draft runs to twelve pages, with the answer on page nine. With Helena, she starts the page with the decision, and draws the week's picture beside it.
