One thread: payroll's export, from a script to software

Every second Thursday, payroll's figures reach Finance through an export that ran for years as a script on one person's laptop. Sam Okafor and Jun Park move it into the data project, where it's versioned, reviewed, tested and deployed the same way every time. Then one night the job that loads the approved claims fails, and the platform finds the cause and proposes a fix, which Sam reviews before pay day.
