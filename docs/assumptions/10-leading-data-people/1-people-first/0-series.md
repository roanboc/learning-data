# People first: the series' own questions and assumptions

> Written on 12 November 2026, when the series was registered on the site from the proposal the author confirmed that day (pull request [#381](https://github.com/roanboc/learning-data/pull/381)). Each film's own questions and assumptions are in its own file beside this one, named as its film folder is, written in the film's own pull request.

*People first* opens the module *Leading data people*, before *The new skills*. Its plan is in [`films/people-first/proposal.md`](../../../../films/people-first/proposal.md), with its *Decided* table; what the author decided there (the five broad traits as the reference, and never for assessment) isn't repeated here. Below is what the series takes as an assumption, for the author to confirm or correct.

| # | Question | Assumption | Where it shows | If it's wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In your first weeks with a new team, what do you ask each person, and what do you never ask? | Helena asks about each person at work: what they're proud of, what they'd like more of, what gets in their way, and what she should know to work well with them. She never asks about health, family or anything a person doesn't offer. | *People before roles* | A scene of that film |
| 2 | For wellbeing, what do you watch for, and what has really helped? | Taken from the author's papers of 2015 and 2016, read as his view then: a rhythm for planning, no weekends as the plan, time to improve, and asking the whole team the same questions in writing. What a leader forgets is each person, behind the product and the team. | *Wellbeing is in the work* | The film's practices change |
| 3 | Has a team of yours taken a personality test, and what came of it? | Not known. The film shows a test only as a conversation a team chooses, with four rules: each person's choice, their own results, degrees and not boxes, and never used to hire, assign or judge. It names no test and draws no type letters. | *No two alike* | A scene of that film |
| 4 | Which agreements have your teams actually kept? | The team's first page holds five: how we meet, how we ask for help, how we disagree, how we decide, and how we say "I can't make that date". It is read each quarter, and when someone joins. | *How, more than who* | The page's lines |
