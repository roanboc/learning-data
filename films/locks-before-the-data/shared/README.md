# The module's shared look and tools

Everything the films of the module *Data protection and privacy* share: the look the author chose on 5 November 2026, **Night glass, frosted glass with solid colour** (variant GA of the style prototype), its people lit at night, the agent, the cards, the tools' pages, and the tools that build and check a film. *Locks before the data* keeps it here; *Only what you may see* and *Kept for a reason* bundle it as it is, as the agent films' later series bundle `films/what-an-agent-is/shared/`.

Never change these files for one film. A film's own pictures, places and helpers go in its own `source/src/`, with a prefix of its own (such as `wi_` for *Who is it, really?*); a change every film needs goes in its own pull request, and every film whose `release.json` names these files is built and published again.

## The look, in one paragraph

A dark petrol hall where every boundary is a pane of glass: frosted where what is behind it may not be read, clear where it may. Light runs along the panes' edges. What is shown (badges, cards, pills, ticks, tags) is frosted glass with words in ice white, and solid, bright colour where the colour means something, with dark words on it. People stand in the light of what they look at, with a cool rim from the glass behind them. The colours mean the same in every film: **cyan** an identity (who or what it is), **green** allowed, **coral** denied or closed, **amber** power switched on, **violet** hidden (personal data, secrets), a quieter violet an indirect identifier, **grey** what needs no protection; **gold** is only the chip that says a card is genuine. Every word is at least 28 px in the 1920 by 1080 frame, as seen, and stands out at least 4.5 to 1 from what is behind it; nothing but the captions goes below y 890.

The ideas films name no product, cloud or tool, and draw none of the tools' pages. A secret (a token, a password, a key) is always a sealed card that says what it is and whose, never a character of it. No film says or shows a feature's release status.

## How a film uses it

A film lives in `films/<series>/<n>-<slug>/`: `script.md`, `site.json`, `release.json`, `decided.md`, `captions/`, and `source/` with:

- `film.json`: `key`, `title`, `sub`, `howto` (the page's line on what the colours mean), `src` (its own files, in order), `poster` ([chapter, line, offset] for the poster's moment), `say` (the voice's respellings: `["\\bJun\\b", "Joon"]`, `["\\bA I\\b", "AI"]` and `["\\bHR\\b", "H R"]` in every film, and each name the voice gets wrong), `story` (the chapters of its opening story, where music may play throughout; the first by default), `urgent` (the chapters where its story turns to a breach, a leak or a rush, the only ones where the score may pulse; none by default), and `"logos": true` for a film that shows the tools' pages.
- `src/narration.js` (one line per id, chapter by chapter), `src/vodur.js` (made by `tools/tts.py`), `src/breath.js` (longer pauses), its own pictures and `scenes.js`, and `src/i18n/es/captions.js`. A film lists the people it shows, in their light, so the player paints them before they're needed: `DP_PEOPLE.push("sam", "jun", ["mei", "lamp"]);`.
- `tools/score.py` (its music and sounds) and, for every other tool, a three-line runner:

  ```python
  # Runs the module's build.py (films/locks-before-the-data/shared/tools/run.py finds it) on this film. Run it from this film's source folder.
  import pathlib, sys
  sys.path.insert(0, str(pathlib.Path(__file__).resolve().parents[3] / "shared" / "tools"))
  import run; run.tool("build.py")
  ```

  one each for `tts.py`, `build.py`, `check.py`, `contrast.py`, `legible.py`, `pace.py`, `stills.py`, `audio.py`, `quiet.py`, `captions.py`, `render.py` and `publish.py`. A film of the module's other series points at this folder instead: `parents[4] / "locks-before-the-data" / "shared" / "tools"` from its own `tools/`.

Then, from the film's `source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `build.py`, `check.py` (which runs `contrast.py` and `legible.py` too), `legible.py --overlaps`, `pace.py`, `stills.py --size 1920`, `quiet.py` (the sound, before it's mixed, and again after every change to `tools/score.py`), `audio.py`, `build.py` again, `captions.py` (and `FILM_LANG=es python tools/captions.py`), and `publish.py` for the site.

To try a frame before a film exists, write `function draft(ctx) { ... }` in a file and run `python films/locks-before-the-data/shared/tools/sketch.py draft.js build/draft.png` (`--logos` to load the tools' logos first): it draws one 1920 by 1080 frame with every call below, and prints how long it took.

## What each file holds

The films bundle, in this order: *The Inner Life of Data*'s helpers (`core.js`, `style2.js`, `ui3.js`, and its `logos.js` with this kit's for a film that asks for them), the film's narration and timings, *A Sharper Sketch*'s `scene()`, two files of the agent films' kit, unchanged, this kit, the film's own files, the engine, and this kit's `finish.js`. Every top-level name here starts with `dp_` or `DP_`, except `ag_model`, the agent, named as the agent films name it so the checks follow it.

**From the agent films' kit** (`films/what-an-agent-is/shared/src/`, its README says more): `kit.js` for timing (`wAt(sc, "line", "word", offset)`, the moment a word is said, which `build.py` checks: call it directly; `ag_in`, `ag_io`, `ag_sine`, `ag_eo`, `ag_spr`, `ag_keys`), the camera (`ag_shots(ctx, S, t, end, [{at, place, keys, draw, later, cut}], dissolve)`, `ag_cutInto([...ids])`: in one room the camera glides on and only what changes fades; between rooms, cut), places painted once (`AG_PLACES.name = ctx => ...`, `ag_place(name)`) and scratch canvases (`ag_buf`); `people.js` for the people's shapes, faces and cast (`AG_CAST`: `jun`, `yusuf`, `mei`, `noor`, `sam`, `anil`, `clara`, `david`; this kit adds `ingrid`, Ingrid Holm, who owns HR's data, and `linh`, Linh Nguyen, the casual tutor, as *Modern data basics* draws them, so they're the same people in both modules), drawn through `dp_person` here. Draw words with `dp_T`, not `ag_T`, so they take the module's colours.

**`src/glass.js`**: the colours (`DP.T` for words, `DP.F` for solid colour, `DP.on` the dark words on it) and their meanings (`dp_t("allowed")`, `dp_f("hidden")`, or a colour's own name); words (`dp_T(ctx, s, x, y, {w, size, col, a, ls, align, f, glow, deco})`, `dp_tw`, `dp_lines`); blur (`dp_blurDraw`, which works where a browser has no canvas blur); frosted glass, which shows the place behind it blurred, from a copy made once per place (`dp_frostPlace`), so frost costs a frame nothing: the place is the one the shot draws, and a card over a scene drawn live names it in `behind` (a place's key, a canvas, or `false`); keep cards off people, whom the frost doesn't show; and what is shown on glass: `dp_panel(ctx, x, y, w, h, {col, r, glowA, glowR, behind})`, `dp_band`, `dp_pill(ctx, x, y, text, col, {size})` (solid, dark words; y is the words' baseline), `dp_tick` and `dp_cross` (drawn on by p), `dp_chip`, `dp_seal`, `dp_scan` (the light that reads a badge), pictures for a badge (`dp_gearPic` a workload, `dp_personPic`, `dp_lock(ctx, x, y, s, col, open)`), `dp_badge(ctx, x, y, w, h, {kind, col, title, lines, mrz, t, scan, pic, seal, chip})` (600 by 372: an identity card of glass), `dp_card(ctx, x, y, w, {kick, title, sub}, {col, k, measure})` (a card that fits its words; returns its box), `dp_tag(ctx, x, y, text, col, {to, p})` (a label with a line of light to what it names), `dp_slip` (one line on a small panel), `dp_frost(ctx, x, y, w, h, paint, {col})` (a value that may not be read, under violet frost), `dp_sealed(ctx, x, y, w, label, {sub, revoked})` (a secret, as a sealed card), `dp_link` (a line of light, drawn on); and the frame around a film: `dp_kicker(ctx, top, title)`, `dp_mark` (the module's mark, a seal round a lock), `dp_veil`, `dp_title(ctx, S, t, t0, title, sub, "FILM 1 OF 9 · LOCKS BEFORE THE DATA")` and `dp_end(ctx, S, t, t0, title, line, "Film 1 of 9", series)`.

**`src/hall.js`**: the hall, `ag_place("hall")`, or a film's own: `AG_PLACES.hallBadge = c => dp_paintHall(c, {seed, horizon, vx, lights, panes, pools: [[x, y, r, a]], haze, after})` (where its lights and panes fall, where the floor meets the dark, and the pools of light where people stand). `dp_wall(ctx, x, y, w, h, {frosted, col, label})` stands a pane in a scene: frosted, what is behind it may not be read; clear, it may.

**`src/night.js`**: `dp_person(ctx, who, x, y, s, o)` draws a person exactly as the agent films' `ag_person` does (the same `x`, `y`, `s` and options: `t`, `flip`, `pose: "hold"` with `hold`, `gaze`, `nod`, `talk`, `smile`, `cut`, `covered`; arms down, a hand only on what the story is about), lit by `o.light`: `"glass"` (the default: the hall's cool light from what they look at), `"lamp"` (warm, for a lantern or a fire at night, in an opening story) or a light of the film's own (`{key: [x, y], kr, kk, amb, kc, shade, rim, rimA, rimW}` in figure space). Each person in each light is painted once, head and body lit pixel by pixel with a cool rim along their back and the top of their head, and kept; their eyes, mouth and a holding arm are drawn live. A person with `lanyard: true` (Sam, in the cast) wears a strap and a glass badge with a cyan band.

**`src/sphere.js`**: the agent, `ag_model(ctx, x, y, r, {t, a, col, halo})`: the agent films' sphere and band, in glass, the band lit in the colour of an identity. It is in one place at a time, never two (the checks follow it). `dp_sphereIcon(ctx, x, y, r, t)` is its picture, small, on a badge or a card.

**`src/pages.js`**: the tools' pages, for the films that show Databricks, GitHub, dbt and Azure as their docs name them on the day, drawn, not filmed. `dp_page(ctx, x, y, w, h, {tool, name, where, title, col})` is a window of frosted glass whose header is the tool's logo, unaltered (`tool`: `databricks`, `dbt`, `github`, `azure`, `entra`, `pim`, `managedIdentity`, `keyVault`, `storage`, `privateEndpoint`, `groups`, `users`), the tool's or service's full name beside it, and where in the tool the page is on the right; it returns the box its content goes in. In it: `dp_rows(ctx, x, y, cols, rows, {lh})` (a table: each cell a string, `{t, col}`, `{mono: true, t}` for a name as the tool writes it, `{pill, col}`, `{tick}` or `{cross}`; returns the y under it), `dp_field(ctx, x, y, label, value, {mono})` and `dp_switch(ctx, x, y, u, {col, label})` (off at 0, on at 1, in the colour of what it switches on).

**`src/logos.js`**: the GitHub mark and Microsoft's Azure and Entra icons, added to *The Inner Life of Data*'s Databricks and dbt logos for a film whose `film.json` says `"logos": true`. They are trademarks of their owners, used unaltered under their terms only to name the products, and credited in the repository's `NOTICE.md`: always shown with the product's or service's full name beside them, never cropped, flipped, rotated or recoloured.

**`src/finish.js`** and **`src/page.html`**: what a film needs before it starts (the fonts and logos; each place and its frost, and each person in `DP_PEOPLE` in their light: the video at once, the site's player the first place at once and the rest one picture at a time while the page is idle, once the film has started) and the video's motion blur; the standalone player page.

**`tools/`**: `lang.py` (where everything lives), `build.py` (what a film is made of, in order, and the agent films' checks when a film is built: no name declared twice, no key named twice in one object, every cue word whole in its line, no caption over 140 characters), `check.py` (every moment draws, no text is under 28 px (*From words to data*'s `legible.py`), every word stands out 4.5 to 1 (`contrast.py`), every place and its frost paints within 2 s and every picture of a person in their light within 0.12 s, and the governance films' `twice.py`, `steady.py` and `jump.py` and the agent films' `edges.py`: nothing shows twice where shots or chapters change, the agent included, no arm jumps in one frame, nothing jumps where the shot changes in one room, and nothing painted for a person reaches past their picture's edge), `contrast.py` (draws the film every half second with no words, and measures what is behind each word, from its darkest to its brightest), `quiet.py` (the sound, checked by the governance films' check with this module's `lang.py`), `run.py` (runs these and *From words to data*'s other tools on a film) and `sketch.py`.
