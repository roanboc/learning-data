## The promise

Every line passes two tests: someone who has never used an AI agent can follow it, and a data engineer who runs agents, a music historian and a data governance lead each find nothing wrong in it. *What an agent is* showed what an agent is made of. This series puts it to work, and this film starts with what it reads: an agent can only be as right as what it reads, and in a data team that comes in four kinds, code, metadata, skills and tools. A word whose meaning lives only in someone's head becomes a confident guess. Writing the meaning down once, where its owner keeps it and the project copies it, serves the agent and every person alike, and a team that used to wire up an agent each, with notes of its own, keeps one context instead. The film is about what the agent reads, and who writes it; where a definition lives is *Written once*'s, and two sides meaning different things is *It feels like understanding*'s. **Write it down once, and people and agents both use it.**

## The story in one paragraph

Arezzo, Italy, about 1025: a monk named Guido teaches boys to sing the church's chants. Each chant is learned by ear, from a master; the books carry small signs above the words that remind a singer who already knows the chant, but show only up or down, not how far. So the masters don't agree: in Guido's words, hardly one singer agrees with another, and there are as many chant books as masters, Leo's or Albert's; learning the chants takes about ten years, and even then imperfectly. Guido draws lines across the page, each line and each space one note, the line for F red and the line for C yellow, and every sign sits on a line or a space: what lived in the master's head is on the page. Within three days some of his boys sing chants they have never heard; a singer who took ten years is made in a year, or two at most; and in Rome, Pope John won't leave his seat until he has learned a verse he has never heard. With one more line, it is today's staff. An agent is like a boy who has never heard the chant. At the university, Clara, in Planning, asks Jun how many of the 18,412 students on census day were commencing. Jun's agent reads what its three read-only tools reach, finds nothing that says what the word means, reasons from the names, and counts 6,931, as sure as if it knew. Mei, in the registrar's office, knows at once: commencing means starting a course at the university for the first time, and a student back from leave isn't commencing. 719 were coming back; the right number is 6,212, and Planning would have planned for 719 new students who weren't new. The meaning lived in one head and one email of 2021. The film names the four kinds of context an agent reads, and what was missing: the meaning, in the metadata. Mei writes it once in the business glossary, and owns it; the project copies it into Planning's data with its source, a column carries it, a test checks it on four students, the contract promises it, and the notes for agents gain a rule: look a word up before you count by it, and ask its owner rather than guess. The team, whose members each wired up their own agent with notes of their own, like the chant books named after their masters, now keeps one context in the project, reviewed like code, with an owner for every meaning. Asked again, the agent looks the word up, counts 6,212 and cites Mei's definition, and a new analyst in Planning reads the same definition her agent reads: like Guido's boys, both can sing a chant they've never heard.

### 4 · What an agent reads · 4:03–4:49

**Narration.** Everything an agent reads for a job is its context, and in a data team it comes in four kinds. Code: the repository, with its recipes, its tests, and its notes for agents. ## Rigour to check before recording

Checked on 4 November 2026. The research notes, with the quotes each line rests on, are in `notes.md` beside this script's copy in the project's files (`agentic-data-engineering/series-2/1-context-engineering/`).

- **Arezzo, about 1025.** Guido, a Benedictine monk who had left the abbey of Pomposa, taught the singers of Arezzo's cathedral from about 1025, invited by its bishop, Tedald (Wikipedia; EBSCO Research Starters). "The church's chants, its prayers sung through the year": the chants of the Office and the Mass, sung through the liturgical year, which the antiphoner holds. "Learned by ear, from a master": the Prologue to his Antiphoner, "the wretched singers and their pupils, though they sing every day for a hundred years, will never sing by themselves without a master a single antiphon"; Wikipedia's *Neume*: "for centuries it was transmitted only orally".
- **The signs.** The neumes above the words: "inflective marks that indicated the general shape but not necessarily the exact notes"; the early ones "did not specify the pitches of individual notes, the intervals between pitches within a neume"; "They were mnemonics, reminding singers of how the melody was sung" (Wikipedia, *Neume*). "Up or down, but not how far" is the film's plain reading of "the general shape" and "did not specify ... the intervals". The page's chant is an Alleluia drawn for the film, its signs at rough heights; heightened neumes, set at rough heights to show the shape, came before Guido and still left the exact distance between two notes to the singer's memory; the film's page is one of those.
- **The masters and their books.** The Prologue: "One scarcely agrees with another, neither the pupil with his master, nor the pupil with his colleague. It is for this reason that the antiphoners are not one, nor yet a few, but rather as many as there are the masters in the various churches; and that the antiphoner is now commonly said to be, not Gregory's, but Leo's, or Albert's, or someone's else." The film's "hardly one singer agreed with another" and "as many chant books as masters: Leo's, or Albert's" say the same in plain words; an antiphoner is a chant book.
- **About ten years, and imperfectly; a year, or two at most.** Guido's letter to Brother Michael (*Epistola de ignoto cantu*, written after his visit to Rome): "if it happens that those singing [and praying] for their teachers, even after a decade of study, still have difficulty with the process, what do you think they will do for us and our assistants if after the space of a year, or two years at most, we can turn out a perfect singer?" (Wright and Simms's translation, from the Latin in the Thesaurus Musicarum Latinarum); in Hermesdorff's translation, the masters' pupils gained "kaum in zehn Jahren eine unvollständige Kenntnis des Gesanges" (barely, in ten years, an incomplete knowledge of singing), and Guido's school makes "im Zeitraum eines, höchstens zweier Jahre einen vollendeten Sänger" (a perfect singer in the space of a year, two at most). The series' openings had "in months"; the film follows the letter. Its "in Guido's words" introduces his own claim.
- **The lines.** The Prologue: "lines are drawn close together, and some rows of sounds occur on the lines themselves, others in the intervening intervals or spaces"; "certain letters of the monochord are written at the beginning of the lines or spaces"; "We use two colors, namely yellow and red ... wherever you see the color yellow, there is the third letter [C], and wherever you see the color red, there is the sixth letter [F]". Scribes had scratched or drawn a line or two before him; Guido's Prologue sets out the whole way of writing that lets a singer read a chant without a master, and the staff is "an innovation traditionally ascribed to Guido d'Arezzo" (Wikipedia, *Neume*), so the film calls it "Guido's answer". The film draws four lines, as chant is still written; a fifth gives today's staff.
- **Three days.** The letter: "after I have begun to teach this method to choirboys, after only three days they are able easily to sing completely unknown chants, a process that requires many weeks using other methods" (Wright and Simms); in Hermesdorff's translation, "konnten einige schon am dritten Tage ganz unbekannte Melodien mit Leichtigkeit absingen" (some of them could already, on the third day, sing quite unknown melodies with ease), so the film says "some of his boys" and "within three days".
- **Rome.** The letter: Pope John (John XIX, pope from 1024 to 1032) heard of the school and of how its boys learned chants they had never heard from Guido's antiphoner, sent three messengers for him, "looked through our antiphoner at length as if it were a miracle of nature", and "stayed put with it until he had learned on his own a verse that he had never heard before" (Wright and Simms); Hermesdorff: he "stand nicht eher von seinem Sitze auf" (didn't get up from his seat) until he had learned a verse he had never heard sung, the film's "won't leave his seat". About 1028 (Wikipedia); Wright and Simms date the letter, written after the visit, about 1033. Where the 1911 *Britannica*'s account differs (its dates, and the pope's number), the film follows Guido's own letter.
- **The monochord.** Guido's teaching tool: the letter's first rule is to play a new chant's letters on a monochord "and thus hear it just as if a master had sung it to you", which Guido calls good only for beginners; the Prologue writes "letters of the monochord" at the start of the lines. The film's sound uses it; the film doesn't name it.
- **The data story's numbers.** 18,412 students on census day, 31 March 2026, never "enrolments", are *A model, reasoning, hands and a loop*'s, reused exactly. 6,931, 719 and 6,212 are this film's, agreed in the series' `numbers.md` on 4 November 2026 before the script used them: 6,931 − 719 = 6,212, about a third of the students on census day, a share a university with many returning students might have. The agent's query counts every learner whose current studying version began in 2026, so a student back from leave, whose new version began this year, is counted as new: the mistake the story turns on.
- **"The government's rule too."** The film's government is unnamed, as the university is. Its rule is the one official statistics use: Australia's higher education data collection (TCSI) counts a student as commencing for a year "if they started studying in a course of study for the first time at the higher education provider ... in that year", and excludes "students who are resuming the same course ... after an absence"; a student who starts a new course is commencing in it. The project's term and its test follow it.
- **The four kinds of context.** The brief's, from Rodrigo's practice: everything kept as code (the repository, read the same way by every person and agent), the metadata (business context, models, contracts and lineage), skills, and tools. "Context engineering" is the name practitioners use for choosing what an agent reads for a job and writing it down (the term spread in 2025; Anthropic's engineering post calls it "the natural progression of prompt engineering"); the film defines it in plain words and names no product. The tools are the module's three, as *A model, reasoning, hands and a loop* named them: Catalog, which lists the tables; Query, which reads the data; and Docs, with the university's definitions; read only, the module's rule.
- **The module's rules.** The agent drafts and a person decides: Jun reads the query, asks the meaning's owner, and the number goes to Planning only after; the agent's tools are read only; the meaning has an owner, who changes it once; the team's context is shared, in the project, reviewed like code, outside a single chat (its memory is *Memory: from one chat to the team*'s).
- **Not shown.** Guido's syllables, *ut, re, mi, fa, sol, la*, which his letter also teaches with the hymn *Ut queant laxis*, and the hexachords; the hand later named after him; his *Micrologus*; why he left Pomposa; the debate on how much of the staff was his; any product for glossaries, catalogs or agents.
