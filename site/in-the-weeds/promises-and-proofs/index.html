Promises and proofs: promise it in a contract, prove it with a test, first

Since 1300, English silver has been tested before it's marked. A core model makes the same kind of promise: every gap between what the business expects and what the sources hold gets a decision, the contracts say what's promised, and the tests prove it, written before the code they check.
