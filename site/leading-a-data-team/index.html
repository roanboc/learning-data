Leading a data team

The third series of Leading data people. Helena Novak leads a data team of eight through a busy year: old reports nobody opens, meetings that decide nothing, alerts nobody acts on, and more requests than people. Four ideas help her lead it, one in each film.

One thread: a team's year, from busy to better

In 2027 the data team keeps 38 old reports, spends eight hours a week in meetings and gets about sixty alerts a week. With Helena, the team stops what nobody uses, improves its own work one small change at a time, makes sure nobody is alone or the only one who knows, and agrees a few rules that let the faculties build their own reports.

Less, on purposeBritain, 2010 to 2012: do lessThe government ran hundreds of websites, with the same advice in different words, for around 560 million pounds a year. A new team kept only what met a real need and wrote it again, and GOV.UK replaced the two main sites with far fewer pages. In tests, people finished more tasks, and faster.
