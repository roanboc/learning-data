# Working with people

*The second series of the module Leading data people.*

The skills that help most when someone asks for data have little to do with tools. On Thursday 11 February 2027, Graham Ellis, the new Director of Facilities, asks for "a dashboard of room use, by Easter"; behind it is a decision worth about A$140 million. Three films: *The question behind the request* (ask what it's for, and agree it in writing); *Explaining so people can decide* (lead with the decision, and show the part of the data it turns on, not only the average); and *Saying no, and what instead* (never a bare no: say what you can do, and when). *The new skills* comes before it, and *Leading a data team* follows. The plan for the module is in [`../the-new-skills/proposal.md`](../the-new-skills/proposal.md); this series' own part is in its [proposal](proposal.md).

**Status:** set up on 5 November 2026, on assumptions the author reviews later; the films are being made, each marked draft on the site, and appear in the table below as each one is out. On the site, the series sits under the topic *Leading data people*, after *The new skills*, at `working-with-people/`, once its opening film is out.

## How the films are made

Like the site's other series, each film is generated from code, with the engine, components, fonts and voice of *The Inner Life of Data*, *A Sharper Sketch*'s scenes, *From words to data*'s tools and the agent films' kit, and checked with the governance and agent films' checks (`twice.py`, `steady.py`, `jump.py`, `edges.py`, `legible.py`, `quiet.py` with `held.py`). A film keeps its own pictures and helpers in its own source. The films are about people, so tools appear only where the story's work happens in them, at the level of what people do. Each film's page links what a newcomer can read next.
