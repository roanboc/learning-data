The first series of Data protection and privacy. A nightly job loads the registrar's data on Jun's own token, from a workspace open to the internet. Four films show the ideas behind a platform's locks, with no product named; five build them in Databricks, GitHub and Azure.

One thread: a nightly job, from a borrowed token to its own name

The job that loads the registrar's data every night runs on Jun Park's personal token, made in 2024 with no end date, from one workspace open to the internet. Film by film, Sam Okafor gives it a name of its own and no secret anyone keeps, puts the workspace behind private paths, moves the specialists' great power into admin accounts switched on for a task, lets production change only through review, and watches all of it.
