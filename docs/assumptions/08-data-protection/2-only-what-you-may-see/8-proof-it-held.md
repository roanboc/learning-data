| 1 | Which story opens the film? The module's list left it to this thread. | The vaccine vial monitor, 1979 to 1996 and on. Heat spoils polio vaccine and leaves no sign, so health workers threw away vials they couldn't vouch for, or a child was given vaccine that no longer protected them. In 1979 the World Health Organization asked for a way to see it; PATH, a health non-profit, worked for years with a company to make one: a square printed inside a circle on each vial's label, which darkens with heat, gradually and irreversibly; lighter than the circle, the vaccine is good; as dark or darker, the vial is thrown away. From 1996 on polio vaccine, for a few cents a vial; UNICEF and WHO estimate it saves more than 14 million dollars a year; today WHO requires one on every vaccine bought through UNICEF. Checked against PATH, two WHO documents and the Stanford case (the script's *Sources*). It carries the film's idea: "It doesn't keep the vaccine cold. It shows whether the cold held." No other opening on the site or in the module is about vaccines or a heat indicator. | Chapter 1, and back in chapters 4, 5 and 7; openings.md |
