A platform that watches itself, and a person who decides

On the night of Monday 2 November, the job that sends casual staff's hours to payroll fails: the claims app now sends 1:30 where it sent 1.50. By morning, the platform has traced the cause, said what it touches and proposed a fix. Sam reviews it, Noor approves it, and pay goes out on Thursday.
