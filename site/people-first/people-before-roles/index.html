People before roles: know the people before you plan the work

On her first day as the university's data manager, in February 2027, Helena Novak finds a chart of seven boxes and a list of 46 open requests. She could plan everyone's work by lunch. First she sits down with each of the seven, notices who the others turn to, and says what won't change yet.
