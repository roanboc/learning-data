Files as data and as context: read, keep or write

In October, Finance received 3,860 supplier invoices, most of them PDF files, and typed each one in by hand; in September, one was paid twice. HR keeps every signed contract, and tutors claimed hours on spreadsheets. Each kind of document gets its own answer: read it, keep it, or write it as data.

Go further in the official docs

Every organisation's documents differ, so the film shows the idea once. Start from the docs, then investigate, adapt, implement, iterate.

  • ai_parse_documentDatabricks: reads a document's words, tables and layout into structured data.
  • ai_extractDatabricks: takes out the fields you name, from text or from what ai_parse_document read.
  • What are Unity Catalog volumes?Databricks: a place for files of any kind, governed as tables are.
  • Intelligent document processingDatabricks: the whole path, from files to data that agents, apps and reports use.
  • Open Data Contract StandardBitol, at the Linux Foundation: the open standard the claims app's contract follows.
