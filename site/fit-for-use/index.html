Fit for use

The first series of Data quality. The graduation list says who graduates, at which ceremony, and the name printed on each certificate. It passed every check, and still 41 certificates were printed again. So the team asks what the list is for, and what good enough means for each use.

Valid isn't true

Every name passed the checks, and 88 were still wrong. Only a reference outside the data, such as the graduate, says what's true.

9 min · Pause and think · Fit for use

One thread: the graduation list, and a name on a certificate

In December 2025, 41 degree certificates had to be printed again: names that lost their accents or were cut short, a double degree printed as one award, graduates missing from the list. Over the next six months, the people who use the list, answer for it and build it write down what it's for and what each use needs, look at a hundred of its records, check its names against the graduates themselves, and agree how fresh it must be.
