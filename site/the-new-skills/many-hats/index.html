Many hats, one picture: go deep in one thing, and see the whole

Ivy Reyes joins the university's data team in March 2027, with a degree in statistics. In her first month she spends a few days with each part of the team's work, and follows one real question through all of it. On one page, from Graham's decision back to the sensors, she sees what no one part could: 14 rooms named two ways.
