The same one, everywhere

A series on master and reference data. Linh graduated from one university, and now studies, tutors and does research there. She is five records in four systems, and nobody sees they are one person. Film by film, the university learns to keep the person apart from her roles, decide once who is who and which value wins, date the roles, own the lists that sort people, use each role's data only for its purpose, share one person across its systems, and keep it right.

One thread: one person, Linh

Linh graduated from the university in 2022 and came back to study health informatics, under a new student number. She tutors a first-year unit, and this year she joined a research project. Each system knows one part of her, by its own number. Film by film, the university gives her one record that every system can read: roles that start and end, each value labelled with where it came from, matches decided once by the people who look after them, the lists that sort people owned and dated, and each role's data used only for its purpose.
