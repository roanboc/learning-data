The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1Prototype first7½ min · Graham asks for a dashboard. The team puts something real in front of him in two weeks, and three short rounds settle a question worth A$140 million.
  2. 2Measures that matter8 min · Helena puts eight measures of what people get on one page, each with a baseline, a definition and the decision it serves.
  3. 3Better every week6½ min · Helena keeps half an hour every Friday for how the team works: one measure, one small change, kept or dropped out loud.
  4. 4Strategy is a habit8½ min · Helena reviews the data strategy every quarter, moves it out loud when the university moves, and ends the year with promised beside delivered.
