# More than a platform

The film was made from the first draft of its script, which the author hadn't answered yet: he asked on 12 November 2026 to continue and be told when it was done. These are the questions it would have asked, and what it assumed.

| Question | Assumed |
|---|---|
| Is the meeting's problem right: two counts of one class, 176 and 149? | Yes. Data Science 101 has 180 seats; the student system counts 176 (signed up), the learning platform 171 (can open the course) and Finance 149 (has paid). |
| Three systems or four? | Three. The proposal had HR and payroll too, but it doesn't count enrolments. |
| May the class's 312% from *The Inner Life of Data* come back? | No. There a wrong sketch causes it; here nobody made a mistake. |
| Is 65 seconds too long for the route? | Kept: each of the eleven stations is named with its question, as the author asked ("at least the stations"). |
| Does the film show people? | No. It is shapes and words: sheets on a table stand for the two offices. |
| A title card? | Yes, after the opening's question, and an end card with the closing line. |
| Which map does the film name? | DAMA-DMBOK's wheel, once, as the best known; two other maps are outlines with no name. The wheel's centre goes to *Data governance*; its ten parts are not matched to the other stations by name. |
| What does the route look like? | The Topics page's route, in its dark colours: a blue line, an amber loop for sharing and protecting, a violet loop for agents. The amber is close to the gold the films use for what people agree on; the film shows one gold card, *enrolled: one definition*, before the amber loop is drawn. |
| Which goal lights its stops? | *Build a report people trust*: the introduction, data modelling, analytics engineering and data quality, numbered 1 to 4. |
| The Spanish narration | es-MX-DaliaNeural, each line starting on its English cue. A line may run up to 0.35 s into the pause after it, so short lines aren't rushed; nine captions were shortened to fit. |
| Where does the film live on the site? | On a page of its own, `/more-than-a-platform/`, with a card on Start here and under *Data platforms* on the Topics page. It has no series page: it is the only film that stands alone. |
| The sound | Chime bars, made for this film, as a station's announcement plays them; checked against the other films' scores by name. |
