| 1 | Which classes does a column get? The module's setup wrote "direct identifier, indirect identifier, special category, none (*Who may see it*)", but *Who may see it* and the example project say `direct`, `pseudonymous`, `none`. | Keep *Who may see it*'s three, word for word, and add two: `indirect` (names no one alone, but with other columns can single out one person: date of birth, home postcode) and `special` (could harm a person if it got out: health, disability, faith). One class per column, the strictest that applies (special, direct, pseudonymous, indirect, none). The film says openly that it sharpens *Who may see it*. Settled with *Tags that travel*'s thread, which writes them as the project's governed tag `personal_data`; in numbers.md. | The whole film; numbers.md; films 12 to 17 |
| 2 | What is a table's level, and how does it relate to its columns? | Four levels, set by the table's owner by the harm if the whole table got out: public, internal, confidential, restricted (a second tag, `level`).
