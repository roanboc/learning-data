The new skills

A series of Leading data people. In 2027 Helena Novak leads the university's data team, Ivy Reyes joins it straight from her degree, and Graham Ellis, the new Director of Facilities, asks for a dashboard of room use. What does a leader need their people to be good at now, and how do they grow it?

The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

Film 1 of 3 · Seeing across the team

Many hats, one picture

Everyone in Helena's team wears several hats. A new analyst, given time with every part, finds 14 rooms that fell between them.

9½ min · Pause and think · The new skills

Film 2 of 3 · Working with an agent

The draft and the decision

An agent drafts Ivy's model in a minute, tidy and sure, and wrong about one word. She frames the work, checks it and decides.

9 min · Pause and think · The new skills

Film 3 of 3 · Learning on the job

Learning while doing

Ivy learns the team's work beside Jun, a small change a day, teaches what she learned, and by June every job has two names.

8½ min · Pause and think · The new skills
