# More than a platform

**Video script, as built, 14 November 2026.** It reshapes the whole film from the author's brief of that day, so that it introduces the portal itself, directly: why it exists, the route of stations, the example project, what it adds beyond DAMA, and what a visitor does at every stop. The author revised the draft four times that day and asked to build it. The film made on 12 November, with its meeting of two numbers, is replaced.

6 minutes 2 seconds, with 790 words of narration (131 words a minute), in eight chapters, in the voice the films have used so far. English narration; Spanish captions, a Spanish page and a Spanish narration over the same pictures. Every project, number and screen is drawn for the film.

It comes after *The Inner Life of Data*, on a page of its own. It has no story from the past, no labs, no scenarios and no Pause and think of its own.

## The brief, in the author's words

> This video should start introducing the learn all data portal, stating that the introduction video shows the basics of a data platform but there is much more beyond it to make data useful to the organization. Then it says this portal was created to bring all the topics of data in one place, because many courses and documentation are either too technical without the business and governance context, or too agnostic without practical and technical advice. [...] This approach also goes a bit beyond DAMA as we cover also enterprise architecture, AI agents in data work, leadership, and other frameworks [...] And that's why this covers an emerging concept defined as enterprise data architecture.

His revisions that day: say that he has worked in applications, data and enterprise architecture roles for more than sixteen years; say that enterprise architecture is rarely taught in context, and why governance became slow work; take extra time on the foundations; show the example project's folders, the business and governance metadata most projects leave out; show the labs and evaluation in action; call the portal *Learning Data*; call the side paths *branch lines*; name dbt and Databricks as the tools that show the ideas, and say that any tools with the same capabilities would do, "as there are many tools wars". He chose to say "many conversations about tools, far fewer about the foundations" rather than claim what data professionals lack, which no data supports, and that the main line "goes deeper into each layer the introduction showed".

### 2 · Why this portal · 0:30–1:34

**Narration.** Many courses and much of the documentation give you only half of that. Some are deeply technical, with no business and no governance around the code. Others stay neutral, and never say what to do, or in which tool. So you join the halves yourself, at work, under pressure. That's how a model passes every test and answers the wrong question, or a policy never reaches the code. The author of this portal has worked in applications, data and enterprise architecture roles for more than sixteen years. The pieces came together slowly, one project at a time. Nobody who works with data should need sixteen years to join them. So the key lessons are gathered here, in one place. This is Learning Data: the context and the practice, from how the organisation works down to the code. Joining the two is what this portal calls enterprise data architecture.

### 3 · The foundations · 1:34–2:44

**Narration.** The portal is laid out as a route of stations, like a railway line, in an order that makes sense. It starts with the foundations. There are many conversations about tools, and far fewer about the foundations under them. Enterprise architecture comes first. It describes how a business works: what it does, what it needs, which systems serve it, and how they connect. In the author's experience, it's rarely taught in context. So people struggle to describe a business, and to understand the one they work in. Then data governance: who decides what the data means, and who may use it. For years it was often seen as slow work that few wanted to do. Often the enterprise foundations under it were weak. Or the technology wasn't ready to deliver governance as a service, built into the daily work. So it stayed a set of policies. This portal shows the newer way: governance built into the work, from the glossary to the code.

### 6 · Beyond DAMA · 4:11–4:53

**Narration.** This ground has been mapped before. DAMA's wheel is the best known, and this portal covers it. It also goes further, into enterprise architecture, AI agents in data work and leadership. It adds other frameworks that proved their worth over those years. Data has many paradigms. Many were made by data people, for data, and look little at the enterprise around them. That is why this portal starts from the enterprise. Enterprise data architecture means exactly that: data designed as part of the organisation it serves.

## Sound

Chime bars, made for this film (`source/tools/score.py`): single bars struck with a yarn mallet, as a station's announcement plays them before it names the next stop. G major for the route and what works, E minor for the half that courses leave out, C major for the foundations and for enterprise data architecture, tuned pure from G. Music under the opening, then only now and then: at a chapter's turn, where something lands on screen, or in a pause. The mark, four notes, plays under the title card, when the last station is named and under the end card. A station arriving is one soft tap. `tools/quiet.py` passes: after the opening the music comes in 24 times, once every 13.8 seconds; 61 effects, at most three in one second.
