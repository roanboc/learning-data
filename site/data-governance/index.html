Governance principles

The first series on data governance. Yusuf Demir starts as the university's data governance lead, with a shelf of thirty-four policies, and the same week two reports disagree on one number. Film by film, he finds what's worth keeping, and who decides and acts.

Prologue · Where governance comes from

The same problem, everywhere

In 2008, a bank couldn't add up its risks. People had met over the same problem since 1980. Why is governance still at the centre?

Film 1 of 10 · Why data governance

Off the shelf

Thirty-four policies on a shelf, and two numbers that disagree. Ask every policy one question: where does it run?

Film 2 of 10 · Decision rights

Who decides

Eight areas, three owners still to be confirmed, and decisions lost in email. Make the work ask its owner, by name.

Film 3 of 10 · One meaning for each word

One word, one home

Five glossaries, three meanings of one word. Keep one that everyone trusts, and sync every tool from it.

Film 4 of 10 · Who may see what

Who may see it

A planner needs one column, and the old path gives her the whole table. Classify each column once, and protect it everywhere.

6 min · Pause and think · Governance principles

Film 5 of 10 · Data quality rules

Rules that run

Monitored means a yearly survey. Give every check an owner, a level and a reason, and read the scorecard from what ran.

6 min · Pause and think · Governance principles

Film 6 of 10 · Fixing data at the source

Fixed where it starts

One cell stays amber because emails are cleaned downstream. Follow the problem back to where it starts, fix it there, and keep the fix as a check.

6 min · Pause and think · Governance principles

Film 7 of 10 · Change control

Change without surprises

A monthly board answers from memory. Keep a record of who uses what, and give every change an impact note the owners fill in.

6½ min · Pause and think · Governance principles

Film 8 of 10 · Data products

A product with an owner

A count that came late, and nobody who answered for it. Write down what the data promises, and name an owner who keeps it.

6 min · Pause and think · Governance principles

Film 9 of 10 · AI for governance

An agent that checks

Every change waits for the one person who knows the rules. Write them down so an agent can check them, and let people decide.

6½ min · Pause and think · Governance principles

Film 10 of 10 · Getting people to take part

Teachers of data

A check stops an analyst, and its page says why and how to fix it. Teach first, so a check stops only the few.
