From words to data

Seven short films on data modelling, from how minds turn the world into words, to how AI can help keep meaning true. They follow one idea, older than any system: the credential, how people prove what they know.

The films

Watch them in order: each film stands alone, and each builds on the ones before it. They make most sense after The Inner Life of Data and A Sharper Sketch.

One thread: the credential

People have always needed to show what they know to someone who wasn't there to see them learn it. The answer has barely changed in centuries: someone trusted makes a claim about someone, in a way that others can check. The university's new microcredentials are its newest form, and each film takes them one step further.
