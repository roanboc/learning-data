What an agent is

The first series on AI agents in data work. Jun builds the university's data project, and this term an agent works beside him. Before any rule or product, ten films ask how today's models came about, what they took from the brain, what an agent is, what agents are like to work with, why that means governing them, and what they tell us about ourselves.

One thread: an agent on Jun's team

Jun builds the university's data project, and this term an agent works beside him: it reads the catalogue, writes queries and checks its own work. Film by film, the team learns what it is and isn't, where it helps and where it misleads, and why it needs an owner, limits and a person's review before the next series puts it to work on data.
