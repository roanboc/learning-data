# Model the business: the series' own questions and assumptions

*Data modelling* · *Model the business*, both films

[Watch the series on the site](https://data.learn-all.org/model-the-business/)

> Kept by the series' thread, 19 November 2026.

Rodrigo asked on 19 November 2026 for a film before *A Sharper Sketch*, made a series with it, that gives the university the enterprise architecture and data governance a good data model stands on, as a follow-up to the intro, and for *A Sharper Sketch* to be adjusted to it. He agreed that this makes issue #405 (a university version of the EA module) unnecessary. The thread built it without waiting for answers, as he asked for every thread on 19 November 2026. Each line: the question I would have asked, what I did instead, where it shows, and what changes if it's wrong. The series' *Decided* table has the same choices (`films/model-the-business/decided.md`).

| 1 | Which framework is the reference for the university's map? | HERM (CAUDIT's Higher Education Reference Models, v3.1.1) for what a university does, since the sector shares it; the site's own enterprise architecture method (*The map before the data*) for the layers; DAMA-DMBOK for owners, stewards, the glossary and systems of record. TOGAF, Zachman, BIZBOK and domain-driven design are named in the proposal, not in the film. | The series' proposal; *The map behind the sketch*, chapters *What it does* to *What the words mean* | A line or two of narration, and the proposal |
| 2 | What does practice add: what goes wrong when a modeller has no map? | From the site's films and the example project: a model built from one system's tables inherits that system's meaning; a box with no owner becomes a number nobody can fix; a sketch always finds words the glossary hasn't decided, and the modeller asks the owner rather than deciding them. | The proposal's brief; the film's last chapters | The brief, and the lines that say it |
