Workloads without secrets: a job that proves who it is

Every night at two, a job loads the registrar's records, and it still runs on Jun Park's personal token, with copies in its settings, in a notebook in GitHub and on his laptop. How can it prove who it is each time it runs, with nothing anyone could copy?
