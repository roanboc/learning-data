# Continuous architecture

*The series' plan is the plan of* AI and continuous architecture, *whose second half it was until 19 November 2026: [`../ai-and-continuous-architecture/proposal.md`](../ai-and-continuous-architecture/proposal.md), with its reference (ArchiMate 4, Erder and Pureur's* Continuous Architecture *named once as the term's source), its insights, its practice and its one true story, the ziggurat of Babylon. That plan's films 4 to 6 are this series' films 1 to 3. What the architecture series share is in [`../business-architecture-for-data/proposal.md`](../business-architecture-for-data/proposal.md).*

The series keeps an architecture alive as a capability, not a project: a first draft from industry reference models and the organisation's own documents, confirmed by the owners; every change tested and walked through the map before anyone builds; and keeping the map someone's job, with an owner, a budget and a rhythm. An agent helps the architects draft and check, and never confirms; how agents themselves are drawn on the map is *AI architecture*'s, which follows it.
