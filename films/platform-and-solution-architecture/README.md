# Platform and solution architecture

*The fourth series of the topic Enterprise architecture, after* The map before the data.

The data platform as a platform, not an application: its parts, one platform or many, where it meets the applications, security all the way through, and one solution designed on it. The series' plan is in its [proposal](proposal.md); what the four new architecture series share is in [`../business-architecture-for-data/proposal.md`](../business-architecture-for-data/proposal.md). *Data architecture* comes before it, *AI and continuous architecture* after.

**Status:** set up on 11 November 2026 from the proposal the author confirmed that day; the films are being made, one at a time, and appear in the table below as each one is out. On the site, the series sits under the topic *Enterprise architecture*, at `platform-and-solution-architecture/`, once its opening film is out. The films carry no draft label: the author discussed them in detail before any was made.

## The look and the sound

- **The look of *The map before the data*:** paper drafts on a wall, the confirmed model in glass, the six layers in their colours, from [`../enterprise-architecture/shared/`](../enterprise-architecture/shared/), with the same people and the same energy utility.
- **One true story across the series**, told in the series' own drawing style, each film opening on its part of it.
- **Its own sound in each film.** Music under the opening story, then only now and then; nothing loops, trembles or pulses, except an urgent pulse where a story turns to an error or a rush.

## How the films are made

Like *The map before the data*, each film is generated from code, with the engine, components, fonts and voice of *The Inner Life of Data*, *From words to data*'s tools and that series' own components. A film keeps its own pictures and helpers in its own source.
