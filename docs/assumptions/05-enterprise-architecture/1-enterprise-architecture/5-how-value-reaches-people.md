## Its process chapters, now one that points on (23 November 2026)

The film was made with the author, so it had no list here. When the portal was consolidated, Rodrigo asked to "prioritise the stories per architecture layer/domain, so let's rebuild the films that need to be rebuilt", and to "keep refining the portal to reduce content redundancy". Its three chapters on processes, *Four kinds of process*, *Levels, again* and *The edges*, taught what *Business architecture for data*'s film 4, *Processes, as deep as data needs*, teaches in full: the process map's four groups, its levels and each process's edges, in a SIPOC. They are now one chapter, *One process, seven steps*, which shows the map, names where it is drawn in full and keeps what this film needs: one process, its seven steps, and the one step that earns its detail. Each line: the question I would have asked, what I did instead, and where it shows.
