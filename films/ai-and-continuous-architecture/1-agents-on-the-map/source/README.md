# Rebuilding Agents on the map

*Agents on the map*, film 1 of 6 of *AI and continuous architecture*, is generated from code like *The map before the data*'s films: the same engine, components, voice and tools (run through [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py)), and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/aca.js`](../../shared/src/aca.js): its mark and cards (`acaMark`, `acaTitle`, `acaEnd`), Babylon (`bab_sky`, `bab_city`, `bab_palm`, `bab_ground`, `bab_tower`, `bab_mound`, `bab_folk`, `bab_basket`, `bab_brick`, `bab_kiln`, `bab_boat`, `bab_rope`, `bab_rod`, `bab_clay`, `bab_quote`, `bab_tag`), the agent (`aca_agent`) and the four steps of how far it may go (`ACA_STEPS`, `aca_steps`, `aca_stepPill`). A change there is a new version of every film of the series. Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás, ArchiMate, Etemenanki, Nabopolassar, Nebuchadnezzar, Marduk, Babel, NIST's, IT), and the moment its poster shows (the four tasks over the ruin, at 1:00). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md); `say` gives the voice numbers in words where the caption keeps digits (620 BCE, 605 BCE, 212 plans). |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: the tower standing again at dawn, then the title; the builders of many lands at work on one plan; and the map with every agent on it, then the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `a1_`): a person seen from the left, and one picture faded as a whole (`a1_personR`, `a1_fade`); a household and ArchiMate's actor, small (`a1_house`, `a1_actor`); the four tasks of the tower's building, each a clay card (`A1_TASKS`, `a1_taskCard`); the map's elements, an assignment to a role, a window, a chat bubble, a sheet of paper, a setting's switch, a dashed group, an owner's tick, a person and a card (`a1_el`, `a1_assign`, `a1_win`, `a1_bubble`, `a1_paper`, `a1_switch`, `a1_group`, `a1_owner`, `a1_who`, `a1_card`); the map with the elements this film names, its two agents in roles of their own and, at the end, every agent with its owner (`A1_APPS`, `A1_ALL`, `a1_map`); and the process of helping a household that can't pay, with its five tasks (`A1_TASKS5`, `a1_proc`). |
| `src/scenes.js` | The eleven chapters, and what they share: the tower's builders and their tasks (`A1_BY`, `A1_GRID`, `a1_hands`), Tomás and Farah (`a1_tomas`, `a1_farah`), the note (`A1_NOTE`), the IT service management tool's list, where agents leave traces and what Tomás finds (`A1_ITSM`, `A1_TRACE`, `A1_FOUND`, `a1_found`), the tasks in the order the narration takes them (`A1_FAR`, `a1_key`), who answers (`A1_WHO`), the register on the map (`A1_REG`, `A1_COLS`), the Tower of Babel's page and its builders' speech marks, no real script imitated (`a1_babelPage`, `a1_speech`, `a1_glyph`), and where one chapter glides on into the next (`CHAPTER_JOINS`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line, and the words they use. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds, in the series' palette: for Babylon, low bowed strings and a soft horn in D minor, coming to D major as the tower stands again, with clay, brick, basket, rope, fire and water, and nothing borrowed from the region's music; for the present, a harp in F major, with a celesta where an agent acts; the series' mark (a fifth, a fourth, a third, rising) at the title and the end. Each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`, from the same word of the narration. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs *The map before the data*'s tool on this film; `tools/quiet.py`, after `tools/audio.py`, runs the governance films' check of the sound. |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/ai-and-continuous-architecture/1-agents-on-the-map/source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `python tools/build.py`, `python tools/audio.py`, `python tools/build.py` again (it bundles the soundtrack), then the checks: `python tools/check.py`, `python tools/legible.py`, `python tools/pace.py` and `python tools/quiet.py`. The video is `dist/agents-on-the-map.mp4`.

## Publish

`python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/agents-on-the-map/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/agents-on-the-map/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `role`, `far` and `answers`. Progress is stored under `ld-agents-on-the-map`.
