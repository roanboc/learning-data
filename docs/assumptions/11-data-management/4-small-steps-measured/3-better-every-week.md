## For Rodrigo's checklist: the audio release

The soundtrack ships as the films merged on 10 November ship theirs: the MP3 beside the player in `site/assets/better-every-week/`, committed with `git add -f`, and `audio.json` recording its checksum and the audio release that will hold it (`python site-tools/media.py index --film better-every-week-en`).
