Deleted means deleted: every copy, on its date

Linh asked to come off the tutors' mailing list. One night's job deleted her row, but the table kept old versions, a send log kept her number, the mailing tool had its own list, and locked copies held her for a month. Where does each copy live, and when does the last one go?

Read the guidance yourself

The platforms' own guides say it in their words: delete where the data starts, then clean up every old file, archive, trace and copy.

  • Prepare your data for GDPR compliance, Azure DatabricksDelete at the start, from a scheduled job driven by a table of requests, then purge and VACUUM.
  • Deletion vectors, and VACUUM, Azure DatabricksWhy a delete may only mark a row, and how old files are removed once their seven days are up.
  • Access tiers for blob data, Azure StorageHot, cool, cold and archive: why a file in the archive tier must be brought back before it can be read.
  • Redacting sensitive data from traces, MLflowMask personal data in an agent's trace before it's saved, so it never leaves the application.
  • Removing sensitive data from a repository, GitHubWhy what's written in a repository is hard to take back: every clone keeps its own copy.
