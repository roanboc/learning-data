How long, and then what: an end date for every record

Until October 2026, the university's platform kept every record with no end date: pay records back to 2016, applications from people never hired back to 2017. Ingrid and Mei write one schedule, by kind of record. When Linh's last contract ends, how long is each record kept, and then what?

Read the guidance yourself

Records managers and privacy laws say it in their own words: decide how long, by kind of record, before it's needed.

  • Scheduling records, US National ArchivesHow a records schedule is written and approved today, in the archive whose schedules began in 1943.
  • General Records SchedulesOne schedule for the records every office keeps, such as pay and personnel.
  • ISO 15489-1:2016, Records managementThe international standard: how long each class of record is kept, and what then, decided by rules.
  • GDPR, Article 5: storage limitationEurope's data protection law: personal data kept no longer than its purpose needs.
