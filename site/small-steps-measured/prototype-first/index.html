Prototype first: something real in front of the person who decides

Graham Ellis asks for a dashboard of room use by Easter. Behind it is whether to ask for a new building, and requests wait 41 working days. Helena's team puts something real in front of him within two weeks, with governed data, and improves it a round at a time until his decision is served.
