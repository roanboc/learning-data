## The brief

The author named the key of the job as design, and an "agile focus (in the sense of prototype and iterate fast)", and asked for the standard elements of "defining strategy, implementing it, measure it and iterate". This series is that loop. *Prototype first* puts something real in front of the person who decides within two weeks; *Measures that matter* measures what people get, not how busy the team is; *Better every week* improves the team's own way of working, one small change at a time; and *Strategy is a habit* reviews the strategy every quarter and moves it out loud when the university's own strategy moves. The module ends where it began: discover, define, plan; deliver, measure, improve; and again.

The series follows March 2027 to January 2028 (the module's shared numbers): Facilities' rooms, taken up the new way on Tuesday 30 March and first used on Wednesday 14 April, eleven working days later; rooms booked 38% of the week but 93% at the peak, and one booked hour in five with nobody there; the measures page from July; the Friday half-hour from 3 September, 14 changes kept by 17 December; reviews on 27 July 2027, 26 October 2027 and 25 January 2028; and the first year's review on Monday 31 January 2028.
