# Rebuilding A first draft, not a blank page

*A first draft, not a blank page*, film 4 of 6 of *AI and continuous architecture*, is generated from code like *The map before the data*'s films: the same engine, components, voice and tools (run through [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py)), and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/aca.js`](../../shared/src/aca.js): its mark and cards (`acaMark`, `acaTitle`, `acaEnd`), Babylon's sky, city, ground, tower, folk, ropes, bricks, tags and quotes (`bab_*`), and the agent and its step on a task (`aca_agent`, `aca_stepPill`). A change there is a new version of every film of the series. Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás, Nabopolassar, Etemenanki, Ur, socle, CIM, IEC), and the moment its poster shows (the king and his diviner at the old foundation at dawn, its line lit gold, the first brick with his stamp set on it, and his words "on its original socle" on clay, at 1:11). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md); `say` gives the voice numbers in words where the caption keeps digits (2,600 years, 2,400 homes, 41, 9, 8 and 4 elements). |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: the new tower rising on its old foundation, then the title; the tower and the confirmed design side by side, each on what it was built from; and the design, confirmed, then the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `a4_`): the sides' colours (`A4_NET`, `A4_RET`, households, red, green and the sun's) and the layers' by name (`A4_STR`, `A4_BUS`, `A4_INF`, `A4_APP`), and each side's paper and glass (`A4_PAPER`, `A4_EDGE`); a face that changes over a moment, a person seen from the left, one picture faded as a whole, Babylon's people and the utility's (`a4_expr`, `a4_personR`, `a4_fade`, `a4_folk`, `a4_who`); the tower's form outlined, its foundation's line, the form on clay and the land between the rivers with Ur (`a4_outline`, `a4_footLine`, `a4_formInk`, `a4_formCard`, `a4_map`); cards, reference models, documents, a line of light, a letter and a speech bubble (`a4_card`, `a4_ref`, `a4_doc`, `a4_flow`, `a4_letter`, `a4_bubble`); the street at noon, its homes, battery, sun and moon, and its voltage (`a4_home`, `a4_battery`, `a4_sun`, `a4_moon`, `a4_gauge`, `A4_HX`, `a4_street`); a design's four parts, a car, a clock, time in blocks and a page (`a4_part`, `a4_car`, `a4_clock`, `a4_blocks`, `a4_page`); the wall and the 18 notes on it, each with its kind, side, words, source and what becomes of it, the owners and their dates, and how a note is drawn, from paper to glass (`A4_COL`, `A4_NOTES`, `A4_N`, `A4_OWN`, `A4_LATE`, `a4_box`, `a4_foot`, `a4_note`, `a4_wallBack`, `a4_wall`, `a4_mid`); the draft's count and the review's tally (`A4_COUNT`, `a4_count`, `A4_FATE`, `a4_tally`); a lock, a crew and the line switched off before crews work (`a4_lockIcon`, `a4_crew`, `a4_isolate`); and the confirmed design, small (`a4_mini`). |
| `src/scenes.js` | The eight chapters, and what they share: the tower's foot and scale (`A4_BY`, `A4_S`); the present at the opening's end (`a4_now`); a person's name (`a4_name`); the draft's notes in their order, each note's source as it's shown, the agent's corner of the wall and a highlight that comes and goes (`A4_DRAFT`, `a4_sourced`, `a4_corner`, `a4_win`); how far the camera tilts down for the review (`A4_LIFT`); and where one chapter glides on into the next (`CHAPTER_JOINS`: the wall holds four chapters). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line, and the words they use. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds, in its own palette: a fortepiano, its chords spread from the bass up, and hummed voices, which sound only where something is confirmed; A flat major, F minor where things go wrong, tuned pure from A flat (the score stops if two notes that aren't pure together would sound); the film's figure (a chord with one note left hanging, then the voices and a step down to rest) once in Babylon and once near the end, and the series' mark at the title and the end; baskets, a rope, clay and a stamped brick, then notes pinned up, sources clipped, glass, a pen, a stroke through, a lock, paper and keys. Each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`, from the same word of the narration. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs *The map before the data*'s tool on this film; `tools/quiet.py`, after `tools/audio.py`, runs the governance films' check of the sound. |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/ai-and-continuous-architecture/4-a-first-draft-not-a-blank-page/source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `python tools/build.py`, `python tools/audio.py`, `python tools/build.py` again (it bundles the soundtrack), then the checks: `python tools/check.py`, `python tools/legible.py`, `python tools/pace.py` and `python tools/quiet.py`. The video is `dist/a-first-draft-not-a-blank-page.mp4`.

## Publish

`python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/a-first-draft-not-a-blank-page/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/a-first-draft-not-a-blank-page/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `draft`, `review` and `confirm`. Progress is stored under `ld-a-first-draft-not-a-blank-page`.
