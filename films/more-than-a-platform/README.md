# More than a platform

*The film that follows The Inner Life of Data, on a page of its own.*

A platform that works can still feed a meeting two numbers for one class: one word, *enrolled*, means three things in three systems. The film says what changes the answer (how the organisation works, what was defined, and who decided), names the topics of the learning route, each with the question it answers, and shows what a visitor can do at every stop. It has no story from the past, no labs, no scenarios and no Pause and think (the author, 12 November 2026).

**Status:** the proposal was confirmed on 12 November 2026; the film is shared with the author for review.

## The look and the sound

- **The intro's platform map**, as the films of *Modern data basics* draw it ([`../data-that-moves/shared/`](../data-that-moves/shared/README.md)): dark navy, glass cards, words in white. The learning route is drawn as the Topics page draws it: one blue line, an amber loop for sharing and protecting, a violet loop for agents. The film has no people: it is shapes and words.
- **Chime bars**, made for this film: single bars struck with a yarn mallet, as a station's announcement plays them before it names the next stop. Music under the opening, then only now and then; a station arriving is one soft tap.
- **Spanish**: captions, the page, and a Spanish narration (`tools/audio_es.py`, es-MX-DaliaNeural) over the same pictures.
