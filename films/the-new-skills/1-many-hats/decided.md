| 5 November 2026 | *Many hats, one picture* opens in Kaifeng, in the four beats: in 1015 a fire breaks out in the emperor's palace one night and spreads, and Ding Wei, a senior minister who had run the state's finances and the building of a palace, is put in charge of the repairs; rebuilding means three jobs, each slow and costly (earth dug far away and carried in, timber arriving by water hauled through the streets, the burned rubble carted away), carts crossing a crowded city load after load, slow for the palace, costly for the state and in everyone's way; his plan makes the three one (the main streets dug for earth become trenches, the Bian canal's bank is broken and its water let in, rafts and boats float the materials to the palace gate, and the rubble fills the trenches when the work is done); "one move, and three jobs done", in Shen Kuo's words some seventy years later, with a saving "in his words" of hundreds of millions, and the city's streets back.
