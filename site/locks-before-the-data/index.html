The films

Watch them in order: the ideas first, then the tools.

  1. 1Who is it, really?8½ min · Why every request to a platform proves who it is, and why its rights come from a role, never a person.
  2. 2No secret to lose9 min · The ways a job or a person signs in, ranked from worst to best by who holds a secret, and for how long.
  3. 3Walls, doors and keys8½ min · Who secures what, walls from the map's areas, private paths, rooms apart, keys the university holds and copies nobody can delete.
  4. 4Power you switch on9 min · A second account for each specialist, its power off until it's switched on for a task, with a reason, a yes and a record.
  5. 5People and groups8 min · People and groups from one directory, a passkey for every sign-in, and admin power switched on only for a task.
  6. 6Workloads without secrets9 min · A job that signs in as itself: a name of its own, a signed note for each deploy, and one password in a vault.
  7. 7Network, storage and keys9½ min · Private doors, a short list out, one identity at the files, and one way around the catalog that you switch on and watch.
  8. 8From a branch to production9 min · Rules set before the change, a reviewer who didn't write it, and a machine that deploys only after a second yes.
  9. 9Who did what9 min · A record nobody in it can change, an alarm for admin power, a stolen session stopped, and a copy nobody can delete.
