# In the weeds of data crafting: the series' own questions and assumptions

*Analytics engineering* · *In the weeds of data crafting*, films 1 to 5 and 7 to 10

[Watch the series on the site](https://data.learn-all.org/in-the-weeds/)

> Kept by the thread "Check that text stays inside its box", 12 November 2026.

The series was made with the author from 29 September 2026, so it had no list here. On 12 November 2026 he saw words run past a card on a phone in another series, and asked: "Can we check this via code to fix those glitches?" The check (`site-tools/fits.py`) found 62 pieces of text past their boxes in nine films of this series. They were fixed while he was away. Each line: the question I would have asked, what I did instead, and where it shows. The series' *Decided* table has the same change in one row.
