Kept, not cleaned: master data looked after every day, with a check at entry

Dujiangyan has watered the Chengdu plain for more than two thousand years, because its inner channel is dug out every dry season, down to a mark in its bed. In 2024, a clean-up project at the university merged two thousand duplicate records of people. A year later there were eleven hundred new ones, because nothing had changed how records were made. Now the application searches before it creates a person, and new duplicates stay under fifty an intake. Mei's office keeps five measures, each with a limit, and an alert points at a case, not at a person to blame. An agent proposes a change to a list, and the list's owner decides. Clean it once, and it decays. Keep it, and it lasts. To close the series, its nine films come together, as one part of data governance.
