# A plan in ninety days

*The opening series of the module Data management.*

A data manager's first ninety days. Helena Novak starts as the university's data manager with 46 open requests that touch every area of data. Four films: *Every area, one job* (see every area as one job, and plan ninety days in three lanes: people, strategy, operations); *Listen first* (ask people the same few questions, count from the systems, and write down what is); *Design it simple* (start from the decision, and design the fewest parts that serve it); and *From gaps to a roadmap* (turn the gaps into stages that each work on their own, on a one-page data strategy). *Making the case* and *Small steps, measured* follow. This folder also holds the plan for the whole module, in the [proposal](proposal.md).

**Status:** set up on 5 November 2026, on assumptions the author reviews later; the films are being made, one at a time, each marked draft on the site, and appear in the table below as each one is out. On the site, the series opens the topic *Data management*, the last topic, at `a-plan-in-ninety-days/`, once its opening film is out.

- **One look with the leading data people module**, assumed on 5 November 2026 for the author to review (the module's *Decided* table, in [proposal.md](proposal.md)). That module's kit, `films/the-new-skills/shared/`, draws both modules' films; every film here bundles it as it is.

## How the films are made

Like the site's other series, each film is generated from code, with the engine, components, fonts and voice of *The Inner Life of Data*, *A Sharper Sketch*'s scenes, *From words to data*'s tools and the agent films' kit, and checked with the governance and agent films' checks (`twice.py`, `steady.py`, `jump.py`, `edges.py`, `legible.py`, `quiet.py` with `held.py`). A film keeps its own pictures and helpers in its own source. Where a film shows data work, the example project, [`../analytics-engineering/project/`](../analytics-engineering/project/), is where it happens. Each film's page links the guides and standards it points to.
