# Rebuilding Rules with a home

*Rules with a home*, film 6 of 7 of *Business architecture for data*, is generated from code like *The map before the data*: the same engine, components, voice and tools (`films/enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/`, which run From words to data's), with the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/ba.js`](../../shared/src/ba.js). It was film 9 of *The map before the data* until 23 November 2026, and its present-day chapters use that series' components as they are. Its own pictures live with its scenes, among them a rule as the series draws one from here on (`d9_rule`): dark glass with motivation's purple edge and ArchiMate's requirement glyph, with its kind, data rule or business rule, as its kicker (`d9_kind`). ## Rebuild

As for [*Processes, as deep as data needs*](../../4-processes-as-deep-as-data-needs/source/README.md#rebuild), from this folder, `films/business-architecture-for-data/6-rules-with-a-home/source/`; the video is `dist/rules-with-a-home.mp4`.

## Publish

`python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/rules-with-a-home/` and draws the poster; then `python site-tools/tiles.py` cuts its tile and `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/rules-with-a-home/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `split`, `expect`, `std` and `onto`, and the first and third open the series' lab *Give the rule a home*. Progress is stored under `ld-rules-with-a-home`. The soundtrack's pointer is `../audio.json` (`python site-tools/media.py index --film rules-with-a-home-en`).
