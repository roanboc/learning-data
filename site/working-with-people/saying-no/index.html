Go further

The film names each idea once. These pages go further, and are free to read. Checked on 10 November 2026.

  • Getting to Yes, by Roger Fisher and William UryThe book's method in brief: separate the people from the problem, focus on interests rather than positions, invent options for mutual gain, and insist on criteria both sides can check.
  • The Power of a Positive No, by William UryThe author's page for the book: a yes to what you must protect, a clear and respectful no, and then a proposal the other person can say yes to.
  • Saving the northern Aral Sea, World Bank, 2008The World Bank's account of the Kok-Aral dam and the works upstream, with the water's level, its salt, the fish and the towns in numbers.
  • World of Change: Shrinking Aral Sea, NASASatellite pictures of the sea year by year since 2000: the southern sea drying, and the northern sea held behind its dam.
  • Aral SeaThe sea's history, from the rivers turned to irrigation to the Kok-Aral dam, and what happened to the towns around it.
