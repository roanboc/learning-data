Measures that matter: count what people get, not how busy you are

In July 2027, Helena's team is busy, but no one can say whether people get what they need. She puts eight measures on one page, in three groups, each with a baseline, one definition, a reader and a decision. By December, days from ask to first use are 14, not 41.
