## For Rodrigo's checklist: the audio release

The soundtrack ships as the films merged on 10 November ship theirs: the MP3 beside the player in `site/assets/better-every-week/`, committed with `git add -f`, and `audio.json` recording its checksum and the audio release that will hold it (`python site-tools/media.py index --film better-every-week-en`). Since PR #243, the *Store audio outside Git* workflow publishes and verifies that release on its own when the change reaches main, and once a day catches anything a run missed, so nothing is run by hand. Left for later, once the copy verifies: `python site-tools/media.py untrack` takes the MP3 out of Git's index (docs/maintenance.md).
