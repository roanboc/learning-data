What an agent is

An agent now works beside the data team. Ten films on what it is, what it's like to work with, and why we govern it.

The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1Decades in the making6½ min · An AI agent joins Jun's team, and seems to come from nowhere. Its ideas took decades: see where today's models came from.
  2. 2Borrowed from the brain12½ min · Why brains guess ahead, what models took from them, and why an agent that expects and checks the gap borrowed the loop.
  3. 3A model, reasoning, hands and a loop7 min · An agent finds the census-day count by trying, checking and trying again. Next year, a plain script does it in seconds.
  4. 4It feels like understanding7 min · The agent counted 2,160 students and its check passed. The lists held 2,880 people, and 720 never had their adviser's call.
  5. 5More than a junior7 min · Jun's team treats its AI agent like a junior. It knows more and works faster, but has no career, nothing to lose and no memory.
  6. 6Minds that change7 min · The agent gives three answers in two days; Clara's memory gives a wrong one. The team checks the work, the same way for both.
  7. 7Ethics we can't read, accountability it can't hold8½ min · An agent's values can't be fully read, and it can't answer for its work. So the team keeps its rights small, and a person answers.
  8. 8Why we govern them7½ min · Jun's agent could do more. First it needs an owner, a name of its own, limits it can't raise, and a person who checks its work.
  9. 9What they tell us about ourselves9½ min · Five open questions agents raise about us: the brain, creativity, copying a mind, feeling, and what makes us human.
  10. 10Many agents, and what emerges8 min · Small read-only agents gather the census reasons on one board, find a pattern no one asked for, and copy a wrong word.
