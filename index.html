The Inner Life of Data

A six-minute film with narration, music and sound. It shows how data is captured in the systems where it's born, refined through bronze, silver and gold with dbt on Databricks, given meaning through a shared conceptual model, and shared without making copies.

How it was made

Everything in the film is generated from code: a canvas animation engine draws each frame, an open-source voice model reads the narration, and the music and sound effects are synthesised to land on the story's cues. The source and rebuild guide are in this repository, so the film can be adapted, for example to another university's terms.
