# AI and continuous architecture: the series' labs and scenarios, questions and assumptions

*Enterprise architecture* · *AI and continuous architecture*, the series' own pack of labs and scenarios · its pull request is linked from the series' README once it merges

[The labs](https://data.learn-all.org/ai-and-continuous-architecture/labs/) and [the scenarios](https://data.learn-all.org/ai-and-continuous-architecture/scenarios/) on the site, once merged

The series' labs and scenarios (`site/assets/ai-and-continuous-architecture/`), made after its fifth film, from the plan Rodrigo merged on 11 November 2026 (pull request [#318](https://github.com/roanboc/learning-data/pull/318)) and his word that evening: "ok, merged, we can now start the films generation." Each question I would have asked, the assumption I made instead, where it shows, and what changes if it's wrong. Each film's own questions are in its file beside this one; the four new series' shared questions are in [`../0-module.md`](../0-module.md).

Written 12 November 2026.

| # | Question | Assumption made | Where it shows | If it's wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One pack for the series, or labs and scenarios for each film? | One pack for the whole series, on its own two pages, as *The map before the data* has: the series' films share one world, one cast and one story, and a visitor finds every lab in one place | `site-tools/build_series.py` (the series' `learn`), the series' pages | Medium: each film would need its own pages and words |
| 2 | How many labs and scenarios? | A lab for each film and three scenarios from each, fifteen in all for five films: as many as *The map before the data* has for each film | `learn.en.js`, `learn.es.js` | Easy: a lab or a scenario is a block of words |
| 3 | What kind of lab? | Every lab is a sort: cards dealt one at a time, each into its box, with why before the next, and a picture of the boxes as rows the cards fly into. The five films each teach a sorting of things (how far an agent goes, shared or its own, where each thing may be read, who does what, which check stops a change), so one kind fits all five, and one kind is one engine to keep | `learn.js` | Medium: another kind is a new part of the engine |
| 4 | What about *Kept alive*, the sixth film, not made yet? | The pack covers the five films out; *Kept alive* adds its own lab and three scenarios when it's made, and the pages count them by themselves | The series' README, *Labs and scenarios* | Easy |
| 5 | Which lab does each Pause and think question open? | Each film's three questions open its own film's lab | Each film's `think.en.js` and `think.es.js` (`stop`) | Easy: one word per question |
| 6 | Where do the labs' facts come from? | From the films, as they say them: the 212 payment plans, the crew planner's key, the chat's place and its levels, the battery's 41, 9, 8 and 4, the 1,900 households and the social tariff's fourteen elements. Nothing a film doesn't say is taught as a fact | Every lab | One card or line each |
| 7 | May the scenarios bring new situations? | Yes, in the films' world and under their rules: network planning's load-forecast agent wanting an analyst's sign-in, finance asking what the agents cost, households asking about their usage, new prices on the day, a faster copy of the tariffs, ten weeks for another new service, a report that fails a check, the map agent's new model, and a nightly job nobody drew. Each answer is the film's rule, said again | `learn.en.js`, `learn.es.js` (`qs`) | Easy: a scenario is a block of words |
| 8 | What may the map agent do when it finds a job nobody drew? | Only report it, with its evidence: it reads production read-only, with personal and sensitive data masked, and never switches anything off; the owner decides, and a release makes any change (AGENTS.md, *Agents never change production*). A wrong option says so | Scenario 53 | One option |
| 9 | Which Spanish words? | The films' Spanish captions: informar, redactar, verificar, ejecutar; un gestor de facturación; quien dirige comercialización; la puerta de enlace; público, interno, confidencial, restringido; comprobaciones; recorrido; lo que está en marcha. *What an agent may read* says once "centro de atención" and the other films "centro de contacto": the pack says "centro de contacto" | `learn.es.js` | Easy |
| 10 | What do the labs' pictures show? | Each lab's boxes as rows, in the films' glass and colours: the four steps rising for how far an agent goes, the six agents and the platform, the levels' colours for what the chat may read, a draft on paper for who confirms, and a stamped brick for the map's checks. Each scenario's picture draws its situation, and how it ends once it's answered | `learn.js` (`PIC`, `VIS`) | Medium: a picture each |
| 11 | Which film's player do the series' pages load, for the films' parts? | *Every change, checked*'s, named in the series' `learn` (`bundle`): it has every part the pictures draw (glass, paper, the agent's orb, the stamped brick), and *Kept alive*'s player, once made, may not | `site-tools/build_series.py` | Easy: one word |
| 12 | Does the pack need a step in the site's guided tour ("?" in the header)? | No: its controls (the labs' tabs, a card's boxes, the scenarios' film list and options) are the kinds other series' labs and scenarios pages have, and the tour has no step for those | `site/assets/learn/tour.en.js`, `tour.es.js` (unchanged) | Easy: a step in each |
