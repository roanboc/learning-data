The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1Owning the outcome7½ min · Every check passes, but forty rooms' counts look wrong. Ivy, who owns the data, says so first, fixes what's hers and raises what isn't.
  2. 2Measure the work, not the people8½ min · Helena's team reads its measures together, asks what slowed the work, never who, and fixes the system, not the people.
  3. 3When someone falls behind7½ min · Tomás falls behind the team after the reports move. Helena finds the causes with him, clears the way, and gives it time.
  4. 4Growing people8 min · Ivy writes her own goals, hears every Tuesday what her work changed, and grows into owning a product.
