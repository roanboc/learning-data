Growing people: their goals, weekly feedback about the work, the next step

In 2027 Ivy Reyes joins the university's data team, in her first job. Helena Novak asks her to write her own goals, gives her feedback every Tuesday about the work and what it changed, asks for feedback on herself, and looks forward with her to a next step with more to own.
