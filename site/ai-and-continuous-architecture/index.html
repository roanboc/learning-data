AI and continuous architecture

The last series on enterprise architecture. Agents and AI services are drawn on the map, AI helps the architects draft and check it, and the architecture is kept alive as a capability with an owner, a rhythm and checks on every change.

The films

Watch them in order, after The map before the data and Platform and solution architecture: each film builds on the one before.

One thread: a map that stays alive

Agents now work in the utility's processes, and the map has to show them. Film by film, Tomás draws agents in roles of their own, puts the AI platform on the map, decides what an agent may read, drafts from reference models instead of a blank page, checks every change, and keeps the architecture alive.
