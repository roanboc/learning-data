Good enough for what? One list, four uses, four levels

In December 2025, the graduation list passed every check and seated the Great Hall well, and still 41 degree certificates were printed again. In January, Malia Fonoti, Mei Tanaka and Jun Park ask who uses the list, for what, and what goes wrong when it's wrong, and agree a level for each use.
