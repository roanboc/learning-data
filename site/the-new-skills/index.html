One thread: a year with the data team, and Graham's rooms

In 2027, a year from now, Helena Novak leads the university's data team, and Ivy Reyes joins it in March. Graham Ellis asks for a dashboard of room use; the question behind it is whether to ask the University Council for a new teaching building. Film by film, Ivy learns the whole route from his decision back to the rooms' sensors, frames work for an agent, and learns beside the people who know.
