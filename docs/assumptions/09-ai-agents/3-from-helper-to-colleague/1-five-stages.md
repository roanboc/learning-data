## Rodrigo's answers, 12 November 2026

His answers to the final checklist's open questions. This film's question was number 14 there; he added to it the same day, when asked whether an agent may read production at all.

| # | The question | His answer | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Is "never stage 5 in production" your rule? | "Yeah, agents can't perform changes in PRD, if there is something that needs to be triggered, for example rerunning failed pipelines due to connectivity, that's a deterministic automation (even some hours later) that reruns it." | Confirmed, and stricter than the assumption: an agent changes nothing in production, at any stage, and what only needs to be started or run again, such as a pipeline that failed on a lost connection, is a plain, scheduled job's, with no model in it, even hours later. Nothing in the film changes, since it already says so: "in production, it drafts the fix, and another person approves every change", "In production, the same task is at stage four: it finds the cause and drafts the fix, and Noor approves it", "It never just runs the build again: a plain job does that", and on screen "in production: drafts only" and "a plain job reruns it". Every film of the module was read against his answer that day (the narration of its 26 films, their Pause and think, the labs and scenarios of *What an agent is*, and the example project's `docs/stages.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `agents/` and `governance/8-mandates/`): none shows an agent changing, starting or running anything again in production. The rule is now one of the repository's own, in `AGENTS.md`, *Rules that always apply* ("Agents never change production"), and in `PLAYBOOK.md` §3 |
| 4, his addition | May an agent read production to diagnose a problem and then open a ticket or pull request for a person? Or is production fully off limits, including reading? | "Can read production with limited access, generally all PII and sensitive+ classified data masked." | Reading production is allowed, limited and masked; changing it stays out, as above. Read as: read-only, only what its role's steps need, with personal data masked and nothing classified sensitive or above in the clear. Nothing in this film changes: in production its agent "finds the cause and drafts the fix", and it says nothing of what the agent may read there. Three films that do say it said "names masked", which a viewer could take for names only; they now say "personal and sensitive data masked", in the voice and on the role's card: *A place on the map*, *Rights follow the role* (whose close also said "none of them reaches production", and now says "none of them lets it change production") and *The map, enforced*. The rule is in `AGENTS.md`, *Rules that always apply* (within "Agents never change production"), in `PLAYBOOK.md` §3, and in the example project's `AGENTS.md` (*Your access*), `agents/` and `docs/databricks-governance.md` (section 9). **Two things for him to check.** In the example project, "sensitive or above" is read as the class `special` and the level `restricted`: production's core and marts are `confidential`, hold neither, and are what the agents read, with direct personal data masked; if confidential tables should be closed to agents too, their grants need narrowing. And *Tools are hands* and *Borrowed rights* still say "names masked" of an agent's samples; they take the fuller words when those lines next change, or sooner if he asks |
