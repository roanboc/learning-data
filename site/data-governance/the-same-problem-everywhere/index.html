The same problem, everywhere: who decides, and who answers

In 2008, a bank couldn't add up how much it stood to lose: the numbers were there, and nobody could put them together. People who look after shared data had met over the same problem since 1980. In 2009 they wrote down what they knew, the DMBOK, with governance at its centre.
