From gaps to a roadmap: stages that each work on their own

In April 2027 Helena and the team set today's state beside the target and find nine gaps. They turn them into six work packages, each with an owner and a date, and three stages that each work on their own. On 27 April the data council approves a data strategy on one page.

Go further

The film names each idea once. These pages go further, and are free to read. Checked on 10 November 2026.

  • The TOGAF StandardThe Open Group's method for enterprise architecture, whose migration planning turns the gaps into work packages and steps between today and the target.
  • ArchiMateThe Open Group's notation, which draws the gaps, the work packages and the plateaus, the steady states along the way.
  • Rail 2000Switzerland's plan that started from the timetable, its hubs and the vote of 1987, and the first stage, opened in 2004.
  • Clock-face schedulingTrains every hour at the same minutes past, as Switzerland's railways ran them from 1982, and where else they run that way.
  • The Swiss path to the Railway of the FutureGisela Hürlimann's history of the Swiss railways from the 1960s to 2000, from the fast lines dropped to Rail 2000 (2005).
