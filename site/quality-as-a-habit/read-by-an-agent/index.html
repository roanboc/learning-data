The agent answered 412, and empty meant two things

In October 2026, Malia asked the graduations team's agent which December graduates hadn't confirmed their name, and it answered 412 at once. An empty date meant two things: 38 hadn't confirmed yet, and 374 had confirmed on the paper form. Nothing written said so, and the agent can't ask.
