# People first

*The series that opens the module Leading data people, before* The new skills.

Before the hats and the skills, the people. In February 2027, Helena Novak takes over the university's data team: seven people, a chart of seven boxes and a list of 46 open requests. Four films: *People before roles* (know the people before you plan the work); *Wellbeing is in the work* (fix the work before you ask people to cope); *No two alike* (ask each person how they work, and never sort them); and *How, more than who* (agree how you work together, and keep to it). *The new skills* follows. The series' plan is in its [proposal](proposal.md); the plan for the module is in [`../the-new-skills/proposal.md`](../the-new-skills/proposal.md).

**Status:** set up on 12 November 2026 from the proposal, which the author asked for in his review of the module that day; the films are being made, each marked draft on the site, and appear in the table below as each one is out. On the site, the series opens the topic *Leading data people*, at `people-first/`, once its opening film is out; until then the topic names it as coming soon.

## The look and the sound

- **Sunlit plaster**, the module's look, assumed on 5 November 2026 for the author to review (the module's *Decided* table, in [`../the-new-skills/proposal.md`](../the-new-skills/proposal.md)). The kit is in [`../the-new-skills/shared/`](../the-new-skills/shared/), and this series bundles it as it is. It draws the whole team of seven for the first time.
- **Two true stories, in the module's own drawing style.** Three films follow the rope bridge of Q'eswachaka, in Peru, each opening on its own part of it, and the last says what the story didn't achieve. *No two alike* steps aside to a day in Confucius's school. Nothing Andean or Chinese is borrowed for the look or the sound.
- **Its own sound in each film.** A lead instrument the site doesn't have, reserved for each film. Music under the opening story, then only now and then; nothing loops, trembles or pulses, except an urgent pulse where a story turns to an error or a rush.

## How the films are made

Like the site's other series, each film is generated from code, with the engine, components, fonts and voice of *The Inner Life of Data*, *A Sharper Sketch*'s scenes, *From words to data*'s tools and the agent films' kit, and checked with the governance and agent films' checks (`twice.py`, `steady.py`, `jump.py`, `edges.py`, `legible.py`, `quiet.py` with `held.py`) and `site-tools/fits.py`. A film keeps its own pictures and helpers in its own source. The films are about people, so tools appear only where the story's work happens in them, at the level of what people do. Each film's page links what a newcomer can read next.

## The series' rules

The module's rules hold. The [proposal](proposal.md) adds these, for every film here:

- **Never for assessment.** Nothing about a person's traits is used to hire, to hand out work, to judge or to assess, and no trait is ranked above another (the author, 12 November 2026).
- **No test is named, and no type letters are drawn,** in a film, on a page or in Pause and think. The script's sources name them.
- **Nobody is labelled, and nothing is diagnosed.** A person's way of working is shown in their own words, and the films say once where a manager's part ends.
- **Private matters stay private.**
- **The people are never the grass.** At the bridge, the people are the ones who build.
- **Two traditions, told with respect:** from their own sources, the bridge in the present tense, and with no judgement of custom.
- **Nothing personal from the author's papers** is shown or named.
