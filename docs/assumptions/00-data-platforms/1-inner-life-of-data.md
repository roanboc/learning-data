# The Inner Life of Data: questions and assumptions

*Data platforms* · *The Inner Life of Data*, in English and Spanish

[Watch it on the site](https://data.learn-all.org/#watch)

> Kept by the thread "Check that text stays inside its box", 12 November 2026.

The film was made with the author, so it had no list here. On 12 November 2026 he saw words run past a card on a phone in another series, and asked: "Can we check this via code to fix those glitches?" The check (`site-tools/fits.py`) found one piece of text past its box in this film. It was fixed while he was away. Each line: the question I would have asked, what I did instead, and where it shows.

| # | Question | Assumption | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On the phone the student enrols with, the unit's name, *Data Science 101*, was a little wider than the screen: two or three pixels on each side. Widen the phone, or draw the name smaller? | Smaller: 11 where it was 12.5, in the phone's own measure, so the name sits inside the screen. Nothing else on the phone changed, and the Spanish film has the same fix. | 0:03 to 0:25 |
| 2 | The phone is drawn by code that the films made after this one share. Change it there? | No: that would be a new version of every film that shares it. This film draws its own screen on the shared phone's body, and the shared code is unchanged. | The same seconds |
| 3 | Draw the posters again? | No. The posters, English and Spanish, show the platform's layers, a moment this change doesn't touch. | The home page, before Play |
