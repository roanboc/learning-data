| 1 | Which part of the Gokaidō does this film take? | The station on the mountain: every Tōkaidō station had the same parts (an office that found horses and porters, a clerk who wrote each one down, inns), but the hardest stage, Odawara to Mishima over the Hakone mountains, had none until the shogunate founded Hakone's in 1618, with fifty households from each end; the stage became two. It stands for the part every platform needs and nobody built. The plan's distance mounds and ferries were left out.
