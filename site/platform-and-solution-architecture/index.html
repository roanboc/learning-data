The films

Watch them in order, after The map before the data and Data architecture: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1An application or a platform?9 min · An application has one purpose, its logic inside. A data platform carries every domain's logic, each rule with an owner.
  2. 2Inside the platform7 min · Draw a data platform's parts before its products, and what's missing shows: here, nothing watched the platform itself.
  3. 3One platform, or many8 min · Every platform brings its own rules: keep one, with every domain owning its data on it, and a second only for a reason you can name.
  4. 4Where it meets the applications8½ min · Each flow on the route that's simplest, cheapest and easiest to govern, and every flow on one map both teams keep.
  5. 5Security all the way through7½ min · Every request says who's asking, and is checked where the data is, with only the rights it needs and no key in the code.
  6. 6One solution, end to end9½ min · Each solution planned end to end: its context, its qualities, its options, and why, written down.
  7. 7A road people choose8½ min · A platform teams choose: a path already laid, the rules built in, a guide, and someone to ask.
  8. 8Built to change9½ min · Buy what every utility needs, build what's your own, keep your data in open formats, and test the way out each year.
