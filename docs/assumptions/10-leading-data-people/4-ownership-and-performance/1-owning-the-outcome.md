| 1 | Is the Green Belt Movement the right opening, told this way? | Yes, as `openings.md` proposed, checked against its sources on 10 November 2026: the movement's own history (rural women's streams drying up, food less secure, firewood further each year; founded in 1977 under the National Council of Women of Kenya), Maathai's Nobel Lecture of 2004 (the needs rural women named; forests cleared for commercial plantations; trees that give fuel, food, shelter and income), Wikipedia (more than 51 million trees; first African woman to win the Nobel Peace Prize, 2004), the University of Kansas Community Tool Box (nurseries from indigenous seed; a token for each seedling that survives, checked at one and three months) and the Goldman Prize (curbing soil erosion; thousands of group nurseries).
