| 1 | Which part of the bridge's story opens this film? | The wear and the renewal, as the series' plan gives it to this film: grass rope weakens in the sun, the rain and the wind; a visitor found the bridge sagging five months after one renewal; it was not renewed in 2020, in the pandemic, and fell in March 2021, at twenty-one months old; so the communities make the whole bridge new each year, on a date, and in June 2021 they wove it again with their own means. Checked against the study Peru's Ministry of Culture published in 2015 and the reports of 2021 | *Before it falls* | The opening's lines and tags |
| 2 | Why was the wear a problem for people, in the story's own words? | "People who cross a gorge on rope need it renewed before it fails, not repaired after." This line is mine. It rests on a writer who crossed the bridge ("an Incan bridge can't be patched up or have parts swapped out. It can only be replaced wholesale") and on the custom itself. The communities' own reason comes first in the film: they hold that the bridge's life depends on their care | *Before it falls* | One line and one sheet |
| 3 | How long was the bridge left, around 1970? | The film says "left for a time", and no length. A visitor of 1970 wrote of a few years, and the Ministry's study calls the lapse brief; the series' plan asks every film to say it this way. Why they let it go and why they went back is the opening film's to tell | *Where to look* | One line and one sheet |
| 4 | What did the fall look like, and who are the people at the edge in 1972? | Both are the film's own drawing. No report describes how the bridge fell: it gives way from the middle, falls to the river, and its ends are left hanging at the bases, with nobody on it and nothing said about that. The two visitors of 1972 are plain figures in field clothes of the time, nobody's likeness, and the keeper is drawn as the series draws a bridge builder | *Before it falls*, *Where to look* | A few seconds of drawing |
| 5 | Is the keeper named? | On screen only, under his words, as the Ministry's study names him: "Luis Choqueneira, a keeper of the bridge · October 1972". The voice says "its keeper". | 8 | How is burn-out said? | In the World Health Organization's spelling, and in plain words for its three signs: worn out, distant from the job, and feeling no longer good at it. "A matter of work, not a medical condition" stands for "an occupational phenomenon", "not classified as a medical condition". Nobody in the film has it: the lines are advice | *Three signs, and a limit* | A few lines and one sheet |
| 9 | Where does a manager's part end, and how is it said? | Once: "And a manager's part has an end. Helena is not a clinician." Nobody in the film needs it | *Three signs, and a limit* | Two lines |
| 10 | Is "hundreds of studies" fair? | The study of 2024 pools 881 samples and 443,556 people, from workplaces, classrooms, sport, parenting and health care. The voice says "hundreds of studies since, at work, in school and in sport", and the screen gives the two numbers; it never says the samples are of people at work. "Somewhat better" stands for a link with performance that the study finds weak to moderate | *Three things everyone needs* | One line, voiced again, and one tag |
| 11 | Which hazards are named, and from whom? | Safe Work Australia's, three of them: job demands, low job control, poor support. The voice says "hazards at work" for "psychosocial hazards". Its page was read; its model code of practice wasn't | *Where to look* | One line and one list |
| 12 | Which ideas come from your papers, and are they named? | Three, with no name: what makes people care isn't what stops them being unhappy; ask the whole team in writing, show the answers and ask again; and go prepared to the people who ask (what we want, what they want, what both want, the least we can accept). Two practices are left out on purpose: extra hours when a team falls behind, and opening a negotiation above one's goal | Chapters 4, 5 and 8 | Those chapters' lines |
| 13 | Which instrument? | A portative organ, the small organ played on the knee, one hand on the keys and the other on the bellows, with its own two stopped pipes as partner; reserved in the module's list on 12 November 2026.
