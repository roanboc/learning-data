# Small steps, measured

*The last series of the module Data management.*

Delivering and improving data work in small steps. Facilities asks for a dashboard of room use by Easter; the real question is whether to build. Four films: *Prototype first* (something real in front of the person who decides within two weeks, then short rounds); *Measures that matter* (a few measures of what people get, each with a baseline and a decision); *Better every week* (one measure, one small change, every week); and *Strategy is a habit* (review the plan every quarter, and move it out loud when the world moves). *Making the case* comes before it. The plan for the module is in [`../a-plan-in-ninety-days/proposal.md`](../a-plan-in-ninety-days/proposal.md); this series' own part is in its [proposal](proposal.md).

**Status:** set up on 5 November 2026, on assumptions the author reviews later; the films are being made, one at a time, each marked draft on the site, and appear in the table below as each one is out. On the site, the series sits under the topic *Data management*, after *Making the case*, at `small-steps-measured/`, once its opening film is out.

## How the films are made

Like the site's other series, each film is generated from code, with the engine, components, fonts and voice of *The Inner Life of Data*, *A Sharper Sketch*'s scenes, *From words to data*'s tools and the agent films' kit, and checked with the governance and agent films' checks (`twice.py`, `steady.py`, `jump.py`, `edges.py`, `legible.py`, `quiet.py` with `held.py`). A film keeps its own pictures and helpers in its own source. Where a film shows data work, the example project, [`../analytics-engineering/project/`](../analytics-engineering/project/), is where it happens. Each film's page links the guides and standards it points to.
