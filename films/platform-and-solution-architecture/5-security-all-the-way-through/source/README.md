# Rebuilding Security all the way through

*Security all the way through*, film 5 of 8 of *Platform and solution architecture*, is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, *The map before the data*'s look and people from [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/), and this series' own kit, [`../../shared/src/psa.js`](../../shared/src/psa.js), which every film of the series bundles: its title and end cards and mark, Ravi Menon, Japan's coast and the road, its walkers, and the platform drawn as a slab. Only what is new for this film lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its source files (the series' kit, then `scenes.js`), the words the voice respells (Tomás, Ama Owusu, Ravi, Edo, Tōkaidō, shogunate, Hakone, Ashi), and the moment its poster shows (every job and person with a pass of their own, every request checked where the data is). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). The film re-times itself to the voice; a line's `say` gives the years and data_loader as the voice should read them. |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and two wordless moments: every job and every person with a pass of their own, every request checked at the catalogue, then the title; and the checkpoint by the lake with the platform's gate, then the end card. |
| `src/scenes.js` | The ten chapters, and what they share (prefixed `p5_`): the utility's room (`p5_room`); a light that travels to the data (`p5_runner`, `p5_route`, `p5_req`); a name on a chip, with its sign, a pass of its own or a line through it (`p5_chip`, `p5_icon`, `p5_slip`), a key, a padlock, a laptop and a red cross (`p5_key`, `p5_lock`, `p5_laptop`, `p5_cross`); the checkpoint at Hakone at dusk, seen from the road, its gate, fence, guardhouse and cedars, and the side path that would go round it (`p5_check`, `p5_gate`, `p5_fence`, `p5_guard`, `p5_cedar`, `P5_HILL`, `P5_FENCE`, `P5_SIDE`); a woman travelling, and the examiner (`p5_woman`); the woman's pass, typing itself (`p5_pass`); Japan's map with Lake Ashi and the checkpoint on it (`p5_map`, `p5_mapGate`, `P5_LAKE`); the data platform with its six domains' tables and the files under them (`p5_dp`, `p5_tab`, `P5_TABS`), the catalogue as a gate across every way to the data, in the Hakone gate's shape (`p5_cat`); the identities the film follows, their passes and grants (`p5_ids`, `p5_grant`, `P5_ID`, `P5_GR`); the platform's log, a card of code and the regulator's letter (`p5_log`, `p5_code`, `p5_letter`); data_loader's jobs and laptops (`P5_DL`, `P5_LAPS`); the secret store (`p5_vault`); and the platform's template (`P5_TPL`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds: a crumhorn, whose reed is shut inside a wind cap so each note comes out exactly as its holes set it, at one level, with a serpent, the leather-covered wooden bass of about 1590, both made here; B flat major, G minor and D minor for what goes wrong, tuned pure from B flat; the series' mark, *the relay*, in B flat (the crumhorn's B flat, C and F rising, the F taken an octave down by the serpent and carried to B flat), under the title and the end card; the film's own figure, *the pass*, the crumhorn's short phrase answered note for note by the serpent an octave down, whole as the examiner checks the woman against her pass, short where nobody can tell who read the bank details, and whole again at the end; no instrument, scale or sound of Japan, and the lake, the road and the gate heard only as water, feet, paper and a brush. Music under the opening story, then only now and then; each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs the series' tool (`../../shared/tools/run.py`) on this film: *The map before the data*'s `build.py`, *From words to data*'s other tools (its `legible.py`, with `--overlaps`, and its `music.py`), and the governance films' checks (`twice.py` and `hands.py` in `tools/check.py`; `quiet.py`, with `held.py`, in `tools/quiet.py`). |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/platform-and-solution-architecture/5-security-all-the-way-through/source/`; then `python tools/quiet.py` after `tools/audio.py`, to check the sound. The video is `dist/security-all-the-way-through.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/security-all-the-way-through/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/security-all-the-way-through/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `who`, `checked` and `default`. Progress is stored under `ld-security-all-the-way-through`.
