The same one, everywhere

A series on master and reference data. Linh graduated from the university, and now studies, tutors and does research there. She is five records in four systems, and nobody sees they are one person. Film by film, the university learns to know her as one person, keep her data right, and use it only for its purpose.

The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

Film 1 of 9 · The person behind the role

One person, many roles

One person, four systems, five records. Keep the person apart from the roles, and ask four questions to know what needs mastering.

6 min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere

Film 2 of 9 · Matching and the golden record

Who is it?

Two records, one person? A check proposes pairs, weights make a score, and a steward decides the hard ones once, with a reason.

5½ min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere

Film 3 of 9 · Survivorship

Which value wins

Five linked records, and the values still disagree. Declare a rule for each field, not one order hidden in the code.

6 min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere

Film 4 of 9 · Roles with dates

Roles come and go

A status is true for a day. Give every role a start, an end and an owner, and ask any question as at a date.

5½ min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere

Film 5 of 9 · Reference data

Lists everyone uses

Three names for one role, and a country counted three times. Give each list an owner, a date and one home.

4½ min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere

Film 6 of 9 · Purpose and consent

For this purpose only

Data given for one role isn't free for every role. Write each role's purposes beside its data, and keep the person's wishes with her.

5 min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere

Film 7 of 9 · Sharing master data

One person, many systems

Four ways to give many systems one person. Search before you create, and choose the lightest way that solves the problem.

4½ min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere

Film 8 of 9 · A master data hub

A hub at work

Rules act, AI suggests, people decide: a week in a master data hub, and five questions to ask any hub.

4½ min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere

Film 9 of 9 · Keeping master data

Kept, not cleaned

A clean-up merged two thousand duplicates, and a year later there were eleven hundred new ones. Check at entry, measure every day, and keep it.

5½ min · Pause and think · The same one, everywhere
