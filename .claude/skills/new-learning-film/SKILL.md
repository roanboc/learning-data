---
name: new-learning-film
description: Process for every new Learning Data film, series or topic change in roanboc/learning-data, conceptual foundation first, then visual craft. Use before proposing, scripting or rendering a film.
---

Read and follow [`AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md) at the repository root: its four stages (conceptual foundation, story and world, video craft at the highest effort, check and publish) and its rules that always apply. Ask the author for the reference framework and their real experience before drafting anything. Share every new or changed video on a review page (`python site-tools/review_page.py <film folder> --pr <number>`, published as a private artifact), with the pull request link, and wait for the author's approval or corrections. Don't attach video files: the chat may only offer them as a download.
