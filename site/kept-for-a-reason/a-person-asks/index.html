Read the guidance yourself

The film shows Databricks, dbt and GitHub, and names one law, Europe's GDPR, only in its sources. These official pages are where its steps come from.

  • Guidelines on the right of accessThe European Data Protection Board: how to know it's the person asking, why every system is searched by every identifier, and what the answer must say.
  • Lineage system tablesDatabricks: the tables, notebooks, jobs and dashboards built from a table, and why lineage can't capture every read or write.
  • Prepare your data for GDPR complianceDatabricks: a table of requests that drives a scheduled job, and deleting so no old version of a table keeps the data.
  • Query history system tableDatabricks: a query's text is kept, and can hold personal data, which is why a person's numbers are never typed into one.
  • Graph operatorsdbt: select a source and every model built from it, with a plus sign, to list everything a person's data flows into.
  • About task listsGitHub: a checklist in an issue, each step ticked as it's done, with a link to its proof.
