Agents at work on data

The second series on AI agents in data work. Jun's agent now works on the census-day count, the number the university reports to the government. Six films show how agents do data work well, from what they read to how they go wrong; three show it on Databricks, governed.

One thread: Jun's agent on the census-day count

Jun's agent works on the census-day count, the number the university reports to the government. Film by film, the team gives it what to read, skills to follow, hands with the fewest rights and a memory the whole team shares; decides how each result will be checked before it starts; designs for the ways it goes wrong; and then runs it all on Databricks, governed like any data, before the next series gives the agent a role of its own.
