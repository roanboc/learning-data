The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. The same problem, everywhere5 min · In 2008, a bank couldn't add up its risks. People had met over the same problem since 1980. Why is governance still at the centre?
  2. 1Off the shelf5½ min · Thirty-four policies on a shelf, and two numbers that disagree. Ask every policy one question: where does it run?
  3. 2Who decides5½ min · Eight areas, three owners still to be confirmed, and decisions lost in email. Make the work ask its owner, by name.
  4. 3One word, one home6 min · Five glossaries, three meanings of one word. Keep one that everyone trusts, and sync every tool from it.
  5. 4Who may see it6 min · A planner needs one column, and the old path gives her the whole table. Classify each column once, and protect it everywhere.
  6. 5Rules that run6 min · Monitored means a yearly survey. Give every check an owner, a level and a reason, and read the scorecard from what ran.
  7. 6Fixed where it starts6 min · One cell stays amber because emails are cleaned downstream. Follow the problem back to where it starts, fix it there, and keep the fix as a check.
  8. 7Change without surprises6½ min · A monthly board answers from memory. Keep a record of who uses what, and give every change an impact note the owners fill in.
  9. 8A product with an owner6 min · A count that came late, and nobody who answered for it. Write down what the data promises, and name an owner who keeps it.
  10. 9An agent that checks6½ min · Every change waits for the one person who knows the rules. Write them down so an agent can check them, and let people decide.
  11. 10Teachers of data7 min · A check stops an analyst, and its page says why and how to fix it. Teach first, so a check stops only the few.
