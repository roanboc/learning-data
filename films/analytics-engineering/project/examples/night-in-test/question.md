# A question for HR: where a tutor who moved counts this term

*From the workload agent, under its mandate, to HR, who decide what a word about staff means (the person domain's
tutor role is HR's: `models/core/person/_person__conceptual.yml`). Copied to Anil Kurian, its owner. The night of
Wednesday 4 November 2026. It changed nothing.*

**The tutor.** `HR|T-26247`, a casual contract to tutor in 2026 Semester 2. HR holds two versions of it: the first,
recorded in July, to teach a Nursing unit; the second, recorded on 28 September, to teach a Public Health unit for
the rest of the term.

**What the workload agent's count does now.** It counts each contract once, in the version that holds today, so
this tutor counts in Public Health, as they did before Wednesday's change. Nursing: 64 teachers, 38 of them casual.

**What no rule says.** Whether a tutor who moves during a term counts in both schools that term, or only in the one
they teach in now. The glossary's tutor is "someone who teaches a unit under a contract, one teaching period at a
time"; it doesn't say where a tutor counts when their unit changes within the period.

**What each answer would change.**

| If a tutor who moves counts | Nursing | Public Health |
|---|---|---|
| Only where they teach now (as now) | 64 teachers, 38 of them casual | No change |
| In both schools, that term | 65 teachers, 39 of them casual | No change |

**What happens next.** HR decides. The meaning goes into the glossary first, then into the workload mart, in a pull
request that people review; a change to a definition is never the agent's. Until then, the count stays as it is.
