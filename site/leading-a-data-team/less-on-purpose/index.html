Less, on purpose: keep what people use, and stop the rest together

In 2027 the university's data team runs 38 old reports, sits in standing meetings about eight hours a week each and gets about 60 alerts a week, most needing nothing. Helena Novak and the team ask who uses each thing, and for what, then stop the rest together, openly and so it can come back.
