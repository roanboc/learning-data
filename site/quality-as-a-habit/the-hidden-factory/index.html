Every name fixed by hand, and the same errors back every year

Before every ceremony, Malia Fonoti and a colleague checked every name on the graduation list by hand: about 320 hours a year, and the same errors came back. In August 2026 the team counts that hidden work, traces each error to where it's born, and fixes it there, with its owner.
