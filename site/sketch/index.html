How to read the film

The film opens in China in 1391, drawn warm, on paper: a household register's four columns, what it held, what came in, what went out and what it holds now. The sketch is the university's conceptual model, in blue ink. The reference model, TCSI, is drawn in warm ink on tracing paper. Blue pins mark what the sketch adopts from the reference; amber pins mark what the university adds. The comparison in the corner closes as the model sharpens. The narration is a synthetic voice.
