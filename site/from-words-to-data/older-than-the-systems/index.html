How to read the film

The past is drawn warm, like clay, parchment and wax, and the film's wax red marks the idea that runs through it; the present is the films' dark glass. Each system keeps its colour: amber for the student system, green for the learning platform, violet for careers, teal for the digital wallet, pink for the short-course platform. Gold is the shared model, and blue marks today's standard and shared lists of values. The university, people and numbers are fictional. The narration is a synthetic voice.

English captions are on by default; turn them off with Captions. The video you download has none on the picture: play it with its caption file, in English or Spanish.
