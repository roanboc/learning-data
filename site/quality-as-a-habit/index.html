Quality as a habit

The second series of Data quality. Every November, two people spend three weeks fixing the graduation list's names by hand, in a copy, and every year the same names come back wrong. The team counts that hidden work, finds where each error is born, answers every graduate who reports one, and improves the list a little every week.

One thread: the graduation list, from fixing by hand to fixing at the source

Every November, two people check every name on the graduation list by hand, in a spreadsheet, and the next year the same names are wrong again. From August to December 2026, the team traces each kind of error to where it's born and fixes it there; a graduate reports his own name and is answered in two days; an agent learns what an empty column means; and every Thursday the team makes one small change and measures it. By December, 3 certificates are printed again, not 41.
