# Rebuilding Rules with a home

*Rules with a home*, film 9 of 11 of *The map before the data* (a working title), is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/`](../../shared/src/), which this film uses as they are. Its own pictures live with its scenes, among them a rule as the series draws one from here on (`d9_rule`): dark glass with motivation's purple edge and ArchiMate's requirement glyph, with its kind, data rule or business rule, as its kicker (`d9_kind`). Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás), and the moment its poster shows (the two rules settled on their sides of the wall, the replaced reading crossing at the gate). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: Tartu's register answering every office that asks, then the title; the two rules settling on their sides of the wall, with the replaced reading crossing between them; and the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `d9_`): Tartu at dawn, looking down the Emajõgi to Kaarsild, with the old town, birches and the family's window (`d9_tartu`, `d9_sky`, `d9_city`, `d9_river`, `d9_bridge`, `d9_banks`, `d9_birch`, `d9_building`, `d9_family`); the nightly script, the readings it looks through and the queue it writes to (`d9_script`, `d9_readings`, `d9_queue`); the rule's five places and the empty place where its home should be (`D9_DOCS`, `d9_doc`, `d9_slot`); and the end's count and Tartu card (`d9_count`, `d9_tartuCard`). |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/enterprise-architecture/9-rules-with-a-home/source/`; the video is `dist/rules-with-a-home.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/rules-with-a-home/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/rules-with-a-home/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `split`, `expect` and `onto`, and the first opens the series' lab *Give the rule a home*. Progress is stored under `ld-rules-with-a-home`.
