A person can ask, and you must be able to answer

Linh Nguyen's tutoring contract ends on 20 November. Four days later she asks what the university holds about her, and to leave the tutors' mailing list. Her data sits under six numbers, in documents and copies too. To answer in 30 days, the university must find it all, with a reason for every part.
