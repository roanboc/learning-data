What changes from today

  • The hero's lead is one line (the series card), not a paragraph. A Continue button goes to the next film you haven't watched.
  • Films are compact rows, not tall cards with a paragraph each. Each row has a Mark watched toggle, so a film seen on a phone can be ticked on the laptop (progress lives in each browser).
  • Labs and evaluation show the same strip as the topic page: labs seen, situations answered, right. The project is one folded line. Every series ends with "Up next", which no series page has today.
