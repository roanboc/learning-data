# Rebuilding One solution, end to end

*One solution, end to end*, film 6 of 8 of *Platform and solution architecture*, is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, *The map before the data*'s look and people from [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/), and this series' own kit, [`../../shared/src/psa.js`](../../shared/src/psa.js), which every film of the series bundles: its title and end cards and mark, Ravi Menon, Japan's coast and the road, its walkers, horses and stations, and the platform drawn as a slab. Only what is new for this film lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its source files (the series' kit, then `scenes.js`), the words the voice respells (Tomás, Ama, Ravi, Sipho, Dlamini, Yasmin, Edo, Tōkaidō, shogunate, IT), and the moment its poster shows (Sipho, and the one line from the agency's answer to the bill, on the platform everyone shares). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). The film re-times itself to the voice; a line's `say` gives the year as the voice should read it. |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and two wordless moments: Sipho and the one line, end to end, then the title; and the utility's room with the line lit and its chips, then the end card. |
| `src/scenes.js` | The twelve chapters, and what they share (prefixed `p6_`): the road at dusk with its station, porters and horses (`p6_road`, `p6_proc`, `p6_board`, `p6_load`), the map with the notice running ahead and a year's journeys (`p6_journeys`, `P6_NOTICE`); the utility's room, with Sipho at its left (`p6_room`, `p6_me`), the three pieces nobody has joined (`p6_pieces`, `p6_list`), and the one line from the agency's answer to the bill (`p6_line`); chips, rows, a runner and routes (`p6_chip`, `p6_row`, `p6_runner`, `p6_route`, `p6_q`, `p6_mark`); a strip of the road that comes back to the story (`p6_strip`); a dashed element for what's out of scope (`p6_dashEl`), a requirement and an event with their own glyphs, and a work package (`p6_mel`, `p6_work`); the table of measures the options are tested against (`p6_table`); a notice in flight (`p6_note`), a bill and a home (`p6_bill`, `p6_home`); and text in flight marked as decoration on purpose (`PH_OFF`, `p6_deco`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds: a tenor recorder, tongued with a small chiff, with a cittern, the wire-strung lute of 1600, plucked with a quill and damped short, both made here; E flat major, C minor and G minor for what goes wrong, tuned pure from E flat; the series' mark, *the relay*, in E flat (the recorder's E flat, F and B flat rising, the B flat taken an octave down by the cittern and carried to E flat), under the title and the end card; the film's own figure, *the parts*, one short tune shared a note each in turn, whole as the officials plan the journey, each part alone and out of step where the teams start their own pieces, and whole again at the end; no instrument, scale or sound of Japan, and the road heard only as feet, a pack horse's hooves, paper and a board set down. Music under the opening story, then only now and then; each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs the series' tool (`../../shared/tools/run.py`) on this film: *The map before the data*'s `build.py`, *From words to data*'s other tools (its `legible.py`, with `--overlaps`, and its `music.py`), and the governance films' checks (`twice.py` and `hands.py` in `tools/check.py`; `quiet.py`, with `held.py`, in `tools/quiet.py`). |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/platform-and-solution-architecture/6-one-solution-end-to-end/source/`; then `python tools/quiet.py` after `tools/audio.py`, to check the sound. The video is `dist/one-solution-end-to-end.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/one-solution-end-to-end/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/one-solution-end-to-end/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `quality`, `options` and `reuse`. Progress is stored under `ld-one-solution-end-to-end`.
