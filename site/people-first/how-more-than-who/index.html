How, more than who: a turn each, promises kept, and one page

In February 2027, Jun and Tomás of the university's data team find two numbers for the same day, and the argument turns to the person. Two people do all the talking. Helena Novak stops it, gives everyone a turn, and the eight of them write one page on how they work together.
