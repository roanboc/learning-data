The films

Watch them in order: the ideas first, then the tools.

  1. 1An agreement first7½ min · Each person signs a promise, each use gets an agreement, and access is made from it: the end of an agreement, not the start.
  2. 2Know what you hold7½ min · Five classes for every column and a level for every table, set once at the source by the data's owner.
  3. 3Show the least7½ min · A study needs learners' records without knowing who they are. Each column gets a way to hide it that keeps what the study needs.
  4. 4Grants by design9 min · Three rights to read a table, a shelf for each set of readers, groups as owners, and the plan kept as code.
  5. 5Tags that travel8 min · Governed tags set once from the owner's file, carried by lineage into every table, and a rule set in advance where two meet.
  6. 6One policy for many tables8 min · One policy for each class, found by its tag: every table protected the day it appears, and the study sees only its faculty.
  7. 7Copies that keep the rules9 min · Made-up learners to build with, totals to share, a clean room for both sides, and no download: every copy keeps the rules.
  8. 8Proof it held8½ min · A review, a morning check, lineage and the audit log: proof, read from records, that the study's protection held.
