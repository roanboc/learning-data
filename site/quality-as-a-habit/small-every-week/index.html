Small, every week: one change, measured, then kept

In December 2025, 41 degree certificates were printed again from a wrong list. Instead of one big clean-up, Malia, Mei, Jun and Sam met for fifteen minutes every Thursday, made one small change, measured it and kept it with a check. A year later, it was 3.
