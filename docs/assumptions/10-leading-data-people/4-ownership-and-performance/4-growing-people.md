| Are the opening's numbers right, and still true? | 1877: "four schools in ten" with a single teacher (8,332 of 19,345, 43%) and "nearly one in three" with a single classroom (6,170, 32%), from Inagaki (1966) as Sarkar Arani, Fukaya and Lassegard (2010) give them.
