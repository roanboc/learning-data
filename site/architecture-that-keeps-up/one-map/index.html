One map, in the repo: keep the architecture where every change is made

In spring 2027, Helena's target picture exists three times, and the council's slide is out of date. In June the map's source moves into the team's repository. Every change is reviewed like code, an agent drafts and a person decides, and the wiki and the slides are made from it.

Go further

The film names each idea once. These pages go further, and are free to read. Checked on 10 November 2026.

  • OS MasterMap Topography LayerOrdnance Survey's own page for the database: every feature with its TOID, its version and its date, and updates of only what changed.
  • ArchiMate, from The Open GroupThe standard language for enterprise architecture, with its layers and the exchange format that lets a model live as a file.
  • Mermaid, diagrams as textWrite a diagram in a few lines of text, and keep it beside the code, where a change to it can be read and reviewed.
  • draw.ioA free diagram editor whose files are text, so they can live in a repository with the rest of the work.
  • Documenting architecture decisions, by Michael NygardThe 2011 post that proposed short decision records: the context, the decision, and what follows from it.
