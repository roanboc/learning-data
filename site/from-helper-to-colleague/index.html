From helper to colleague

The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1Five stages, not a switch8½ min · Anil's workload agent is at four stages at once, one per kind of task. After one bad night, one task stepped down, then back up.
  2. 2Borrowed rights9 min · Jun's helper works on his sign-in, with all his rights. One step would have approved another agent's fix as Jun.
  3. 3A place on the map9 min · Planning's new agent will work alone. First it takes its place on the map: a role on three of seven steps, an owner, a gate.
  4. 4Rights follow the role8 min · Planning's agent gets its rights from the three steps of its role, not from Jun's 46. What it doesn't do is another role's step.
  5. 5The map, enforced8½ min · Every change said Jun. Now the planning agent works under its own name in every tool, with rights written from the map.
  6. 6Every change through review7½ min · The planning agent proposes more classes for 161 units. Jun reviews the code, and a second person approves and releases it.
  7. 7A mandate, written and enforced10 min · Anil's workload agent works alone at night in test, under one signed page on the map, and every tool holds it to that page.
