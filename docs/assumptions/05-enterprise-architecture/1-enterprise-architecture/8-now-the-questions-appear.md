# Now the questions appear: questions and assumptions

*Enterprise architecture* · *The map before the data*, film 8

[Watch it on the site](https://data.learn-all.org/enterprise-architecture/now-the-questions-appear/) · [Its new lab, Trace it down](https://data.learn-all.org/enterprise-architecture/labs/#trace)

> Kept by the thread "Name Goal, Question, Metric in film 8", 17 November 2026.

The film was made with the author, so it had no list here. On 17 November 2026 he chose Goal, Question, Metric as the practice the films follow down from a goal, asked which film should name it, and chose to make both: the line in this film, and the script's second candidate lab, with a metric tree. Both were made while he was away. Each line: the question I would have asked, what I did instead, and where it shows.

| # | Question | Assumption | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where does the film name the method, and how often? | Once, in chapter 5, right after the chain's three steps: "It's Goal, Question, Metric, a method from the 1980s, with a decision added." Its name shows on screen as the voice says it, "Goal–Question–Metric · 1980s", and leaves before the shape moves aside. | 2:41 to 2:46 |
| 2 | "1984" or "the 1980s"? | "The 1980s": the method began with Basili and Weiss's work at NASA Goddard (1984) and grew through that decade; Basili, Caldiera and Rombach set it out in 1994. Both are in the script's sources. | Chapter 5; the script's *Rigour* |
| 3 | How does the picture show what the film adds? | The rows the method names (goal, question, measure) light as the voice names them, while the decisions wait, dimmed; then the decisions light in gold, "+ a decision". | 2:41 to 2:46 |
| 4 | A sound for it? | Two soft ones, under the music's limits: a knock as the method's name comes, and a tap as the decision lights. `quiet.py` passes. | 2:41 to 2:46 |

| 6 | In the lab, what is the tree's root? | The regulator's measure, minutes without power per customer per year (SAIDI), with the planners' measure (SAIFI) as one branch. So the measure the learner traced turns out to be part of a bigger tree, with a branch nobody asked about: minutes per interruption (CAIDI). | The lab, cards 4 to 7 |
| 7 | How does the tree tell a formula from a guess? | A solid line is a formula (× or +) and always holds; a dotted line is a driver, a guess to test with data, such as trees near the lines. A legend says so. | The lab, from card 4 |
| 8 | Who gets the offered question, and how? | Grace, who owns operating the network: "Which lines stay off longest?", with the decision it would serve (where crews start). It goes as a question in a ticket, and is built only if she says yes, as the site's rule for business asks has it. | The lab, card 8 |
| 9 | Does a Pause and think question in chapter 5 open the new lab? | No new pause: the film keeps its three, and its page opens both its labs. | The film's page |
