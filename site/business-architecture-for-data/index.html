The films

  2. 2What the business needs to know7 min · Start from the industry's model, then confirm it. Each concept, defined once in its domain, in the business's own words.
  3. 3Who creates it, who owns it7 min · One capability creates each concept, and its owner owns it. Each owner names a steward to look after it, day to day.
