Kept for a reason

The third series of Data protection and privacy. Linh's tutoring contract ends, and she asks what the university holds about her. Three films show purpose, retention and people's rights over their data, with no product named; two show deleting and answering on the platform.

The films

Watch them in order: the ideas first, then the tools.

Film 1 of 5 · Purpose and consent

A reason to keep it

Every piece of personal data needs a reason you can say. Ask only for what that reason needs, and check before a new use.

One thread: a tutor's contract ends, and she asks

Linh Nguyen's tutoring contract ends on 20 November 2026. Four days later she asks the university what it holds about her, and to be taken off the tutors' mailing list. Film by film, Ingrid Holm, who owns HR's data, finds why each record is kept and until when; what must stay by law and what can go; and, with Jun, how deleting on the platform really deletes, until Linh gets her answer on time, with proof.
