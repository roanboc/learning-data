> Kept by the thread "Check that text stays inside its box", 12 November 2026.

A film of this series that has its own list keeps it beside this file. This list is for one change to three of the films. On 12 November 2026 the author saw words run past a card on a phone in another series, and asked: "Can we check this via code to fix those glitches?" The check (`site-tools/fits.py`) found six pieces of text past their boxes in three films of this series. They were fixed while he was away. Each line: the question I would have asked, what I did instead, and where it shows. Each film's own *Decided* rows have the same change.
