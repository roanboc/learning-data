The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1A case worth funding8½ min · Three options, one of them carrying on as now; costs once and every year; benefits with owners; and one clear ask, on one page.
  2. 2Risks, said out loud7 min · Helena writes each risk of the move in one sentence, with an owner and an early sign, and one sign shows in time.
  3. 3Everyone at the table9½ min · Helena asks each of fourteen stakeholders what they want from data, in their words, and tells everyone the same story, every month.
  4. 4One list, not twenty7½ min · Helena brings every request in through one door, onto one list, and orders it in the open: never by who asks, or how loudly.
