From helper to colleague

The third series on AI agents in data work. Planning's tickets pass from Jun's helper, on his own sign-in, to an agent with a place on the university's map: a role, an owner, and rights that come from its work. Four films show the ideas; three build them in Databricks, GitHub and dbt.

The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

Film 1 of 7 · Earned autonomy

Five stages, not a switch

Anil's workload agent is at four stages at once, one per kind of task. After one bad night, one task stepped down, then back up.

8½ min · Pause and think · From helper to colleague

One thread: Planning's agent, from helper to colleague

Planning's tickets, drafted all year by Jun's helper on his own sign-in, pass to the planning agent, a colleague owned by Clara. Film by film, the team sees what a helper borrows, puts the agent on the map in a role, gives it only the rights its work needs, writes them into every tool, sends every change through review, and writes down what an agent may do alone, before the next series runs a team of people and agents.
