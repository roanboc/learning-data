The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1One person, many roles6 min · One person, four systems, five records. Keep the person apart from the roles, and ask four questions to know what needs mastering.
  2. 2Who is it?5½ min · Two records, one person? A check proposes pairs, weights make a score, and a steward decides the hard ones once, with a reason.
  3. 3Which value wins6 min · Five linked records, and the values still disagree. Declare a rule for each field, not one order hidden in the code.
  4. 4Roles come and go5½ min · A status is true for a day. Give every role a start, an end and an owner, and ask any question as at a date.
  5. 5Lists everyone uses4½ min · Three names for one role, and a country counted three times. Give each list an owner, a date and one home.
  6. 6For this purpose only5 min · Data given for one role isn't free for every role. Write each role's purposes beside its data, and keep the person's wishes with her.
  7. 7One person, many systems4½ min · Four ways to give many systems one person. Search before you create, and choose the lightest way that solves the problem.
  8. 8A hub at work4½ min · Rules act, AI suggests, people decide: a week in a master data hub, and five questions to ask any hub.
  9. 9Kept, not cleaned5½ min · A clean-up merged two thousand duplicates, and a year later there were eleven hundred new ones. Check at entry, measure every day, and keep it.
