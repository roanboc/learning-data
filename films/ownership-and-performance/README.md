# Ownership and performance

*The last series of the module Leading data people.*

What it means to own an outcome, how a team should measure its work, and how people grow. From 1 July 2027 Ivy Reyes owns her first data product, *Teaching space use*, which Facilities and the faculties read every Monday. Three films: *Owning the outcome* (own what it's for, not only what you did); *Measure the work, not the people* (measure the work together, and fix the system, not the people); and *Growing people* (their own goals, weekly feedback about the work, and a next step). *Leading a data team* comes before it; the module ends with it. The plan for the module is in [`../the-new-skills/proposal.md`](../the-new-skills/proposal.md); this series' own part is in its [proposal](proposal.md).

**Status:** set up on 5 November 2026, on assumptions the author reviews later; the films are being made, each marked draft on the site, and appear in the table below as each one is out. On the site, the series sits under the topic *Leading data people*, after *Leading a data team*, at `ownership-and-performance/`, once its opening film is out.

## How the films are made

Like the site's other series, each film is generated from code, with the engine, components, fonts and voice of *The Inner Life of Data*, *A Sharper Sketch*'s scenes, *From words to data*'s tools and the agent films' kit, and checked with the governance and agent films' checks (`twice.py`, `steady.py`, `jump.py`, `edges.py`, `legible.py`, `quiet.py` with `held.py`). A film keeps its own pictures and helpers in its own source. The films are about people, so tools appear only where the story's work happens in them, at the level of what people do. Each film's page links what a newcomer can read next.
