The question behind the request: ask what it's for, and agree it in writing

Graham Ellis, the new Director of Facilities, asks for a dashboard of room use by Easter. In half an hour, mostly listening, Helena and Noor find the decision behind it, whether to ask for a new teaching building, and agree the question with him in writing, beside his words.
