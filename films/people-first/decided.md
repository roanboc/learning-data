| 12 November 2026 | Proposed: the series follows no single framework. Checked on the web on 12 November 2026; the sources are in the proposal. |
| 12 November 2026 | Proposed: personality is shown as traits by degree, never as types. The evidence, checked on 12 November 2026: people differ in lasting ways; the research describes it with a few broad traits, each a scale; a type is a scale cut in two, so people near the middle change letters on a retest; and the tests' own publishers say results are never for hiring or for limiting anyone. So the films name no test and draw no type letters; a test appears only as a conversation a team chooses, with four rules (each person's choice, their own results, degrees and not boxes, never used to hire, assign or judge); and what the team keeps is a page each person writes in their own words, *How I work*. |

| 12 November 2026 | Corrected, after the films' threads read the bridge's own sources. The study that Peru's Ministry of Culture published in 2015, *El Q'eswachaka de Canas*, changes three things the proposal had taken from the heritage pages, and "made new every June", in the row above that proposes the stories, is true of today only. The bridge hasn't been made new every year since the Incas without a break: a steel bridge was built almost a hundred metres away in 1968, the renewal lapsed for a few years, in October 1972 the bridge's keeper warned two visitors that it was dying, and the communities took it up again the next January, by their own choice. It was made on 6 January into the mid 1990s, and in June since. Nothing was published about it before 1970, so its age is the communities' own account. It doesn't carry people every day: most people cross on the steel bridge, and people still cross the rope bridge on foot. And the study of 881 samples behind the three needs isn't about work alone: its samples are from work, school, sport, parenting and health care, and on how well people work it has 22, for support for autonomy. So no film says "every year since the Incas", "every June" of the past, that the bridge has never been more than a year old, or that it carries people every day. The parts of the story are shared out again: the years they stopped and their return are *People before roles*'s, as the true answer to why they rebuild it; the wear, the missed year of 2020 and the fall of 2021 are *Wellbeing is in the work*'s; the three days, and what the story didn't achieve, stay with *How, more than who*. The stories, the frame and the four films are as the author confirmed them. |
