One measure, one definition, in an open format

In August, three tools gave the Faculty of Health three numbers for casual teaching cost, each right for its own question. Now Rohan Desai, in Finance, defines it once, with its owner, words and rules, as a metric view near the data, and an open format carries it to any tool.
