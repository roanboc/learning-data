# Decided: From words to data

The series' own decisions. With each film's rows (`<n-film>/decided.md`), they make the *Decided* table in [proposal.md](proposal.md): run `python site-tools/build_series.py` after adding a row. A decision a film's thread makes, even one for the whole series, goes in that film's file, so threads never edit the same lines.

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|

| 28 September 2026 | The series title: *From words to data*. |
| 28 September 2026 | The spine is the credential: how people prove what they know. The microcredential is its newest form, and the word's history shows how language and meaning evolve. |
| 28 September 2026 | The opening film starts before writing: how language began, how the brain links words to things, and how other animals handle concepts. |
| 16 October 2026 | *Meaning machines can read* gains a fifth chapter, *One map, many views*: each concept is written once, in Noor's architecture map (in ArchiMate, or any other architecture modelling language), with its definition, owned by the business, its relationships and its rules; the glossary, the conceptual model, the logical model (which alone adds how many, and the keys), the ontology in OWL and the tests are views drawn from it, one way, so they agree instead of competing. A first cut, *Ontology or model?*, set the ontology and the data model side by side as rivals with different jobs; it was reworked the same day to show them as one connected thing. Two labs, four scenarios and a fifth Pause and think question go with it, and *What's in a word* names its glossary and its conceptual model on screen. |
| 5 November 2026 | Words never show twice where one chapter meets the next, in this series and in every film drawn with *The Inner Life of Data*'s engine, as the author asked on 5 November 2026 after the fix of the zoom jumps in the governance and agent films ("Yes, fix that everywhere"). The engine fades each chapter into the next over 0.8 s, and where both showed the same words a little apart or at another size, those words showed twice for that time: seven joins here, in *Older than the systems*, *Many ways to read*, *Both at once* and *Keeping it true*, and 80 in 32 films in all. The new `shared/src/joins.js`, which every such film bundles after the engine, reads the film's `CHAPTER_JOINS`: the chapter carries the picture on (`glide`), lets what was there leave first, over the bare background (`leave`), or cuts (`cut`). The seven here are later moments in the same place, so what was there leaves first. `tools/check.py` now runs the governance films' `twice.py` on every chapter join, so CI stops one that shows words twice. |
| 10 November 2026 | One pack of labs and scenarios for the whole series, as the author asked ("one per series, not per video"), instead of 3 to 5 labs and 8 to 12 scenarios on every film's own pages: 7 labs and 7 situations, one lab for each film's main idea, the most hands-on, and one situation for each lab: *Count the credentials*, *Fit and gap*, *Break the update*, *Same question, four shapes*, *Apply the changes in order*, *Ground the answer*, *Approve or reject*. They're on the series' labs and scenarios pages; each film's old pages lead there, and its Pause and think keeps only the links to its lab in the pack. Each lab and situation stays in its film's words, which the pack names; the engine fetches a film's words and bundle only when one of them opens. |
