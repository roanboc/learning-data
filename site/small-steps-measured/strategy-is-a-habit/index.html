Strategy is a habit: review the plan every quarter, and move it out loud

Helena's data strategy is approved in April 2027, and she puts its reviews in the calendar. In October the university decides to offer short online courses, and the plan must move: one part moves up, one waits, and everyone it touches hears why. In January, promised sits beside delivered.
