| 10 November 2026 | *When someone falls behind* opens in Fremont, California, in the four beats: in 1982 General Motors closes its car plant, and thousands of workers lose their jobs; the plant had a name as one of the worst in the country (one worker in five absent on a normal day, complaints in the thousands, cars with parts missing or fitted wrong), many said the problem was the workers, and for the buyers it meant faults to fix, for the workers no job and a name that followed them; in 1984 General Motors and Toyota reopen it as NUMMI with more than eight in ten of the same people, groups of thirty work two weeks beside the people of a Toyota plant in Japan, and back home they work in small teams, with a team leader who steps in and a cord anyone can pull to call for help; within two years its cars have about as few faults as Toyota's in Japan, absence and complaints fall steeply, and one study found the old way would need half as many workers again. Managers and workers were both right about what they saw. It comes back in *Find the cause* (the cord brought help, not blame) and at the close (the people didn't change first; what surrounded them did). |
