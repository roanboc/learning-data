Quality as a habit

Fixing data by hand hides the cause. Count the hidden work, fix it where errors are born, and improve a little every week.

The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1The hidden factory10 min · Two people fixed every name by hand, 320 hours a year, and the same errors came back. Count the hidden work, then fix where it's born.
  2. 2Report, fix, tell7½ min · A graduate finds his name printed wrong. Answer every report, fix it where it started, and tell the person who reported it.
  3. 3Read by an agent8 min · An agent reads only what's written. Say what each column means and what empty means, and read its answer before you act.
  4. 4Small, every week9 min · 41 certificates printed again became 3: fifteen minutes every Thursday, one small change, measured, then kept with a check.
