The draft and the decision: the agent drafts; you frame, check and decide

Ivy Reyes, in her sixth week on the university's data team, asks the team's agent for a first draft of a model of room use. In a minute it's there: tidy, tested, sure, and wrong about one word. She catches it by reading what the agent assumed and checking the draft against what she knows is true.
