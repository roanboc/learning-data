## The brief

The first three series give each person skills and the team a way to work. This one asks what happens when someone answers for an outcome, and how a team knows it's getting better without turning measures against people. Ownership is answering for an outcome, not only doing a task, so *Owning the outcome* follows Ivy as the owner of her first data product: knowing who uses it and for what, noticing first, saying so first, and fixing it or raising it, in a just culture where nobody is blamed. Measures are for learning how the work flows, so *Measure the work, not the people* shows the team reading them together to find what to fix in the system, each measure paired with one that balances it, and never ranking anyone. And people grow through goals they help set, feedback about the work and a next step, so *Growing people* follows Ivy's year of one-to-ones and conversations with Helena.

The series follows Ivy from July to December 2027 (the module's shared numbers): *Teaching space use*, owned by her from Thursday 1 July, refreshed every Monday by 07:00; the sensors' change in 40 rooms on Tuesday 17 August; the measures page from July (days from ask to first use 41, 24, 14; promises met; people using products), a measure for the team, never a target for one person; her weekly one-to-ones with Helena, on Tuesdays; her mid-year conversation on Tuesday 20 July; and her end of year on Tuesday 14 December 2027.
