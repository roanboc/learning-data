## Rodrigo's decision of 12 November 2026: plan, do, study, act, across the site

After his answer on the data quality film *Small, every week* ("let's go with PDSA"), he was asked, on 12 November 2026: "PDSA across the site. Should other modules that say "plan, do, check, act" switch to "study" too, for one consistent term?" His answer:

> yes, knowing the difference was explained in the video that introduced the topic

This film never says that cycle's name. Its script's sources did, and so did the series' table of references, so only those changed (PR #NNN).

| # | Question | Assumption made | Where it shows | If it's wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do the film's own four steps change: plan, define the check, do, check? | No. They are your work cycle for an agent's task, not the name of Shewhart's and Deming's cycle: the second step defines a check before the work starts, and the fourth is a person checking the work against the plan. So the title, the chapters, the voice, the pictures, the captions and the page stay as they are. | The whole film | The fourth step becomes "study": the title, a chapter's name, lines of voice, the four steps across the top, the captions and the page change, which is a rework of the film |
