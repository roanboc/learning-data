# Rebuilding The AI platform

*The AI platform*, film 2 of 6 of *AI and continuous architecture*, is generated from code like *The map before the data*'s films: the same engine, components, voice and tools (run through [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py)), and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/aca.js`](../../shared/src/aca.js): its mark and cards (`acaMark`, `acaTitle`, `acaEnd`), Babylon's sky, palms, folk, bricks and boats (`bab_*`) and the agent (`aca_agent`). A change there is a new version of every film of the series. Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás, Nabopolassar, Euphrates, Databricks'), and the moment its poster shows (the river wall and its new embankment of baked brick, at 1:10). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md); `say` gives the voice numbers in words where the caption keeps digits (2,600 years, 310 households). |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: the embankment standing in the river at dawn, then the title; the river wall and the temple-tower in the same light, one brick in both; and the map with the platform under every agent, then the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `a2_`): Ravi Menon, copied from *Platform and solution architecture*'s kit, a person seen from the left and one picture faded as a whole (`a2_personR`, `a2_fade`, `a2_who`); Babylon from across the river, in world space wider than the frame: its light, its two walls of mud brick with their towers and what the rain does to them, the city seen through the breach, the bank, the brickfield, palms and reeds, the embankment of baked brick rising course by course, the walls in the water, the storm and the rain (`a2_light`, `a2_breach`, `a2_slump`, `a2_top`, `a2_wall`, `a2_inside`, `a2_ground`, `a2_field`, `a2_palms`, `a2_reeds`, `a2_embank`, `a2_mirror`, `a2_water`, `a2_clouds`, `a2_rain`, `a2_view`); the kiln's work on clay cards, the temple-tower in section with its skin of baked brick, and the dig (`A2_BRICKS`, `a2_brickCard`, `a2_section`, `a2_dig`); the six agents with their roles and owners, the platform's four capabilities and each agent's own stack (`A2_AG`, `A2_CAP`, `A2_OWN`, `a2_capBlock`, `a2_stack`, `a2_platform`); windows, cards, owners' ticks, a household and paper (`a2_win`, `a2_card`, `a2_owner`, `a2_house`, `a2_paper`); a model, the gateway and a call through it (`a2_model`, `a2_gate`, `a2_call`); and a service on the map (`a2_svc`). |
| `src/scenes.js` | The eleven chapters, and what they share: the six agents on one platform (`a2_six`), Ravi's note (`A2_NOTE`), the three ways the narration names (`A2_WAYS`), the map with the platform's services at its foot (`A2_SVC`, `a2_map`, `a2_mapEl`), the gateway's room, which holds three chapters (`A2_MOD`, `A2_USE`, `A2_LOG`, `A2_VOUT`, `a2_vendorAt`, `a2_room`, `a2_roomEnd6`, `a2_roomEnd7`), Databricks' drawn window and its icons (`A2_DBX`, `A2_TOOLS`, `a2_icon`), and where one chapter glides on into the next (`CHAPTER_JOINS`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line, and the words they use. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds, in its own palette: tuned flowerpots of fired clay, struck softly with felt mallets, and an alto ocarina, breathed in slurred phrases; A major, F sharp minor where things go wrong, tuned pure from A; the film's figure (three pots, then the ocarina) once in Babylon and once near the end, and the series' mark at the title and the end; water, clay, brick, fire, bitumen and the rain, then paper, keys and soft knocks. Each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`, from the same word of the narration. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs *The map before the data*'s tool on this film; `tools/quiet.py`, after `tools/audio.py`, runs the governance films' check of the sound. |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/ai-and-continuous-architecture/2-the-ai-platform/source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `python tools/build.py`, `python tools/audio.py`, `python tools/build.py` again (it bundles the soundtrack), then the checks: `python tools/check.py`, `python tools/legible.py`, `python tools/pace.py` and `python tools/quiet.py`. The video is `dist/the-ai-platform.mp4`.

## Publish

`python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/the-ai-platform/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/the-ai-platform/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `test`, `gateway` and `swap`. Progress is stored under `ld-the-ai-platform`.
