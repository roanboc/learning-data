# Decided: The new skills

The module's decisions, and this series' own. The module's other series keep their own in their folders (`../working-with-people/`, `../leading-a-data-team/`, `../ownership-and-performance/`). With each film's rows (`<n-film>/decided.md`), they make the *Decided* table in [proposal.md](proposal.md): run `python site-tools/build_series.py` after adding a row. A decision a film's thread makes, even one for the whole module, goes in that film's file, so threads never edit the same lines.

Considered and not followed as the spine: DAMA-DMBOK's roles and responsibilities, the Agile Manifesto's principles, servant leadership (Robert Greenleaf, 1970) and *Team Topologies* as a whole; each is named where a film uses its idea. |
| 5 November 2026 | *Leading a data team* teaches four ideas the author named for leading a team: minimalism, kaizen, Ubuntu, and decentralisation and emergence, an assumption for the author to review.
