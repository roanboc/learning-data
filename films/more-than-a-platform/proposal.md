## The brief

The author, on 12 November 2026: "we might need another video just explaining the different dimensions of data and how the portal presents them, like a tutorial showing all the capabilities. It could be the intro video to get serious about data and using the portal." It should be "more marketer". "The film should present the tools in the portal though, even if we don't go in detail on functionality and at least the stations. Remember that Leading people should be the last station."

What the author tells a newcomer that getting serious about data means: "Understanding the organisation, the definitions and the meaning in increasingly complex environments."

**For whom:** everyone who has just watched *The Inner Life of Data*, and anyone deciding whether the site is worth their time.

**The one decision it helps with:** what to learn next, and why the platform alone isn't enough.

**Length:** about 3 minutes 20 seconds, some 420 words at the films' pace.

## Reference and alternatives

- **Follows:** DAMA-DMBOK, second edition (revised 2024): its wheel, with data governance at the centre and ten areas around it. The film names it once, as one well-known map. The author: "state it could be any other but DAMA is well known."
- **Worth knowing, one line each:**
  - DCAM (EDM Association): the same ground as capabilities an organisation can be assessed on.
  - ISO/IEC 38505: governance of data, for boards and executives.
  - TOGAF and ArchiMate: where data sits in the whole organisation, which the wheel doesn't draw.
  - Data mesh: who owns data, by domain, and data as a product.
- **Where it adopts, extends or differs:**
  - It adopts the wheel as a named map, and the idea that governance sits in the middle.
  - It extends it with two things the wheel doesn't have as areas: AI agents in data work, and leading the people who do the work.
  - It differs in order and in shape. The film follows the site's learning route, a line with a start, a suggested path and two loops, not the wheel's circle, and it doesn't list the wheel's areas.

## The idea, and the thread

*The Inner Life of Data* ends on a decision: a new class opens, and 140 more students get a seat. It also says, in passing, what made that number true: "an enrolled student is one still enrolled on census date."

This film picks up that one word, *enrolled*, and follows it as the university gets more complex:

1. **One office.** One system, one meaning. Nobody has to ask.
2. **Four systems.** The student system, the learning platform, HR and payroll, and finance each count "enrolled" their own way. The platform moves all four, perfectly. The class reads 312% full.
3. **Many people, and agents.** More teams build on the data, and an AI agent answers questions from it. Whatever the word means now is repeated everywhere, at once.

At each step the platform is fine, and the answer depends on something else: how the organisation works, what was defined and by whom, and whether the meaning was kept. That is the author's advice, shown once.

## In practice

From the author: newcomers reach for the platform first. What they miss is the organisation, the definitions and the meaning, and they miss them more as the environment grows.

Assumptions of mine, for the author to confirm or correct:

- Two offices usually find out that their numbers differ in a meeting, not in a test.
- The definition often exists, in someone's head or in a policy, but not where the code can use it.
- With agents, a wrong definition travels further and sounds more sure of itself.

## How it is made

- **Look:** *The Inner Life of Data*'s design language and engine, so the two films read as a pair. The route is drawn as the site draws it: blue for the suggested route, amber for *Share and protect*, violet for *Agents*.
- **The portal's tools as shapes, not screens.** No picture of the interface, no counts of topics or films in the voice, and no feature's status: the film must not change when the site does.
- **Tools show, they don't lead.** Databricks and dbt appear only as the platform from *The Inner Life of Data*. No product is named in the voice.
- **Sound:** a palette of its own, with *The Inner Life of Data*'s four-note motif once, at the start, to tie them.
- **Languages:** English narration, in the voice the films have used so far; Spanish captions, page and narration (es-MX-DaliaNeural).
- **On the site:** in the topic *Data platforms*, after *The Inner Life of Data*, on a page of its own; Start here shows its poster as a card that opens it. No labs, no scenarios and no Pause and think; its "try it" is the guided tour.

## Opening story

Every film opens on a story with four beats. This one has no story from the past: its story is the ending of *The Inner Life of Data*. The four beats, in its own data story:

| Beat | In the film |
|---|---|
| Scenario | A university, the week after the tap: the platform works, and a new class has opened. |
| Problem | Two offices bring two numbers for the same class to the same meeting, and neither is wrong by its own definition. A decision about seats waits on it. |
| Solution | The offices agree what *enrolled* means, write it once, and build from it. |
| Benefits | One number, the 98% *The Inner Life of Data* shows, and a decision made that week: 140 more students get a seat. |
| Back to the story | At the end: the platform has matured, and the hard part is everything else. |

This is an exception to "start each film in the past". The author accepted it on 12 November 2026: "Fine without story, it's a follow up."
