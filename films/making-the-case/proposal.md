## The brief

*A plan in ninety days* ends with a data strategy and a roadmap the data council approves. A roadmap without money, without its risks said, without the people it touches and without one list of work stays on the wall. This series is what the author named as the standard elements of a manager's job: "presenting business cases for data initiatives, risks, benefits", and keeping stakeholders happy. *A case worth funding* asks for the money to retire the old warehouse, on one page; *Risks, said out loud* names what could go wrong, with owners and early signs; *Everyone at the table* keeps fourteen stakeholders on board with the same story every month; and *One list, not twenty* gives all the data work one door and one list, ordered in the open.

The series follows May to July 2027 (the module's shared numbers): the case on Tuesday 25 May, three options, A$380,000 once, A$270,000 a year saved from January 2028, paid back in about 17 months; eight risks, R1 to R8; fourteen stakeholders on one monthly page; and from Monday 7 June, one front door with five questions and one list: 61 requests from eleven places, 9 duplicates, 7 no longer needed, 45 put in order.
