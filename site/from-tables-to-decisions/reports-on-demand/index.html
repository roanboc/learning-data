Reports on demand, dashboards for what comes back

Rohan Desai, in Finance, needs what casual teaching has cost the Faculty of Health this semester. It used to take a report from the data team, and a week. Now he asks Genie One and gets A$1.84 million, with its query, and the questions asked every pay day get one dashboard.

The film stays at the level of architecture. Databricks' own pages show how to set each piece up, and stay current as the tools change.

  • Use Genie OneAsk questions of your data in your own words, with dashboards and apps in one place.
  • Genie AgentsAn agent for one subject: its datasets, example queries, SQL expressions and instructions in the organisation's words.
  • Curate an effective Genie AgentStart small, let a domain expert define it, and be its first user.
  • Test and monitor a Genie AgentBenchmarks: questions with their SQL answers, run after each change and rated Good or Bad.
  • DashboardsAI/BI dashboards, for the figures read at a glance.
  • Manage scheduled dashboard updates and subscriptionsRefresh a dashboard on a schedule, and send it to the people who act on it.
  • Genie Agents with dashboardsThe Ask Genie button on a published dashboard, linked to an existing Genie Agent.
  • Unity Catalog metric viewsA measure defined once, then queried from dashboards, Genie Agents and SQL, grouped by any of its fields.
