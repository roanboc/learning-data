The map before the data

A series on enterprise architecture, and why it matters for data. Tomás, a new enterprise architect at a publicly owned energy utility, learns how the place works before he asks what its data should answer: who it serves, why it must change, what it must be able to do, how value reaches people, and who does the work.

One thread: the first weeks

Day one is a badge, an org chart, a list of 140 systems and an invitation to a meeting about “the transition program”. Film by film, Tomás builds a map of the utility, from why it exists down to what runs it. Only then can he say which questions it needs to answer, where each data rule belongs, and what a change in strategy means for the data.
