Platform and solution architecture

The fourth series on enterprise architecture. Ravi Menon, who leads the utility's data platform, shows why it's a platform and not an application: what's inside it, why one is simpler than many, where it meets the applications, how security runs through it, and how a solution is designed on it.

Film 1 of 8 · Where the platform sits

An application or a platform?

An application has one purpose, its logic inside. A data platform carries every domain's logic, each rule with an owner.

8½ min · Pause and think · Platform and solution architecture

One thread: the road every domain shares

Billing, outages and metering each have their applications; the data platform is the road they share. Film by film, Ravi Menon draws its parts, keeps it to one, joins it to the integration platform, secures it end to end, designs one solution on it, makes it the road teams choose, and makes sure it can change.
