# Rebuilding When strategy moves

*When strategy moves*, film 10 of 11 of *The map before the data* (a working title), is generated from code like *Day one*: the same engine, components, voice and tools, and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/`](../../shared/src/), which this film uses as they are. Its own glyphs (a requirement for a rule, as ArchiMate 4 draws one; a data product; a measure; a decision) and everything else it needs live with its pictures, prefixed `d10_`. Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás, M-Pesa), and the moment its poster shows (an agent's counter in Kenya, cash going into mobile money). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md). |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: the man's family upcountry with the cash he sent, then the title; the light reaching the bottom of the map, every owner lit; and the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `d10_`): Kenya and its neighbours, sketched from longitude and latitude (`D10_COAST`, `D10_VIC`, `D10_RIFT`, `D10_PEAKS`), Nairobi, the towns on the road west and the road itself (`D10_NAI`, `D10_TOWNS`, `D10_ROAD`, `d10_roadAt`), with a camera over the map (`d10_camAt`, `d10_map`); a bus, an envelope and a note in no country's design (`d10_bus`, `d10_envelope`, `d10_note`); a phone of 2007 in a hand, and as an icon (`d10_phone`, `d10_phoneIcon`), a lender (`d10_lender`), and an agent's counter, close up, with the agent's and the customer's hands (`d10_counter`, `d10_hand`); a chip on a layer (`d10_chip`); a home (`d10_house`); the film's glyphs and elements, an owner's tag and a "new" badge (`d10_glyph`, `d10_el`, `d10_owner`, `d10_new`); a principle, the three principles together, and the form a household would have filled in (`d10_principle`, `d10_principles`, `d10_form`); a timeline of months (`d10_gantt`); the two sides and the wall, as in *Who does it* (`d10_sides`, `d10_wall`); a decision record (`d10_adr`); a data rule in the form *Rules with a home* sets, small on the map, and the line that realises it (`d10_rule`, `d10_ruleMini`, `d10_realise`); a contract on a short card (`d10_conMini`); the catalogue's page for eligibility, with its column mask (`d10_catalogue`); a household's bill, the letter and the call centre's answer, and the application for tenants (`d10_bill`, `d10_letter`, `d10_call`, `d10_apply`); the data product, what amount due means, a version of its contract, and the contract in dbt (`d10_product`, `d10_meaning`, `d10_ver`, `d10_yml`); and the catalogue's lineage (`D10_LIN`, `d10_lineage`). Someone at the right of the frame is drawn facing the other way, so they point into it (`d10_personR`). |
| `src/scenes.js` | The eleven chapters, and what they share: what the idea took on each layer (`D10_KL`); the direction's lines (`D10_DIR`); the elements the change touches, with their owners, and the rest of the map (`D10_WALK`, `D10_REST`); who must agree (`D10_AGREE`); the decision record (`D10_ADR`); the three rules (`D10_RULES`); the contract in dbt (`D10_YML`); the plateaus and what changes between them (`D10_PLAT`, `D10_DIFF`); the page for the minister's office and what production finds instead (`D10_PAGE`, `D10_PROD`); the street on the first of July (`D10_ST`); and the answer's list, lifted off the map (`D10_OWN`, `D10_RMIN`, `D10_CON`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds: a felt piano over pizzicato strings and soft pads in F-sharp minor for Kenya in 2007; then the series' palette (nylon plucks, a soft flute, warm pads), here in A major, with the series' mark at the title and the end; the ripple, soft felt-piano notes falling one for each layer as the change reaches it; the felt piano again wherever the change reaches a household; and the film's one rhythm, a quick, low pulse under "by tomorrow". Each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs the series' tool (`../../shared/tools/run.py`) on this film. |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/enterprise-architecture/10-when-strategy-moves/source/`; the video is `dist/when-strategy-moves.mp4`.

## Publish

As for [*Day one*](../../1-day-one/source/README.md#publish): `python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/when-strategy-moves/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/when-strategy-moves/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `walk`, `counter` and `lands`. Progress is stored under `ld-when-strategy-moves`.
