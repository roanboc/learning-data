Governance you can use

The third series on data governance. What the university decided, and what Noor's map drew, is built where the work happens, in dbt and on Databricks: owners and checks in the project, access on the platform, one meaning, quality you can see, products with contracts, and a place to find them.

One thread: from the map to the tools

Each film builds one of the flows on Noor's map, in the example dbt project and on Databricks, and names the films of the first two series it expands. Every file and screen is a real one: what a practitioner opens to make governance run.

Who's at the table

Cyan outlines are technical; gold outlines are business.

Jun, who puts owners, terms and classes in the dbt project, and a check that won't let a model in without them
