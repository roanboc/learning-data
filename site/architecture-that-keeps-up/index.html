One thread: from the team's services to a path for every provider

After the data strategy is approved, Helena Novak writes the team's four services and who each is for, and walks one down every layer. In June the architecture's map moves into the team's repository, where every change is reviewed and everything else is made from it. Then the team gives every data provider a path: the rooms' sensors send events, while the timetable, bought as a service, never says what was deleted, so the team finds out every night.
