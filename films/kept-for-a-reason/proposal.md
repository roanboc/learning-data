## The brief

On 28 October 2026, the plan named privacy and the data lifecycle as missing before the last module (retention, deletion and the right to be forgotten, archiving). On 5 November 2026 the author put them in this module, renamed *Data protection and privacy*, as a series of their own, with consent, retention, deletion and archiving. The series teaches the principles most privacy laws share, never one country's law, and shows them on the platform.

## The films

| # | Folder | Film (working title) | What it shows | The idea to take away |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1-a-reason-to-keep-it` | A reason to keep it | Every piece of personal data has a purpose; collect only what it needs; consent is one reason among several (a contract, a law, a public task); a privacy check before a new use | Keep data for a reason you can say. |
| 2 | `2-how-long-and-then-what` | How long, and then what | The life of data: in use, archived, deleted; a retention schedule by kind of record, owned by the record's owner; holds that pause deletion; what the law says must stay, and what mustn't | Every record has an end date, decided before it's needed. |
| 3 | `3-forgetting-a-person` | Forgetting a person | People's rights over their data: to see it, correct it, have it erased, and object; finding every place a person's data lives (the keys of *The same one, everywhere* and lineage); what can't be erased, and saying why | A person can ask; you must be able to answer. |
| 4 | `4-deleted-means-deleted` | Deleted means deleted | On the platform: a delete leaves old files until they're cleaned up (`VACUUM`, the time-travel window); retention set on each table; archives kept cold; copies found through lineage; personal data in logs, traces and prompts, kept out or kept short | Deleting is done when the last copy is gone. |
| 5 | `5-a-person-asks` | A person asks | Linh's request end to end: a ticket; her records found through her keys and lineage; a copy of what's held, sent; the mailing list cleared; pay records kept for their legal time, with the reason given; proof of each step in the repository and the audit log, within the university's 30 days | A request answered on time, with proof. |
