The films

Watch them in order: each film builds on the one before.

  1. 1People before roles7½ min · A chart of seven boxes and 46 requests on her first day. Before she plans anyone's work, Helena sits down with each person.
  2. 2Wellbeing is in the work9½ min · Fourteen urgent requests wait on Marta, in no order. Helena doesn't ask her to cope: she changes the work, then asks the whole team.
  3. 3No two alike9 min · Erin says nothing in a meeting, and Helena guesses wrong. So she asks each person how they work, and changes the meeting.
  4. 4How, more than who10 min · Two numbers, and an argument that turns to the person. Helena gives everyone a turn, and the team writes one page on how it works.
