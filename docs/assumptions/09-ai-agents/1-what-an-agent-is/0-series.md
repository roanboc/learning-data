# What an agent is: the series' own questions and assumptions

*AI agents in data work* · *What an agent is*, films 1, 4, 5 and 7

[Watch the series on the site](https://data.learn-all.org/what-an-agent-is/)

> Kept by the thread "Check that text stays inside its box", 12 November 2026.

A film of this series that has its own list keeps it beside this file. This list is for one change to four of the films. On 12 November 2026 the author saw words run past a card on a phone in another series, and asked: "Can we check this via code to fix those glitches?" The check (`site-tools/fits.py`) found six pieces of text past their boxes in four films of this series. They were fixed while he was away. Each line: the question I would have asked, what I did instead, and where it shows. Each film's own *Decided* rows have the same change.

| # | Film | Question | Assumption | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | All four | Where a box is too small for its words: shorten the words, or change the box? | Keep every word. A box is made from its words at the size they are drawn, and where two words on one line were wider than their card, they take two lines. These films already draw every word at 28 px or more, so nothing here was raised for a phone: each box had a width of its own, set by hand. | The six places below; the series' other six films had none |
