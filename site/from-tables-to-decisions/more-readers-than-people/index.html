More readers than people, each reading as itself

On pay day, Genie One tells Rohan Desai that casual teaching has cost the Faculty of Health A$1.84 million so far, but Finance's budget pack in Power BI says A$1.56 million under the same name. So every reader, people, reports, an app, an agent and a partner, reads one definition, each as itself.
