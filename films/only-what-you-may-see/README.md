# Only what you may see

*The second series of the module Data protection and privacy.*

Protecting data by what it is. The Faculty of Health asks for learners' records for a study. Eight films: three on the ideas, with no product named (an agreement first; know what you hold; show the least, the ways to hide a value), then five in Databricks and dbt (grants by design, tags that travel downstream, one policy for many tables, copies that keep the rules, and proof it held). *Locks before the data* comes before it; *Kept for a reason* follows. The plan for the module is in [`../locks-before-the-data/proposal.md`](../locks-before-the-data/proposal.md); this series' own part is in its [proposal](proposal.md).

**Status:** set up on 5 November 2026, on assumptions the author will review; the films are being made, one at a time, in order, and appear in the table below as each one is out. On the site, the series will sit under the topic *Data protection and privacy*, between *Data governance* and *AI agents in data work*, at `only-what-you-may-see/`, once its opening film is out.

## The look and the sound

- **Night glass, frosted glass with solid colour**, the module's own digital security look, decided by the author on 5 November 2026: a dark hall of frosted panes; cards, chips, bands, pills and ticks in full, clear colour; every word at least 4.5 to 1 against what is behind it and at least 28 px in the 1920-pixel frame, none under the captions. Cyan is an identity, green allowed, coral denied or closed, amber power switched on, violet hidden personal data, in every film. The module's kit, in `../locks-before-the-data/shared/`, used as it is.
- **Its own sound in each film.** A lead instrument the site doesn't have, reserved for each film. Music under the opening story, then only now and then, at a chapter's turn or under a moment on screen; nothing loops, trembles or pulses, except an urgent pulse where a story turns to a breach, a leak or a rush.

## How the films are made

Like the site's other series, each film is generated from code, with the engine, components, fonts and voice of *The Inner Life of Data*, *A Sharper Sketch*'s scenes and *From words to data*'s tools, and checked with the governance and agent films' checks (`twice.py`, `steady.py`, `jump.py`, `edges.py`, `legible.py`, `quiet.py` with `held.py`). The ideas films name no product; the tool films show Databricks and dbt, named as their docs name them on the day, and never say a feature's release status. A film keeps its own pictures and helpers in its own source. The example project, [`../analytics-engineering/project/`](../analytics-engineering/project/), is where the data story happens. Each film's page links the official docs it points to.
