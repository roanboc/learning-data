# The Inner Life of Data, frozen for the Making of films

*Data for Films* and *That's not quite right* draw *The Inner Life of Data* as it was when they were made: they stop it at
2:31, quote its length and its file sizes, and show small copies of its frames. These are that film's own files as they were
then (its narration, timings, pauses and the four scene files), so reworking *The Inner Life of Data* doesn't change these two films.
The shared components and the engine (`core.js`, `logos.js`, `style2.js`, `ui3.js`, `engine3.js`) still come from
`films/inner-life-of-data/source/src/`, where they stay unchanged because every film draws with them.

Don't edit these copies. To show a newer version of *The Inner Life of Data*, copy its files here again, and check the frame at 2:31
(`T231` and `FOCUS` in `../frames.js`), the figures the narration quotes, and both films' stills.
