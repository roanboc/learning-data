From tables to decisions

The third series of Modern data basics. Finance asks what casual teaching costs, and the answer used to wait for a report. Three films: answers on demand, with dashboards only for the questions asked again and again; each measure defined once; and the same definitions for people, agents and systems.

One thread: what casual teaching costs

Rohan Desai, in Finance, asks what casual teaching cost the Faculty of Health this semester. It used to take a report and a week. Film by film, the team serves the answer on demand, keeps a dashboard only for the figures asked every fortnight, defines casual teaching cost once, and serves that one definition to people in Finance's own tools, to agents and to the systems that need it.
