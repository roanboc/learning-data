## The brief

The first three series give each person skills and the team a way to work. This one asks what happens when someone answers for an outcome, and how a team knows it's getting better without turning measures against people. Ownership is answering for an outcome, not only doing a task, so *Owning the outcome* follows Ivy as the owner of her first data product: knowing who uses it and for what, noticing first, saying so first, and fixing it or raising it, in a just culture where nobody is blamed. Measures are for learning how the work flows, so *Measure the work, not the people* shows the team reading them together to find what to fix in the system, each measure paired with one that balances it, and never ranking anyone. When someone's work falls behind the team's, there is often a good reason, so *When someone falls behind* follows Helena looking for the cause with Tomás, the team's report developer, before judging: training, fit for the role or a hard time; removing what's in the way; and measuring his gap against the team's own gain, over a year, as the author advised on 10 November 2026: "identifying the root cause (training, fit for role, personal traits) and removing all blockers you can, sometimes you just need a bit of patience". And people grow through goals they help set, feedback about the work and a next step, so *Growing people* follows Ivy's year of one-to-ones and conversations with Helena.

The series follows Ivy from July to December 2027 (the module's shared numbers): *Teaching space use*, owned by her from Thursday 1 July, refreshed every Monday by 07:00; the sensors' change in 40 rooms on Tuesday 17 August; the measures page from July (days from ask to first use 41, 24, 14; promises met; people using products), a measure for the team, never a target for one person; Tomás's year, after the reports move onto the platform: the team's days from ask to first use fall from 41 to 14, his own fall less and unevenly, and by December his gap has narrowed by about half; her weekly one-to-ones with Helena, on Tuesdays; her mid-year conversation on Tuesday 20 July; and her end of year on Tuesday 14 December 2027.
