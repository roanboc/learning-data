Learning while doing: learn on the real work, beside someone, then teach it

Ivy Reyes joins the university's data team in March 2027. For six weeks she pairs with Jun an hour a day on the team's real models, one small change at a time. From April the team keeps two hours every Thursday for learning. In May she teaches what she learned, and by June every job has two names.

Go further

The film names each idea once. These pages go further, and are free to read. Checked on 10 November 2026.

  • Communities of practiceJean Lave and Etienne Wenger's idea, from their book Situated Learning: apprentices, from tailors in Liberia to midwives in Mexico, learn by taking part in real work, first at its edge.
  • Deliberate practiceAnders Ericsson's idea: skill grows from practice aimed at what you can't do yet, with quick feedback from someone who can.
  • Pair programmingTwo people at one keyboard, one typing, one reviewing and thinking ahead, swapping often: the practice extreme programming made popular.
  • The 70-20-10 rule, from the Center for Creative LeadershipWhere the rule of thumb comes from: research with managers on where learning at work happens, from hard tasks, other people and courses.
  • Paulo FreireThe Brazilian educator: his literacy work with adults, the coup of 1964, his exile, and Pedagogy of the Oppressed.
