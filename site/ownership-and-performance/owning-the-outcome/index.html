Owning the outcome: own what it's for, not only what you did

From July 2027 Ivy Reyes owns the university's room data. One Monday the refresh runs on time and every check passes, but forty rooms' counts have dropped. She says so first, fixes what's hers, raises what isn't, and nobody is blamed.
