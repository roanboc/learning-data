Data that moves

The first series of Modern data basics. Linh Nguyen's tutoring contract starts in the HR system; payroll pays her, and Finance asks what teaching costs. Moving that data once took a team. Now a connector, a query in place or a mirror does it, and the work left is agreeing what it means.

Moving is easy, agreeing is the work

HR's contracts once took Finance two days to read. Tools move them now; the work left is agreeing what each field means.

7 min · Pause and think · Data that moves

One thread: a tutor's contract, from HR to Finance

Linh Nguyen tutors HI101 this semester, on a contract born in the HR system, which sends each change as it's made and its whole file again each teaching period. Payroll pays her every second Thursday, and Finance asks what casual teaching costs. Film by film, the team moves that data the simplest way the tools offer, agrees the contract at each interface, sets how fresh each part must be, and keeps one copy on one platform, governed once.
