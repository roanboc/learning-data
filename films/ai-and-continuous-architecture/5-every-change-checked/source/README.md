# Rebuilding Every change, checked

*Every change, checked*, film 5 of 6 of *AI and continuous architecture*, is generated from code like *The map before the data*'s films: the same engine, components, voice and tools (run through [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py)), and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/aca.js`](../../shared/src/aca.js): its mark and cards (`acaMark`, `acaTitle`, `acaEnd`), Babylon's sky, city, ground, folk, bricks, tags and quotes (`bab_*`), and the agent and its step on a task (`aca_agent`, `aca_stepPill`). A change there is a new version of every film of the series. Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás, Inés, Nabopolassar, Marduk, Arahtu, ID), and the moment its poster shows (a baked brick drawn out of the dark wall, its edge stamped, with the inscription's words on clay above it, at 0:51). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md); `say` gives the voice numbers in words where the caption keeps digits (2,600 years, 140 changes, 1,900 and 3,400 households), and the brick's words without their quotation marks and ellipsis. |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: the utility's map, every element stamped, a change checked and walked over it, then the title; the river wall and the map side by side, each half with its words; and Tomás and Grace with the answer, then the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `a5_`): the sides' and layers' colours (`A5_NET`, `A5_MOT` and the rest); a face that changes over a moment, a person seen from the left, one picture faded as a whole, Babylon's people and the utility's, and a name (`a5_expr`, `a5_personR`, `a5_fade`, `a5_folk`, `a5_who`, `a5_name`); Babylon's river front, its city wall, bank, palms, reeds, the embankment of baked brick and the river (`A5_HZ`, `a5_top`, `a5_wall`, `a5_ground`, `a5_palms`, `a5_reeds`, `a5_embank`, `a5_mirror`, `a5_water`, `a5_view`); the wall's face close up, one brick lit, a brick drawn out with its stamped edge, the dig and the archaeologists' plan with the sixteen bricks (`A5_FB`, `a5_face`, `A5_ONE`, `a5_brick3`, `a5_dig`, `A5_FIND`, `a5_plan`); a clip, the series' glyphs, an element of the map, its stamp of owner and source, and the map with a change over it (`a5_clip`, `a5_glyph`, `a5_el`, `a5_stamp`, `A5_TM`, `A5_TEX`, `a5_texMap`); the checks, a check's history of runs, the wall seen side on, the case that must fail, and the gate a failing change waits at (`a5_checkRow`, `a5_checks`, `a5_runs`, `a5_wallV`, `a5_case`, `a5_gate`); a clock and a loop (`a5_clock`, `a5_loop`); a street of homes, a crew, a letter, a calendar day and a phone's text (`a5_home`, `a5_env`, `a5_crew`, `A5_SX`, `a5_street`, `a5_letter`, `a5_day`, `a5_phone`); tables and jobs, with a reader nobody had drawn, a line of light, cards, a ticket, an owner, the agent's badge, a mask over what's personal, a decision, a bubble, blocks of time and a ringing handset (`a5_table`, `a5_job`, `a5_flow`, `a5_card`, `a5_ticket`, `a5_owner`, `a5_badge`, `a5_mask`, `a5_dec`, `a5_bubble`, `a5_blocks`, `a5_handset`). |
| `src/scenes.js` | The eight chapters, and what they share: a label that springs in and leaves (`a5_say`); the stretches later kings laid again (`A5_KINGS`); the present at the opening's end (`a5_now`); the day's changes on the map (`a5_changes`); the six checks and the words that light each (`A5_CHK`); the social tariff's walk, its fourteen elements and five owners (`A5_TL`, `A5_T14`, `A5_TOWN`, `a5_tmap`, `a5_towners`); the new change's board, its rows, elements and who must agree, and its ticket (`A5_WR`, `A5_WE`, `A5_WOWN`, `a5_tix`); and the running estate, its tables and readers, and its live trace (`A5_ET`, `a5_live`). In each room, the words of one moment leave before the next moment's come in. |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line, and the words they use. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds, in its own palette: a xylophone struck softly with yarn mallets, for what's laid and walked (a brick, a layer, each row as the agent walks it, and a low F left unanswered where a check stops something), and a glass harmonica, which holds the opening story's chords and after that plays only where a person decides; E flat major, C minor where things go wrong, tuned pure from E flat (the score stops if two notes that aren't pure together would sound); the film's figure (the xylophone stepping down, answered by the glass harmonica's chord) once in Babylon and once near the end, and the series' mark at the title and the end; the river lapping, baskets, a stamp in clay, a stylus, a trowel, then paper, a pencil, ticks, knocks, a lock and a buzz. Each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`, from the same word of the narration. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs *The map before the data*'s tool on this film; `tools/quiet.py`, after `tools/audio.py`, runs the governance films' check of the sound. |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/ai-and-continuous-architecture/5-every-change-checked/source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `python tools/build.py`, `python tools/audio.py`, `python tools/build.py` again (it bundles the soundtrack), then the checks: `python tools/check.py`, `python tools/legible.py`, `python ../../../../site-tools/fits.py`, `python tools/pace.py` and `python tools/quiet.py`. The video is `dist/every-change-checked.mp4`.

## Publish

`python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/every-change-checked/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/every-change-checked/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `checks`, `test` and `estate`. Progress is stored under `ld-every-change-checked`.
