Documents as data

The last series of Modern data basics, in one film. Finance receives thousands of supplier invoices a month as PDF files, and HR keeps every signed contract. Documents are data to extract and context for agents. The film shows the platform's functions that read them, and where the docs go further.

7½ min · Pause and think · Documents as data

One thread: an invoice and a contract

Finance receives about 3,860 supplier invoices a month, most of them as PDF files, and HR keeps every contract its staff sign, Linh Nguyen's among them. The film reads them as data, with the platform's functions, into tables Finance can check, and keeps each file and its metadata as context an agent may cite, while who may read a document stays the work of governance and data protection.
