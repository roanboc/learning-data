# Decided: People first

The series' own decisions. The module's are in [`../the-new-skills/decided.md`](../the-new-skills/decided.md), with its plan in [`../the-new-skills/proposal.md`](../the-new-skills/proposal.md). With each film's rows (`<n-film>/decided.md`), they make the *Decided* table in [proposal.md](proposal.md): run `python site-tools/build_series.py` after adding a row. A decision a film's thread makes goes in that film's file, so threads never edit the same lines.

Every row below but the first is a proposal for the author to confirm; a row with the author's answer follows them when it comes.

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 12 November 2026 | Asked for by the author on 12 November 2026, at 01:37 UTC, after reviewing the module: "we are not really starting with people. As mentioned, people are the most important asset in all, so the series needs to start with just people, collaboration. Get inspiration from the Ubuntu and other people-first frameworks before we start with the hats, skills and so on. People before roles is important, their wellbeing and their personal traits. We have few no nothing about it and needs a much better framework." And on personality, having looked at a sixteen-type test's page for teams: "we are aware that there are personalities and they also influence the way we collaborate. This is the psychological aspect that I think is very important." So the module is to open with people, before the hats and the skills. |
| 12 November 2026 | Proposed: a new series, *People first* (*Las personas primero*), in `films/people-first/`, opens the module, before *The new skills*. Four films: *People before roles*, *Wellbeing is in the work*, *No two alike* and *How, more than who*. They are set in February 2027, Helena Novak's first month with the team, before Ivy Reyes joins on Monday 1 March, so the module's year starts a month earlier and *Many hats, one picture* follows on. The module becomes five series and eighteen films. |
| 12 November 2026 | Proposed: the series follows no single framework. Its frame is four questions a leader asks before the work: who is here, what do they need, how do they differ, how do they work together. The backbone is ISO 27500, the standard on the human-centred organisation; the stance is Ubuntu, which *The team comes first* goes on teaching in practice, with the Andean *minka* and *ayni*; and each film rests on the best-tested research for its question: the three needs of self-determination theory, job demands and resources with the World Health Organization's definition of burn-out and Safe Work Australia's psychosocial hazards, the five broad traits of personality, and the studies of how teams work together. Checked on the web on 12 November 2026; the sources are in the proposal. |
| 12 November 2026 | Proposed: personality is shown as traits by degree, never as types. The evidence, checked on 12 November 2026: people differ in lasting ways; the research describes it with a few broad traits, each a scale; a type is a scale cut in two, so people near the middle change letters on a retest; and the tests' own publishers say results are never for hiring or for limiting anyone. So the films name no test and draw no type letters; a test appears only as a conversation a team chooses, with four rules (each person's choice, their own results, degrees and not boxes, never used to hire, assign or judge); and what the team keeps is a page each person writes in their own words, *How I work*. |
| 12 November 2026 | Proposed: the series follows one true story from outside the West, as the author preferred for new series on 11 November 2026: the rope bridge of Q'eswachaka (Quehue, Cusco, Peru), made new every June by four Quechua-speaking communities, checked on 12 November 2026 against UNESCO, Peru's Ministry of Culture and the bridge builders' own words. *People before roles*, *Wellbeing is in the work* and *How, more than who* each open on their part of it, and the last says what the story didn't achieve. *No two alike* steps aside on purpose: a bridge of grass has no honest part for how people differ, since one blade is like another and people aren't. It opens in China about 2,500 years ago, on an exchange the Analects keeps: two students ask Confucius the same question and get opposite answers, because he answered the person. Checked on 12 November 2026 in the Chinese text, James Legge's translation, Sima Qian and Zhu Xi. The other way for that film, the oral teachings of the carpenters of Hōryū-ji (Nara, Japan), is in the proposal; Japan already opens five films on the site, and those teachings are checked only from secondary sources. The people are never the grass: they are the families and the builders, and the bridge is what they make and renew together. |
| 12 November 2026 | Proposed: none of the fourteen films is voiced or drawn again. *Many hats, one picture*, which the author validated with *The new skills* on 11 November 2026, stays as it is. When the series is registered on the site, *People first* becomes the series before *The new skills* in the chain; the page texts that call *The new skills* the module's first series, and the two after it the second and third, are reworded without a number; the module's line names people first; and the four series' folders of assumptions are numbered again. |
| 12 November 2026 | Proposed: the look is the module's sunlit plaster, with the kit bundled as it is; the series draws the whole team of seven for the first time, so Marta Silva, the analyst, gets a face, an assumption for the author. Each film is made in its own pull request, and its page carries the module's Draft label until the author has watched it. |
