One list, not twenty: one door for every request, ordered in the open

Requests reach Helena's team in eleven places, and whoever reaches it first is answered first. From 7 June 2027, one front door and one list: 61 requests become 45, ordered in the open by value and effort, six at most in progress, and every not now with a reason and a date.
