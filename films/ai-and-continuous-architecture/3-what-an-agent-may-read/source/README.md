# Rebuilding What an agent may read

*What an agent may read*, film 3 of 6 of *AI and continuous architecture*, is generated from code like *The map before the data*'s films: the same engine, components, voice and tools (run through [`../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py`](../../../enterprise-architecture/shared/tools/run.py)), and the series' own components in [`../../shared/src/aca.js`](../../shared/src/aca.js): its mark and cards (`acaMark`, `acaTitle`, `acaEnd`), Babylon's sky, clay, folk and tags (`bab_*`) and the agent (`aca_agent`). A change there is a new version of every film of the series. Only what is new lives here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `film.json` | The film's key, title, subtitle, how to read its pictures, its own source files, the words the voice respells (Tomás, Inés, Etemenanki, Uruk, Borsippa, Databricks'), and the moment its poster shows (the original and the checked copy side by side, the words at its end on clay, at 1:04). |
| `src/narration.js` | The narration, one line per id, following the chapters of [the script](../script.md); `say` gives the voice numbers in words where the caption keeps digits (229 BCE, 180 cubits, 91.5 metres, 41,000 questions). |
| `src/vodur.js` | The voiced length of each line, written by `tools/tts.py`. |
| `src/breath.js` | The few longer pauses, and three wordless moments: the chat among the households it answers, then the title; the scribe's checked copy beside the chat's place, the original read in both; and the map with the chat's place on it, then the end card. |
| `src/plan.js` | This film's pictures (prefixed `a3_`): the layers' colours by name and the four levels' (`A3_LV`); Inés Duarte, copied from *Data architecture*'s kit into this film's own cast (`PEOPLE.ines`), a face that changes over a moment, a person seen from the left and one picture faded as a whole (`a3_expr`, `a3_personR`, `a3_fade`, `a3_who`); the room in Uruk at night, its lamp, the scribe and the original on its stand (`a3_room`, `a3_scribe`, `a3_lying`); tablets, the lines at a copy's end and the quote on clay (`a3_tablet`, `A3_FOOT`, `a3_foot`, `a3_quote`); the tower's stages, the copies in a row with their slip, Babylonia on clay and the tower's base seen from above (`a3_stages`, `a3_copy`, `a3_babMap`, `a3_base`); households, speech bubbles, exports and the contact centre's notes (`a3_house`, `a3_bubble`, `a3_file`, `a3_notes`); a level, a data product and its contract (`a3_level`, `a3_prodGlyph`, `a3_product`, `a3_contract`); the chat's place, its identity, a mask, a household's key, the small tool and a phone (`a3_place`, `a3_badge`, `a3_mask`, `a3_key`, `a3_tool`, `a3_phone`); and windows, cards, owners' ticks, a ticket and icons (`a3_win`, `a3_card`, `a3_owner`, `a3_ticket`, `a3_icon`). |
| `src/scenes.js` | The nine chapters, and what they share: the chat among the households (`A3_HX`, `a3_hh`); whatever it could reach (`A3_SRC`); the map opened as a view of three layers, with the products, their levels, the chat's place and its identity, which holds two chapters (`A3_SL`, `A3_PROD`, `a3_geo`, `a3_view`); the street under planned work (`A3_STX`); Databricks' drawn window and Unity Catalog's tree (`A3_DWX`, `A3_TREE`); and where one chapter glides on into the next (`CHAPTER_JOINS`). |
| `src/i18n/es/captions.js` | The Spanish captions, one per English line, and the words they use. Spanish is the captions and the page; the pictures stay in English. |
| `tools/score.py` | The film's music and sounds, in its own palette: a viola d'amore, whose strings beneath ring in sympathy only with the notes they're tuned to, and tuning forks on their boxes, struck with felt and damped after two or three seconds; D major, B minor where things go wrong, tuned pure from D; the film's figure (a fork's note, the viola d'amore playing it, the strings beneath answering) once in Uruk and once near the end, and the series' mark at the title and the end; a lamp's flame, clay, a reed on clay, then paper, keys, knocks and taps. Each effect fires with the thing it belongs to in `src/scenes.js`, from the same word of the narration. |
| `tools/` | Each tool runs *The map before the data*'s tool on this film; `tools/quiet.py`, after `tools/audio.py`, runs the governance films' check of the sound. |

## Rebuild

As for [*Day one*](../../../enterprise-architecture/1-day-one/source/README.md), from this folder, `films/ai-and-continuous-architecture/3-what-an-agent-may-read/source/`: `python tools/tts.py`, `python tools/build.py`, `python tools/audio.py`, `python tools/build.py` again (it bundles the soundtrack), then the checks: `python tools/check.py`, `python tools/legible.py`, `python tools/pace.py` and `python tools/quiet.py`. The video is `dist/what-an-agent-may-read.mp4`.

## Publish

`python tools/publish.py` copies the player, the soundtrack and the Spanish captions to `site/assets/what-an-agent-may-read/` and draws the poster, then `python site-tools/build_series.py` makes the pages. "Pause and think" is `site/assets/what-an-agent-may-read/think.en.js` and `think.es.js`: it stops after `level`, `tool` and `who`. Progress is stored under `ld-what-an-agent-may-read`.
