The Inner Life of Data

How to read it. Light is data, and each source system has its own colour. The sketch (the conceptual model) gives every piece its place. dbt holds the recipes and tests; Databricks runs them. Gold paintings are data products, and their labels are dbt exposures. Projectors share the original without making copies. The narration is a synthetic voice.
