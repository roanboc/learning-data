Grants by design: once, by area and role

The registrar's tables got their readers one grant at a time: 163 grants made by hand on 58 tables, a new table waiting six days for its first reader, and nine tables owned by the person who made them. Now they move into a catalog of their own, and Mei Tanaka asks for a plan before a single grant.
