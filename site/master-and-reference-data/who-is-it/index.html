Who is it? Master or reference data, the golden record, and a match decided once

In 1556, a village took the wrong man for Martin Guerre, because so much matched. At the university, a duplicate check proposes three pairs, and Mei's office decides each one: Linh's two student numbers, linked with a reason; HR's record of her, scored weight by weight, its birth date one digit apart; and two students called Aisha who share a number but are two people. A golden record keeps every source as it arrived, so any link can be undone.
